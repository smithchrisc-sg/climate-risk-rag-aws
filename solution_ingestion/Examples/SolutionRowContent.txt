Description: "The Disaster Recovery Funding Arrangements (DRFA) is Australia's primary national funding mechanism for disaster relief and recovery, jointly financed by the Commonwealth and state/territory governments. It operates as a framework under which state and territory governments activate and administer measures tailored to their jurisdictions, often delivered in partnership with local governments and sectoral agencies., 
The Tasmanian Relief and Recovery Arrangements (TRRA) is Tasmania’s principal state-level framework for funding relief and recovery for communities and local governments during or after emergencies. It functions alongside the DRFA, allowing the state to combine its own policies with national support for comprehensive recovery packages."
Key Highlights: "DRFA is a national, co-funded, multi-stream model with flexible state-based administration and local government delivery. It includes measures like the Restoration of Essential Public Assets (REPA) for public infrastructure and sector-specific support such as primary producer assistance., 
TRRA is state-designed and tailored to Tasmania, covering individuals, councils, small businesses, primary producers, and non-profit organizations. It integrates seamlessly with DRFA for joint assistance."
Results: "Cairns Regional Council implemented a A$24.417 million REPA remediation program after Tropical Cyclone Jasper, including permanent bridge replacement and landslip and drainage repairs., 
In 2024, DRFA-backed Recovery and Restoration Grants in Tasmania provided up to A$11,450 for essential household replacement and repair/restoration of principal residences, available for three months post-impact."
