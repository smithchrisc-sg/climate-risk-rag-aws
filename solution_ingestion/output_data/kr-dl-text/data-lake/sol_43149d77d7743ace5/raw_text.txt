Introduction

This solution, CAREC Disaster Risk Transfer Facility (CAREC DRTF) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Pakistan between Government of Pakistan, Asian Development Bank (ADB), CAREC Secretariat, and WTW (Willis Towers Watson). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

The CAREC Disaster Risk Transfer Facility (CAREC DRTF) is a regional initiative under the CAREC Program, supported by the Asian Development Bank (ADB), aimed at providing a comprehensive disaster risk financing solution for member countries, including Pakistan. It focuses on pooling and coordinating sovereign disaster risk financing needs, utilizing common risk analytics through the Disaster Risk Modelling Interface (DRMI), and structuring risk transfer solutions such as reinsurance and capital-market instruments like the ADB-sponsored Disaster Relief Bond (DRB). The facility also offers ancillary services such as capacity building, data governance, and advisory support.

Key Highlights

The CAREC DRTF features regional pooling across CAREC members, integration of the DRMI analytics platform, and the proposed ADB-sponsored Disaster Relief Bond. It aligns with CAREC’s climate agenda and regional public goods approach, offering a structured roadmap informed by member feedback, including Pakistan. The facility aims to move member countries from ad hoc, post-disaster budget reallocations to ex ante, risk-informed, and pooled disaster risk financing.

Results

Delivered regional technical assistance outputs including a consolidated risk modelling interface (DRMI), a proposed roadmap for the facility, and a concept for an ADB-sponsored Disaster Relief Bond. Convened two in-person engagement events, including one in Islamabad in 2023, to build consensus and capacity. Quantitative payout results are not yet applicable pending full operationalization.
