Introduction

This solution, Second Cambodia Growth and Resilience Development Policy Financing addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Cambodia between Royal Government of Cambodia and World Bank Group (International Development Association—IDA). The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Second Cambodia Growth and Resilience Development Policy Financing (DPF) is a $275 million policy-based credit initiative by the World Bank, aimed at supporting Cambodia's private sector competitiveness, fiscal resilience, and social protection for vulnerable households. It is part of a series designed to aid Cambodia's post-pandemic recovery, improve the investment environment, and enhance institutional capacities to manage economic and disaster-related shocks. The initiative emphasizes environmental regulation and disaster risk management, aligning with Cambodia's structural reform needs.

Key Highlights

The initiative is part of a larger Development Policy Operation series, with the first operation approved in 2022. It focuses on boosting private sector competitiveness through streamlined business registration and expanded access to finance for SMEs, strengthening fiscal resilience by enhancing public-private partnership management, and protecting vulnerable households by extending social protection mechanisms. Additionally, it emphasizes improved environmental regulation and disaster risk management to support sustainable growth.
