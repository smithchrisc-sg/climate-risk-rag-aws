Introduction

This solution, Cyclone Reinsurance Pool mandatory participation and PACE Cyclone insurer onboarding addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between Australian Reinsurance Pool Corporation (ARPC), Australian Government, Mandated Australian general insurers, Lloyd’s underwriters, and Unauthorised foreign insurers. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on regulation, collaboration, and accessibility.

Description

The Cyclone Reinsurance Pool, managed by the Australian Reinsurance Pool Corporation (ARPC), mandates participation from Australian general insurers to provide reinsurance for cyclone-related risks. The initiative aims to improve insurance affordability and availability in cyclone-prone regions through centralized, cost-neutral reinsurance pricing. PACE Cyclone is the digital portal used for insurer onboarding and ongoing participation.

Key Highlights

The initiative features a whole-of-market mandate ensuring broad risk pooling, cost-neutral pricing to maximize savings for policyholders, and explicit incentives for risk mitigation. The PACE Cyclone portal facilitates secure and efficient onboarding and data management for insurers. The initiative is backed by a federal guarantee and has the potential to leverage private retrocession in the future.

Results

By January 2025, full participation of mandated insurers was achieved, covering approximately 95% of the market in 2024. Premium reductions of up to 38% were reported in selected regional areas, with an average 39% reduction in home premiums in the highest cyclone risk areas.
