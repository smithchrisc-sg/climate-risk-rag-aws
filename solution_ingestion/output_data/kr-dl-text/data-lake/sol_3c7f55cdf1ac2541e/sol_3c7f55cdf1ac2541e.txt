Introduction

This solution, Financial Support Plan for Addressing Trade Risks, 
Crisis-Response Special Guarantee addresses natural catastrophe in South Korea between Ministry of Economy and Finance (MOEF), Korea Credit Guarantee Fund (KODIT), Korea Technology Finance Corporation (KOTEC), and National Assembly. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

In 2025, South Korea launched a fiscal initiative to bolster public credit guarantee capacity for SMEs affected by trade-related shocks, domestic demand weakness, and disaster-related disruptions. The initiative, funded by a supplementary budget, aimed to expand guarantee supply, increase coverage ratios, provide fee relief, and streamline approvals through the Korea Credit Guarantee Fund (KODIT) and the Korea Technology Finance Corporation (KOTEC). This was part of a broader fiscal support strategy to stabilize financing conditions and bolster demand.

Key Highlights

Rapid policy deployment to address SME finance and demand issues., 
High guarantee coverage with fee relief, reducing the cost of capital and credit risk for SMEs., 
Multi-dimensional targeting capturing trade shocks, regional industrial crises, and disaster zones., 
Integration with broader fiscal stimulus measures like consumption vouchers and infrastructure outlays., 
Non-temporary designation of the loan guarantee intervention, indicating a sustained safety net.

Results

KRW 170 billion allocated to KODIT and KOTEC under the Financial Support Plan., 
KODIT planned to provide KRW 3.3 trillion in liquidity support through the Crisis-Response Special Guarantee., 
Working capital loans up to KRW 500 million per firm with 90% guarantee coverage and fee reductions.
