Introduction

This solution, Bangladesh Sustainable Recovery, Emergency Preparedness and Response Project (B-STRONG) addresses natural catastrophe in Bangladesh between Government of Bangladesh and World Bank. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Bangladesh Sustainable Recovery, Emergency Preparedness and Response Project (B-STRONG) is a World Bank-financed initiative aimed at reducing flood risk and enhancing recovery efforts in Bangladesh, particularly in the Sylhet and Chattogram Divisions. The project focuses on rebuilding critical infrastructure, strengthening flood protection systems, and supporting climate-resilient agriculture and livelihoods. It integrates physical infrastructure improvements with community preparedness and economic inclusion measures to build systemic resilience against natural disasters.

Key Highlights

B-STRONG features an integrated approach combining infrastructure repair with long-term resilience measures, such as climate-proofing roads and bridges and upgrading forecasting systems. It includes the construction of 79 multipurpose flood shelters that double as primary schools, ensuring year-round community value. The project emphasizes gender-responsive components, supporting women's homestead and community gardening, and aims to protect 1.6 million people through its initiatives.

Results

Expected outcomes include the protection of 1.6 million people, construction or rehabilitation of 79 multipurpose flood shelters, support for 380,000 people through economic inclusion and cash-based interventions, and assistance to 65,000 farm households for climate-resilient agriculture.
