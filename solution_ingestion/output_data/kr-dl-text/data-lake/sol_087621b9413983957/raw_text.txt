Introduction

This solution, Regional Drought Resilience Planning Program (Tasmania) — RDRP (Tasmania) addresses natural catastrophe in Australia between Resilience and Recovery Tasmania, Department of Premier and Cabinet (DPAC), Australian Government’s Future Drought Fund, Department of Agriculture, and Fisheries and Forestry (DAFF). The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Regional Drought Resilience Planning Program (Tasmania) is a state-level initiative under the national Future Drought Fund framework, aimed at enhancing drought resilience across Tasmania. It involves creating tailored drought resilience plans for the North, North-West, and South regions of Tasmania, focusing on preparedness, adaptation, and management of future droughts. The program emphasizes community engagement, evidence-based planning, and integration with existing climate adaptation initiatives.

Key Highlights

Place-based tri-regional design with distinct plans for North, North-West, and South regions., 
Evidence-led baseline and tracking through detailed data analyses and a statewide drought resilience assessment map., 
Strong participation architecture with open advisory groups and multi-format engagement., 
Rapid activation via the Quick Wins Grants Program 2025, providing immediate funding to kickstart priority actions., 
Integration with existing climate initiatives like the Tasmanian Coastal Adaptation Pathways and Climate Resilient Councils projects.

Results

Three regional drought resilience plans finalized and published in July 2025., 
Data products including regional Drought Risk, Resilience & Adaptive Capacity Data Reports and a statewide drought resilience assessment map completed in 2023., 
Quick Wins Grants Program 2025 allocated A$330,000 across Tasmania, with individual grants ranging from A$5,000 to A$20,000., 
Open governance structures and stakeholder engagement mechanisms established.
