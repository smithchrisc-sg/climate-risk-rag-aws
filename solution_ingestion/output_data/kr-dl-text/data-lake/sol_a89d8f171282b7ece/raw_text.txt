Introduction

This solution, FY2025 Drainage Development Works addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Singapore between Ministry of Sustainability and the Environment (MSE), PUB, Singapore’s National Water Agency, National Environment Agency (NEA), and Land Transport Authority (LTA). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The FY2025 Drainage Development Works is a S$150 million initiative by Singapore's Ministry of Sustainability and the Environment and PUB, Singapore’s National Water Agency, aimed at reducing flash-flood risks due to intense monsoon rainfall and sea-level interactions. It includes six new projects starting in 2025 and 19 ongoing upgrades, all part of a comprehensive 'Source–Pathway–Receptor' stormwater management framework. The initiative is designed to alleviate flood hotspots and integrate with long-term coastal protection strategies.

Key Highlights

The initiative features a systems-level design under land constraints, legal reinforcement through amendments to the Sewerage and Drainage Act, and integration with coastal protection and transport advisories. It emphasizes rapid operational response, with most flood incidents subsiding within an hour, and aims for system-wide resilience gains rather than expanding drainage capacity.

Results

The initiative has contributed to reducing flood-prone areas from about 3,200 hectares in the 1970s to less than 25 hectares today. Since 2011, approximately S$2.5 billion has been invested in drainage infrastructure, with the FY2025 package funding six new projects and supporting 19 ongoing works.
