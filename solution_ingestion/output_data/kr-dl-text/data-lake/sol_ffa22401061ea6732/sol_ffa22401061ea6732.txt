Introduction

This solution, UNDP Insurance and Risk Finance Facility (IRFF) – Nepal Country Programme addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Nepal between Government of Nepal, United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ), Biodiversity Finance Initiative (BIOFIN), Insurance Development Forum (IDF), and Milliman. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration, leveraging tech, and raising awareness.

Description

The UNDP Insurance and Risk Finance Facility (IRFF) in Nepal aims to enhance the country's financial resilience against natural catastrophes by integrating innovative insurance and risk finance solutions. The initiative focuses on policy and regulatory reforms, market development, and capacity building. It includes a sub-sovereign parametric flood insurance scheme, nature-related insurance for human-wildlife conflict, and agricultural insurance innovations leveraging AI and real-time monitoring. The program aligns with UNDP's broader guidance on integrating climate and disaster risk finance and insurance into national adaptation planning.

Key Highlights

Sub-sovereign parametric flood insurance to protect public assets and services, 
AI-based livestock identity and claims verification, 
Climate-smart platform with real-time loss monitoring for potato farmers, 
Integration with biodiversity finance for human-wildlife conflict, 
Multi-stakeholder Tripartite Agreement model linking UNDP, IDF, and BMZ

Results

One insurance solution in development, 
Three institutional/policy processes underway, 
180 training participants, 
Nepal ranked 10th globally for extreme weather impact, 
Government disaster spending of approximately NPR 50 billion annually from 2012 to 2020
