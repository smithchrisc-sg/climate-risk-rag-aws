Introduction

This solution, Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake (NHC) – National Reinsurance Purchase for NHCover addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in New Zealand between Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake (NHC), International reinsurance markets, and Insurance-linked securities (ILS) investors via Totara Re Pte. Ltd.. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake (NHC) administers the NHCover, a national reinsurance program in New Zealand, funded by levies on insured homeowners. The 2025 expansion of this program, known as the Public Reinsurance Program Expansion, aims to provide comprehensive coverage for natural catastrophe risks beyond earthquakes, as mandated by the Natural Hazards Insurance Act 2023. The program secures large-scale reinsurance capacity from international markets and includes a catastrophe bond to diversify risk and enhance resilience.

Key Highlights

The program secured USD 10.3 billion in reinsurance capacity for 2025, an increase of USD 1.15 billion from 2024, demonstrating strong international market confidence. It includes a USD 225 million catastrophe bond, Totara Re 2023-1, to diversify risk. The program's research-led approach in hazard science and modeling provides transparency and informs national decisions on building standards and planning. The Natural Hazards Insurance Act 2023 modernized governance and broadened the scheme's scope.

Results

The program secured USD 10.3 billion of reinsurance capacity, accessible after NZD 2.2 billion of scheme costs, and included a USD 225 million catastrophe bond. This represents an increase from the 2024 program, which had a reinsurance limit of NZD 9.2 billion.
