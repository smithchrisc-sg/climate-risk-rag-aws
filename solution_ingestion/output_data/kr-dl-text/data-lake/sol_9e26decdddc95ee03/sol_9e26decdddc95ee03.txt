Description

The Sandiganbayan Building II project is a judiciary infrastructure modernization initiative in the Philippines, aimed at enhancing court capacity, efficiency, and public access to justice. Located in Quezon City, this 13-storey, green, ICT-enabled facility will consolidate all seven divisions of the Sandiganbayan anti-graft court, providing sufficient workspace for approximately 600 personnel. It is designed to serve as a model for future court buildings, promoting sustainability and digital readiness.

Key Highlights

The Sandiganbayan Building II is envisioned as a model for future court facilities, featuring a green building design with robust ICT infrastructure. It will have 13 floors and two basement levels, with a gross floor area of up to 24,960 square meters. The project emphasizes co-location of all court divisions to streamline operations and improve public services. The design and procurement processes, including agency-to-agency engagement for architectural design and competitive bidding for civil works, set institutional standards for judiciary infrastructure.

Results

The groundbreaking for the Sandiganbayan Building II was completed in October 2024. The Detailed Architectural and Engineering Design (DAED) has been assigned to the University of the Philippines Building Research Service. Civil works are pending competitive bidding, with the building expected to be operational by 2028.
