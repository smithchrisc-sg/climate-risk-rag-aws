Introduction

This solution, Appointment of PUB as National Coastal Protection Agency addresses natural catastrophe in Singapore between Public Utilities Board (PUB). The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on regulation, collaboration, and leveraging tech.

Description

The Public Utilities Board (PUB) was appointed as Singapore's National Coastal Protection Agency in April 2020. This appointment consolidated the responsibility for defending Singapore's 300 km low-lying coastline against sea-level rise and compound flood risks under a single statutory board. PUB's multi-pronged program includes policy and legislation, standard-setting, advanced modeling, site-specific planning, and a research-and-innovation portfolio. The initiative aims to implement coastal defenses in a timely and flexible manner, integrating hybrid nature-based and hard-infrastructure measures.

Key Highlights

Centralized Governance: PUB's appointment reduces fragmentation and aligns coastal, drainage, and water-supply considerations for whole-of-basin risk management. Legislation and Code of Practice: New legislation and a national Code of Practice standardize design and operations, ensuring effective implementation and lifecycle guidance. Hybrid Solutions: Incorporates nature-based solutions with traditional infrastructure, enhancing multi-functionality and community acceptance. Advanced Modeling: Coastal-Inland Flood Model optimizes interventions across the drainage-coast interface. R&D and Innovation: The Coastal Protection and Flood Management Research Programme (CFRP) accelerates solution development with a dedicated Centre of Excellence.
