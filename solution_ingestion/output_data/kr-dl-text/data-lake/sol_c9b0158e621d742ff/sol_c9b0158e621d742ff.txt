Introduction

This solution, New Zealand Government Landslide Risk Financing Initiatives (2020–2025) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in New Zealand between Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE), GNS Science, National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA), Toka Tū Ake EQC, Christchurch City Council, Insurance Council of New Zealand (ICNZ), NZGS/BRANZ, and Universities. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The New Zealand Government Landslide Risk Financing Initiatives (2020–2025) is a cross-agency, system-wide set of instruments designed to manage landslide risks. It involves national regulatory and building guidance, land-use planning, risk assessment, public information, and insurance arrangements. The initiative aims to identify, reduce, price, transfer, and finance landslide risks, aligning with international risk governance principles and disaster risk reduction ethos.

Key Highlights

The initiative features cross-agency integration, explicit life-safety risk criteria, land-use zoning methods, rapid post-event guidance for repairs, and strong linkage to insurance. It aligns with ISO 31000 and AGS best practices, providing a comprehensive framework for landslide risk management and financing.

Results

The initiative has produced nationally applicable planning and building guidance, improved decision frameworks, enhanced post-event messaging, and strengthened the knowledge base, including a national landslide database. Quantified loss reductions are not published.
