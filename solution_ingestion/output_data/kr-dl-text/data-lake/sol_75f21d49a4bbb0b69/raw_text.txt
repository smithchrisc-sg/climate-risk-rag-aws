Introduction

This solution, Greater Mekong Subregion Flood and Drought Risk Management and Mitigation Project (Cambodia component) addresses natural catastrophe in Cambodia between Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology (MOWRAM), Department of Hydrology and River Works (DHRW), Provincial Department of Water Resources and Meteorology (Pursat), National Flood Forecasting Center (NFFC), Asian Development Bank (ADB), Strategic Climate Fund (SCF), and Greater Mekong Subregion Secretariat. The program was implemented in 2012 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Greater Mekong Subregion Flood and Drought Risk Management and Mitigation Project (Cambodia component) is a comprehensive initiative aimed at addressing the dual challenges of seasonal floods and droughts in Cambodia's Pursat province. The project focuses on rehabilitating the Damnak Chheukrom Mega Irrigation System, enhancing national flood forecasting capabilities, and promoting community-based disaster risk management. It integrates structural upgrades with nonstructural measures, such as community training and gender-targeted initiatives, to improve water management and resilience against climate variability.

Key Highlights

The project uniquely combines flood and drought mitigation through infrastructure rehabilitation and community engagement. It features a blend of climate finance, including loans and grants, and emphasizes gender mainstreaming and community ownership. The initiative also leverages regional knowledge for local implementation, with a strong focus on community-based disaster risk management and water governance.

Results

The project improved water management for approximately 8,000 households across 50 villages, enhancing the management of 16,000–16,100 hectares of agricultural land. It benefited at least 10,000 people through improved flood management, reduced flash floods, and reliable water supply for dry-season cropping. The initiative also led to social benefits, such as reduced migration, and included targeted training for women.
