Description

The Nepal Disaster Risk Management Development Policy Credit with a Catastrophe Deferred Drawdown Option (Cat DDO) is a financial instrument provided by the World Bank to support Nepal in managing disaster risks. It offers a contingent line of credit that can be drawn upon in the event of a natural catastrophe, providing immediate liquidity to the government. The initiative is part of a broader framework for disaster risk management in Nepal, aligned with the Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2017, and involves multiple public and international organizations to strengthen risk analytics, institutional capacity, and policy reforms.

Key Highlights

The Cat DDO provides Nepal with a financial safety net to quickly access funds in the aftermath of a disaster, ensuring rapid response and recovery. It is integrated into Nepal's legal and policy framework for disaster risk management, enhancing the country's preparedness and resilience. The initiative involves collaboration with various international organizations to support complementary risk reduction and preparedness programs.
