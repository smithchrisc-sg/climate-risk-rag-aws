Introduction

This solution, Integration of Anticipatory Action into Nepal’s National Disaster Risk Management (DRM) System addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Nepal between National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Authority (NDRRMA), Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM), Provincial and local governments, UNFPA, UNICEF, UN Women, WFP, WHO, Nepal Red Cross Society (NRCS), and UN Central Emergency Response Fund (CERF). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The initiative aims to integrate anticipatory action (AA) into Nepal's national disaster risk management system, transitioning from reactive to proactive disaster management. It involves the development of the National Anticipatory Action Framework and Roadmap (2026–2030), which institutionalizes forecast-informed, pre-emptive measures across government levels, financing, and delivery systems. The initiative builds on previous AA pilots, particularly the 2021 flood AA pilot, and seeks to establish a coherent, nationally owned system.

Key Highlights

The initiative marks Nepal's first national AA framework and roadmap, shifting from reactive to proactive disaster risk management. It employs a two-step forecast trigger model and pre-arranged financing, proven in the 2021 pilot via CERF. The framework emphasizes a 'whole-of-society' approach, integrating markets, local governments, and communities, and recognizes the need for cross-border early warning systems.

Results

The 2021 flood AA pilot targeted over 80,000 people across 23 municipalities, with up to $6.7 million in pre-arranged CERF financing. The 2025 initiative officially initiated the national framework and roadmap development by NDRRMA.
