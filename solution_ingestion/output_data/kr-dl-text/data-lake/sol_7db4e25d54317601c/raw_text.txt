Introduction

This solution, Northern Rivers Agricultural Drainage Reset Program addresses natural catastrophe in Australia between NSW Reconstruction Authority, Soil Conservation Service, NSW Department of Primary Industries and Regional Development, Local councils and drainage authorities in the Northern Rivers, and Australian Government National Emergency Management Agency. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Northern Rivers Agricultural Drainage Reset Program is a two-year pilot initiative aimed at restoring and upgrading agricultural drainage systems in the Northern Rivers region of New South Wales, Australia. The program seeks to mitigate flood risks and enhance agricultural productivity by cleaning, repairing, and upgrading drainage networks that were damaged during the 2022 floods. It involves collaboration between various public organizations and is part of a broader disaster recovery framework.

Key Highlights

The program is integrated within the NSW Government’s disaster recovery architecture, complementing other initiatives like the Resilient Homes Program. It features innovative barge-based operations for efficient access to riverine sites and involves multi-agency co-funding and co-delivery, spreading cost and risk. Community engagement through door-knocking and pop-up offices ensures that the program's priorities align with local needs.

Results

The program covers 77 agricultural drainage sites across Ballina, Rous, Tweed, and Clarence. In the Tweed River area, 35 out of 43 sites accessible by river were completed using a barge, demonstrating efficient delivery capabilities.
