Introduction

This solution, Ba River Flood Mitigation Dredging addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Fiji between Ministry of Agriculture and Waterways, Hall Contracting Pty Limited, Hall Pacific, and Vanua Ko Ba Holdings. The program was implemented in 2015 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Ba River Flood Mitigation Dredging initiative is a recurring programmatic measure undertaken by the Government of Fiji to mitigate severe flooding in the Ba River catchment area. This initiative involves targeted dredging interventions to increase the river's discharge capacity, thereby safeguarding Ba Town and surrounding communities from riverine and coastal flooding. The most recent dredging phase, conducted from August 2024 to June 2025 by Hall Contracting, removed over 300,000 m3 of sediment from a critically silted 3.6 km stretch of the river mouth, aligning with Fiji's National Flood Management Strategy and broader flood mitigation efforts.

Key Highlights

The initiative is a core component of Fiji's National Flood Management Strategy, aligning with the Sendai Framework and Sustainable Development Goals. It involves the use of cutter suction dredging (CSD) technology to excavate and remove sediment, restoring the river's conveyance capacity. The 2024–2025 dredging campaign was executed by Hall Contracting using the CSD Kikilu, focusing on a 3.6 km stretch from the river mouth to the open sea. This operation is part of a broader programmatic approach that includes complementary reach-scale actions and an adaptive maintenance model to manage sediment in the Ba River system.

Results

The 2024–2025 dredging phase successfully removed over 300,000 m3 of sediment, reducing flood risk for approximately 49,000 people in the Ba River basin. Previous phases, such as the 2015 operation, removed 450,000 m3 of sediment, demonstrating the initiative's ongoing impact on flood mitigation.
