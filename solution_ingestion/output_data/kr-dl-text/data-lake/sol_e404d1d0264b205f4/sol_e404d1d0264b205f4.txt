Introduction

This solution, Cambodia Water Security Improvement Project (P176615) addresses natural catastrophe in Cambodia between Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology (MOWRAM), Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries (MAFF), Provincial Departments of Water Resources and Meteorology (PDWRAMs), Farmer Water User Communities (FWUCs), and The World Bank. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Cambodia Water Security Improvement Project (P176615) aims to enhance water security and resilience against climate and hydrometeorological risks in Cambodia. It focuses on improving water resources planning, infrastructure, and service delivery through a combination of policy strengthening, integrated basin-level planning, and infrastructure investments. The project is co-implemented by the Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry, and Fisheries, with financial support from the World Bank.

Key Highlights

Integration of Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM) and service delivery to reduce fragmentation between planning and operations., 
Mainstreaming of Nature-Based Solutions (NBS) within basin plans and investment selection., 
Explicit multi-purpose storage objective with a target of increasing reservoir retention capacity by 40 million cubic meters., 
Dual-ministry implementation to enhance cross-sector coordination between water management and agricultural productivity.
