Introduction

This solution, Climate Resilience Programme (Otago Regional Council) addresses natural catastrophe in New Zealand between Otago Regional Council, Kānoa – Regional Economic Development & Investment Unit (MBIE), and Te Uru Kahika (Regional and Unitary Councils Aotearoa). The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Climate Resilience Programme (CRP) is a targeted initiative by the Otago Regional Council aimed at reducing flood risks in the Lower Clutha and Lower Taieri regions of Otago, New Zealand. The program consists of four projects that combine engineered flood defenses with nature-supportive measures to address the increasing frequency and severity of heavy rainfall events. It was initiated as part of New Zealand's COVID-19 recovery infrastructure stimulus and extended through the Regional Infrastructure Fund established in 2024.

Key Highlights

The CRP focuses on protecting critical regional infrastructure and highly productive agricultural land through a blend of grey infrastructure upgrades and ecological improvements. Key projects include the West Taieri Contour Channel and bridges upgrade, Outram floodbank seepage mitigation, Riverbank Road stopbank stabilization, and Robson Lagoon flow control structures. The program emphasizes hydraulic capacity, geotechnical robustness, and ecological integration, ensuring both rapid economic stimulus and material risk reduction.

Results

The program secured NZD $5.44 million in initial funding in 2020 and an additional NZD $5.4 million in 2024 for the continuation of the West Taieri Contour Channel Resilience Upgrade. It aims to protect up to 11,300 hectares of agricultural land, Dunedin International Airport, and critical transport routes from flood risks.
