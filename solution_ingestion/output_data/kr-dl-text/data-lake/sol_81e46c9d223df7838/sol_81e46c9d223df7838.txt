Introduction

This solution, National Disaster Risk Reduction Framework (NDRRF) 2017–2030 addresses natural catastrophe in Papua New Guinea between National Disaster Centre (NDC) of Papua New Guinea, Government of Papua New Guinea departments and agencies, United Nations Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction, Framework for Resilient Development in the Pacific (FRDP) 2017–2030, and APEC Disaster Risk Reduction Plan. The program was implemented in 2017 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and regulation.

Description

The National Disaster Risk Reduction Framework (NDRRF) 2017–2030 is Papua New Guinea's central policy instrument for reducing disaster risk and building resilience. It aligns with international frameworks like the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction and the Framework for Resilient Development in the Pacific. The NDRRF provides a whole-of-government approach to disaster risk reduction, setting measurable national targets across various domains such as mortality, economic loss, and infrastructure resilience. It emphasizes multi-hazard early warning systems, risk information access, and international cooperation.

Key Highlights

Alignment with international frameworks such as the Sendai Framework and APEC Disaster Risk Reduction Plan, 
Seven national targets by 2030, including reducing disaster mortality and economic losses, 
Emphasis on multi-hazard early warning systems and risk information access, 
Whole-of-government coordination to prevent policy fragmentation, 
Localization through provincial and local DRR strategies
