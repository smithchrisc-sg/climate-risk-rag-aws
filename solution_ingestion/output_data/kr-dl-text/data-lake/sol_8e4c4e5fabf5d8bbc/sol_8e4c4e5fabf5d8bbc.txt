Introduction

This solution, Connect PNG Infrastructure Development Plan (2020–2040) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Papua New Guinea between Government of Papua New Guinea, Department of Works & Highways (DoWH), Department of Prime Minister & NEC, Provincial governments, and Local-level governments. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

The Connect PNG Infrastructure Development Plan is a 20-year initiative aimed at constructing, rehabilitating, and maintaining 16,000 km of road infrastructure in Papua New Guinea. It seeks to reduce economic isolation, improve market access, and promote long-term growth by creating economic corridors. The plan emphasizes resilient design, innovation in planning and monitoring through GIS and digital tools, and sustained financing to ensure durable and equitable connectivity, particularly for rural and resource-rich areas.

Key Highlights

The program is a statutory, long-horizon initiative with a focus on completing national 'missing links', including the Trans-Island Highway. It integrates with the National Economic Corridor Development Plan and MTDP IV, emphasizing climate-resilient and sustainable roads. The use of GIS mapping, remote sensing, and digital monitoring enhances planning accuracy and maintenance scheduling.

Results

As of the latest reports, the program has achieved 12% completion with approximately K2 billion spent. The target is to complete 2,500 km of new roads by 2027, with a minimum of K500 million/year committed until at least 2026.
