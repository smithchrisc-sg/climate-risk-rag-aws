Introduction

This solution, Full-cost insurance and planting income insurance for rice, wheat, and corn addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in People’s Republic of China between Ministry of Finance (MoF), Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs (MARA), State Administration of Financial Supervision (SAFS), Provincial and local governments, and Licensed insurance companies. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The nationwide rollout of full-cost and planting income insurance for rice, wheat, and corn in China aims to stabilize farmers' incomes, encourage staple grain cultivation, and strengthen national food security. The program, backed by substantial government premium subsidies, covers production risks from natural disasters and market volatility, while also addressing structural risks related to declining staple acreage.

Key Highlights

The program uniquely combines full-cost and revenue protection for staple grains, with high subsidy intensity and alignment with WTO rules. It scales from pilot programs initiated in 2018, integrating into China's rural revitalization and food security strategies. The insurance covers full production costs, including land and labor, and protects against revenue shortfalls due to yield and price risks.

Results

In pilot counties, there was a 48% average increase in the 'security level' for the three staples over the past three years. More than CNY 300 billion in agricultural insurance premium subsidies have been disbursed since 2007, with over CNY 20 billion budgeted in 2024 specifically for rice, wheat, and corn insurance programs.
