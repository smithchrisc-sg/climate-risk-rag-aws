Introduction

This solution, Bangladesh Sustainable Recovery, Emergency Preparedness and Response Project (B-STRONG) addresses natural catastrophe in Bangladesh between Government of Bangladesh, Bangladesh Water Development Board (BWDB), Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE), Bangladesh Agricultural Development Corporation (BADC), Department of Disaster Management (DDM), Local Government Engineering Department (LGED), Social Development Foundation (SDF), International Development Association (IDA), and World Bank Group. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Bangladesh Sustainable Recovery, Emergency Preparedness and Response Project (B-STRONG) is a comprehensive initiative aimed at enhancing flood resilience and recovery in Bangladesh. It focuses on flood protection infrastructure, agricultural restoration, and economic inclusion to mitigate the impacts of riverine and pluvial flooding exacerbated by climate change. The project is financed by the International Development Association of the World Bank Group and involves multiple public agencies in Bangladesh.

Key Highlights

Multi-purpose flood shelters that also serve as primary schools, optimizing public investment., 
Integrated multi-sector design combining infrastructure and community preparedness., 
Targeted agricultural transformation with seed villages and climate-resilient technologies., 
Large-scale economic inclusion with over 380,000 beneficiaries through skills and cash-support programs., 
Robust institutional architecture with multiple ministries and agencies involved.

Results

1.6 million people protected through flood protection infrastructure and shelters., 
79 multi-purpose flood shelters constructed or rehabilitated., 
At least 65,000 farm households supported with climate-resilient technologies., 
Over 380,000 people benefit from economic inclusion and skill development programs.
