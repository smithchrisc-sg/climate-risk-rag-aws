Description

The Sponge City Program (SCP) is a national initiative in China aimed at managing urban stormwater through low-impact, nature-based solutions. It involves the use of blue-green infrastructure, such as permeable pavements and rain gardens, to infiltrate, store, detain, convey, and treat runoff. The program addresses urban flooding, water pollution, water scarcity, and coastal storm surge exposure, with measures tailored to local conditions.

Key Highlights

The SCP employs Nature-based Solutions (NBS) and Low Impact Development (LID) strategies to manage stormwater. It integrates public-private partnerships and involves various stakeholders, including government agencies, real-estate developers, and research institutions. The program is notable for its citywide, multi-scale approach and its focus on sustainable urban development.
