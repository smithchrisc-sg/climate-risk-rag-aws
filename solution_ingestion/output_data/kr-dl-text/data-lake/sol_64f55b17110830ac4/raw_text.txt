Introduction

This solution, Resilient Infrastructure for Adaptation and Vulnerability Reduction (RIVER) Project addresses natural catastrophe in Bangladesh between Local Government Engineering Department (LGED), Government of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, and World Bank. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration, accessibility, and raising awareness.

Description

The RIVER Project is a government initiative in Bangladesh, financed by the World Bank, aimed at reducing vulnerability to riverine and flash floods through climate-resilient infrastructure and community-based capacity building. It focuses on constructing multipurpose flood shelters, improving access roads, and enhancing community preparedness for disaster response.

Key Highlights

The project features dual-use climate-resilient shelters that serve as schools and community centers during non-emergency times and as evacuation centers during floods. It emphasizes inclusive participation, co-creation with local communities, and accountability to affected populations. The project also integrates environmental and social safeguards aligned with the World Bank's frameworks.

Results

The project is ongoing, with anticipated benefits for approximately 1.4 million people across 14 districts. Quantifiable outputs such as the number of shelters and roads constructed are expected to be reported during implementation.
