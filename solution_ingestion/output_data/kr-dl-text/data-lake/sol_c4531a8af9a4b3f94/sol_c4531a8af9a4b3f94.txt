Introduction

This solution, Emergency Flood Assistance Project (EFAP) addresses natural catastrophe in Pakistan between National Highway Authority, Government of Sindh, Works & Services Department, Government of Balochistan, Agriculture and Cooperatives Department, and Asian Development Bank (ADB). The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Emergency Flood Assistance Project (EFAP) is a multi-agency initiative financed by the Asian Development Bank to aid Pakistan's recovery from the 2022 flood catastrophe. With a budget of $531 million, EFAP focuses on restoring connectivity through roads and bridges, improving water availability and flood protection for agriculture, and implementing multi-hazard-resilient design standards. The project involves federal and provincial components, with significant roles played by the National Highway Authority and provincial governments.

Key Highlights

EFAP is structured to provide rapid recovery and medium-term resilience, aligning urgent rehabilitation with long-term risk reduction and social safeguards. It includes a multi-component structure focusing on roads, bridges, and agricultural water management. The project integrates multi-hazard-resilient design criteria and establishes a comprehensive safeguards architecture with a Draft Environmental and Social Management Framework. Transparency and citizen engagement are emphasized through public portals and grievance mechanisms.
