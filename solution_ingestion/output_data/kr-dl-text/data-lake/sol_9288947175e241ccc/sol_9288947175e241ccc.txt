Introduction

This solution, Hazards Insurance Partnership (HIP) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA), Treasury, Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS), Australian Climate Service, Insurance Council of Australia (ICA), Hollard, Munich Re, and Swiss Re. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and regulation.

Description

The Hazards Insurance Partnership (HIP) is a public-private collaboration initiated by the Australian Government to address rising insurance premiums and improve consumer outcomes related to natural hazards intensified by climate change. Announced in the October 2022 Federal Budget, HIP aims to establish a structured workplan for industry and government to jointly identify pressing insurance challenges, share risk information, and target mitigation efforts to make natural hazard coverage more understandable and affordable for consumers.

Key Highlights

HIP is a first-of-its-kind national-scale partnership between government and industry, characterized by its structured collaboration and data-sharing mandate. It explicitly links to public mitigation funding through the Disaster Ready Fund (DRF), aligning risk intelligence with capital deployment to maximize risk reduction. The partnership includes multi-agency and multi-scalar participation, with the involvement of major reinsurers like Munich Re and Swiss Re, embedding global catastrophe risk modeling expertise into its operations.

Results

Quantifiable outcomes such as measurable premium reductions or coverage expansions have not been published. However, HIP has achieved institutionalization and scope growth, with increasing participation from major reinsurers. It has also aligned policy with mitigation funding through the DRF, and there is a commitment to improving consumer clarity regarding hazard coverage.
