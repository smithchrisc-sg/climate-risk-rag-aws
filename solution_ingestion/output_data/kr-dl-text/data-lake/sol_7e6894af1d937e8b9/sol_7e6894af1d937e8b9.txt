Introduction

This solution, Supporting Flood Forecast-Based Action and Learning in Bangladesh (SUFAL), Phase I and Phase II addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Bangladesh between Department of Disaster Management (DDM), Disaster Management Committees (DMCs), Flood Forecasting and Warning Centre (FFWC), Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD), National Taskforce on Forecast-based Financing/Action (FbF/FbA), European Union Civil Protection and Humanitarian Aid Operations (ECHO), Aktion Deutschland Hilft (ADH), USAID, CARE, Concern Worldwide, Islamic Relief Bangladesh, Oxford Policy Management, Regional Integrated Multi-Hazard Early Warning System for Africa and Asia (RIMES), ESDO, FIVDB, POPI, and SKS Foundation. The program was implemented in 2019 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The SUFAL initiative is a multi-partner anticipatory action program aimed at enhancing Bangladesh's capacity to act early based on reliable flood and related hazard forecasts. It focuses on riverine monsoon floods in the Brahmaputra–Jamuna basin and expands to flash floods and lightning in the northeastern Haor wetlands. SUFAL is integrated into Bangladesh's national disaster risk management ecosystem and contributes to the global anticipatory action community.

Key Highlights

SUFAL employs impact-based forecasting to translate hydrological and meteorological forecasts into expected local impacts, improving relevance for communities and decision-makers. It supports the FFWC in generating localized flood forecasts with extended lead times and provides real-time lightning alerts. The initiative also involves risk mapping and localization, setting thresholds and triggers for anticipatory measures, and developing early action protocols.
