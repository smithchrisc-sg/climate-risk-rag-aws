Introduction

This solution, Scaling-up of Glacial Lake Outburst Flood (GLOF) Risk Reduction in Northern Pakistan (GLOF-II) addresses natural catastrophe in Pakistan between Government of Pakistan, Government of Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan Meteorological Department, United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), and Green Climate Fund (GCF). The program was implemented in 2017 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The GLOF-II project aims to mitigate the risks associated with glacial lake outburst floods in Northern Pakistan, particularly in the Gilgit-Baltistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa regions. It builds upon the pilot GLOF-I project by expanding its scope to 12 districts, integrating structural and non-structural measures such as engineering interventions, hydrometeorological monitoring, early warning systems, and community capacity building. The project is funded by the Green Climate Fund and implemented by the UNDP in collaboration with the Government of Pakistan.

Key Highlights

The project features an integrated risk reduction model combining structural measures, hydrometeorological monitoring, and community capacity building. It expands from 2 pilot districts to 12, covering over 7 million people. The project includes a gender assessment and action plan, and it focuses on community-based disaster risk management. It also involves significant technical upgrades for the Pakistan Meteorological Department.

Results

The project is under implementation with targets including 250 engineering structures and comprehensive early warning systems rollout. It aims to cover over 7 million people in the Gilgit-Baltistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa regions. Annual Performance Reports from 2017 to 2023 indicate ongoing construction, equipment installation, and policy enhancements.
