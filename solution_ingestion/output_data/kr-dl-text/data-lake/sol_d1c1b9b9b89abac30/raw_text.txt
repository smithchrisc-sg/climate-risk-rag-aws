Introduction

This solution, Developing Climate Disaster Risk Financing Framework and Parametric Insurance in Fiji addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Fiji between Reserve Bank of Fiji, Ministry of Agriculture and Waterways, United Nations Capital Development Fund (UNCDF), United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), United Nations University Institute for Environment and Human Security (UNU-EHS), India–UN Development Partnership Fund, UN Office for South–South Cooperation (UNOSSC), Governments of Australia, New Zealand, UK, Luxembourg, SUN Insurance, FijiCare, and Tower Insurance. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration, accessibility, and raising awareness.

Description

The initiative aims to enhance Fiji's financial resilience to climate shocks through the deployment of market-based micro and meso-level parametric insurance and complementary climate disaster risk financing instruments. It is part of the Pacific Insurance and Climate Adaptation Programme (PICAP) and involves collaboration between various international, public, and private organizations to provide rapid financial support to vulnerable communities affected by cyclonic winds and heavy rainfall.

Key Highlights

First parametric microinsurance in the Pacific, VAT-free premiums to improve affordability, South–South knowledge transfer leveraging Indian parametric experience, co-creation with communities and insurers, rapid, rules-based payouts, and integration of digital solutions for enrollment and claims.

Results

In the 2024/2025 insurance period, 3,664 individuals enrolled with $2.47 million in protection, over FJ$107,000 in payouts delivered, and 87% of prior payout recipients restarted income-generating activities. The scale-up target is to cover at least 5,000 households (~25,000 people) and include at least 2,000 social welfare recipients.
