Introduction

This solution, Preparing Australia Program (PAP) addresses natural catastrophe in Australia between Australian Government, Minister for Emergency Management and National Recovery and Resilience, National Recovery and Resilience Agency (NRRA), Australian Climate Service (ACS), State and territory governments, Local governments, and Emergency service agencies. The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Preparing Australia Program (PAP) is a $600 million national initiative launched in response to the Royal Commission into National Natural Disaster Arrangements. It aims to reduce disaster risk and build resilience at the community level through locally identified and led projects. The program's Local Stream provides competitive grants for projects that improve resilience and reduce the impacts of future natural hazards such as bushfires, floods, and tropical cyclones.

Key Highlights

The program uses risk-based prioritization informed by the Australian Climate Service to target high-risk areas. It supports locally led projects across social, economic, natural, and built environmental domains, emphasizing community ownership and adaptation. The program allows for a wide range of grant sizes, from $20,000 to $10 million, enabling both small and large-scale projects. It also encourages co-investment and local sourcing to enhance regional capacity.
