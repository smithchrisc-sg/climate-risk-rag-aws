Description

The China–Singapore Joint Call for Proposals on “Climate Resilience & Adaptation” (2025) is a bilateral research funding initiative aimed at strengthening cross-border research collaborations between China and Singapore. It focuses on addressing priority adaptation challenges in urban systems, food systems, public health, and disaster risk, in response to the exceeded 1.5°C warming threshold. The initiative is part of larger national frameworks: CAS’s International Partnership Program in China and Singapore’s RIE2025 plan.

Key Highlights

The initiative is strategically anchored in national programs, enhancing prospects for research continuity and impact scaling. It emphasizes the urgency of practical adaptation solutions due to the breach of the 1.5°C threshold. The call encourages proposals in four comprehensive subtopics while allowing flexibility for high-impact deviations. Governance and accountability are ensured through eligibility criteria and adherence to A*STAR's terms and conditions. Administrative clarity is provided with explicit guidelines for applicants.
