Introduction

This solution, Government Agricultural Insurance Premium Subsidy addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Philippines between Department of Finance (DOF), Insurance Commission, Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC), Department of Agriculture (DA), World Bank, and Palawan Group of Companies/PalawanPay. The program was implemented in 2024, 
2025 and focuses on accessibility, collaboration, and regulation.

Description

The Agricultural Insurance Premium Subsidy Expansion in the Philippines aims to enhance access to subsidized crop and agricultural insurance through the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC). This initiative is part of broader governance and operational reforms led by the Department of Finance, focusing on better subsidy targeting, enabling public-private partnerships, and modernizing product offerings and operations. The expansion seeks to protect farm livelihoods from climate-related and operational risks and increase insurance uptake among vulnerable producers.

Key Highlights

The program is one of the most heavily subsidized agricultural insurance schemes globally. It includes governance reforms such as Insurance Commission oversight and forthcoming PCIC Charter revisions to improve subsidy targeting and enable PPPs. The PCIC–Palawan partnership leverages a wide payout network and digital wallet to enhance last-mile disbursements, especially in remote areas.

Results

In 2024, PCIC insured over 4.2 million farmers, a 7.76% increase from 2023, with total coverage of PHP 141.561 billion. Despite this, only about one-third of farmers are insured, highlighting the need for improved targeting and operational enhancements.
