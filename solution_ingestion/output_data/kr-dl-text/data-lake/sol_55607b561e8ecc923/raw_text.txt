Introduction

This solution, Sindh Flood Emergency Rehabilitation Project (SFERP) addresses natural catastrophe in Pakistan between Government of Sindh and World Bank (International Development Association - IDA). The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Sindh Flood Emergency Rehabilitation Project (SFERP) is a collaborative initiative between the Government of Sindh and the World Bank, launched in 2023 to address the aftermath of the 2022 floods in Sindh, Pakistan. The project aims to rehabilitate critical infrastructure, create short-term livelihood opportunities, and enhance provincial capacity for climate and disaster risk management. It employs a hybrid flood risk management approach that combines conventional civil works with nature-based solutions, such as wetland restoration and vegetative riverbank protection, to achieve cost-effective and durable resilience gains.

Key Highlights

SFERP integrates nature-based solutions with traditional engineering to manage flood risks effectively. It includes infrastructure rehabilitation, livelihoods support, and institutional strengthening. The project is notable for its comprehensive environmental and social management plans and stakeholder engagement strategies, ensuring community involvement and adherence to the World Bank's Environmental and Social Framework. Additional financing of approximately US$150 million was approved in 2025 to scale activities.

Results

As of mid-2025, the project is rated Satisfactory on progress and development objectives, with an overall risk rating of Substantial. Implementation is underway across multiple districts, with formal Environmental and Social Management Plans and Stakeholder Engagement Plans operational.
