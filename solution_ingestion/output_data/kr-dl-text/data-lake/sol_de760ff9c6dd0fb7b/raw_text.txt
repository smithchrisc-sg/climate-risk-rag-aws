Introduction

This solution, Government-led Landslide Risk Reduction and Mitigation Initiatives in Fiji (2020–2025) addresses natural catastrophe in Republic of Fiji between National Disaster Management Office (NDMO), Mineral Resources Department (MRD), Fiji Meteorological Service (FMS), Fiji Roads Authority (FRA), Ministry of Local Government, Municipal Councils, and Planning Authorities. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The Government-led Landslide Risk Reduction and Mitigation Initiatives in Fiji (2020–2025) is a comprehensive portfolio of measures aimed at reducing landslide risks in Fiji. It is aligned with the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction and the National Disaster Risk Reduction Policy. The initiatives focus on risk governance, hazard mapping, risk-informed land-use planning, infrastructure protection, and community preparedness. The program is coordinated by the National Disaster Management Office with technical leadership from the Mineral Resources Department and operational delivery by agencies like the Fiji Roads Authority.

Key Highlights

The initiative is government-led and involves multiple agencies, integrating landslide risk into disaster risk reduction governance. It emphasizes lifeline continuity, mainstreaming hazard information into planning approvals, and aligns with Sendai targets. Unique features include engineering slope stabilization, road corridor protection, and community awareness programs.

Results

The initiative has documented progress in risk knowledge, mainstreaming disaster risk reduction in planning, and protecting critical infrastructure. Multiple slope stabilization works and hazard mapping activities have been completed, and coordination has improved. However, there is a need for more detailed landslide-specific metrics and strengthened monitoring frameworks.
