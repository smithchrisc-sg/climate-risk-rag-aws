Introduction

This solution, ADB–MoF–NDRC Initiative for Parametric Flood Insurance (2021) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in People’s Republic of China between Ministry of Finance (MoF, PRC), National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC, PRC), and Asian Development Bank (ADB). The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The ADB–MoF–NDRC Initiative for Parametric Flood Insurance is a collaborative effort aimed at exploring and designing a parametric flood insurance facility for the Yangtze River basin in China. This initiative seeks to provide rapid financial liquidity following flood events, using pre-agreed triggers based on hazard intensity to ensure fast payouts. It aligns with China's 14th Five-Year Plan and ADB's country partnership strategy, focusing on disaster risk financing and integrated flood risk management.

Key Highlights

The initiative is characterized by its high-level collaboration between ADB, MoF, and NDRC, aligning with national and international strategies for disaster resilience. It emphasizes a basin-wide approach for the Yangtze River, aiming for quick payouts to support emergency response and recovery. The parametric insurance model is designed to be replicable across China and potentially other regions, enhancing fiscal readiness and resilience against flood risks.

Results

The initiative has primarily resulted in knowledge exchange and concept development, with no operational payouts reported as of 2025. It has contributed to policy alignment with China's national strategies and ADB's regional goals, but quantifiable outcomes such as the number of policies or payouts have not been documented.
