Introduction

This solution, World Bank Group’s Disaster Risk Financing and Insurance Program (DRFI/DRFIP) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in China between World Bank Group, Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR), SECO (Switzerland), DFID/FCDO (UK), USAID, Government of Japan, UNISDR/UNDRR, WFP, OECD, and ASEAN+3. The program was implemented in 2010, 
2018 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The World Bank Group’s Disaster Risk Financing and Insurance Program (DRFI/DRFIP) aims to provide financial protection strategies to governments, helping them manage fiscal and economic risks from natural hazards and climate-related shocks. The program supports the design and implementation of comprehensive financial protection strategies, integrating sovereign disaster risk financing, agricultural insurance, property catastrophe risk insurance, and scalable social protection. It leverages partnerships with international organizations and private market actors to enhance financial resilience, particularly in China through the ASEAN+3 regional collaboration.

Key Highlights

The program features cross-cutting collaboration across World Bank Group units and partnerships with organizations like GFDRR and InsuResilience Global Partnership. It emphasizes public-private partnerships to strengthen catastrophe insurance markets and integrates disaster risk financing with macro-fiscal policy and preparedness. The Financial Protection Forum serves as a knowledge platform to disseminate best practices.

Results

The World Bank committed US$7.5 billion to disaster risk management projects in FY24, providing a context for scaling disaster risk financing solutions. The ASEAN+3 regional facility, established in December 2018 with China as a participating economy, aims to enhance financial resilience. Specific outcome metrics for China are not publicly detailed.
