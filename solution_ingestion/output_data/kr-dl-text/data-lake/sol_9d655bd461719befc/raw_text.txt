The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The Philippines First Financial Sector Reform Development Policy Financing (DPF) is a programmatic initiative aimed at supporting the Philippines' financial sector reform agenda. It leverages the World Bank’s Development Policy Financing instrument to provide budget support conditioned on policy and institutional reforms. The initiative is structured around three pillars: strengthening financial sector stability, expanding financial inclusion, and catalyzing climate and disaster risk finance. The program is designed to ensure continuity and momentum of reforms through political transitions, with technical assistance from the World Bank and informed by the 2020 joint IMF–World Bank Financial Sector Assessment Program Update.

Key Highlights

The initiative is characterized by its programmatic operation with continuity across political transitions, ensuring sustained reforms. It is informed by the 2020 joint IMF–World Bank FSAP Update and supported by comprehensive World Bank technical assistance. The three-pronged policy architecture balances safety, inclusion, and sustainability, integrating stability, financial inclusion, and climate/disaster risk finance. Despite substantial overall risk, the operation achieved satisfactory ratings for progress toward the Project Development Objective and implementation progress.

Results

The first and second Financial Sector Reform DPFs together amounted to US$1.0 billion, with US$400 million for the first operation and US$600 million for the second. As of June 26, 2022, the first operation’s progress toward the Project Development Objective and overall implementation progress were rated Satisfactory, with the second DPF under preparation for FY23.
