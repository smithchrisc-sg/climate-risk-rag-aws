Introduction

This solution, Development Policy Financing with a Catastrophe Deferred Drawdown Option (Cat DDO) – Nepal, 2020–2025 addresses natural catastrophe in Nepal between Government of Nepal (Ministry of Finance and competent DRM agencies), World Bank, and Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR). The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Catastrophe Deferred Drawdown Option (Cat DDO) for Nepal is a contingent credit line provided by the World Bank to offer immediate budget support in the aftermath of natural disasters. It is part of the World Bank's broader Disaster Risk Financing and Insurance Program (DRFIP), in collaboration with GFDRR, aimed at enhancing financial protection against natural hazards. The Cat DDO is embedded within a development policy financing operation that supports disaster risk management reforms and readiness in Nepal.

Key Highlights

Immediate budget support upon disaster shock; Anchored in a DRM policy reform program; Part of a larger World Bank–GFDRR DRFIP agenda; Complements market insurance and regional risk pools; Supported by analytics and capacity building.

Results

Nepal-specific quantitative results are not included; globally, Cat DDOs have been approved for countries such as Colombia, Costa Rica, and the Philippines with cumulative amounts exceeding US$1.3 billion.
