Introduction

This solution, Nepal Insurance Authority (NIA) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Nepal between Government of Nepal, Nepal Rastra Bank, Nepal Insurance Authority, International Association of Insurance Supervisors, Insurers, Reinsurers, Intermediaries (brokers, agents, surveyors), and Industry associations. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on regulation, collaboration, and raising awareness.

Description

The Nepal Insurance Authority (NIA) is the statutory regulator of Nepal’s insurance and reinsurance market, established under the Insurance Act, 2079 (2022). It replaced the former Insurance Board (Beema Samiti) to modernize insurance supervision, enhance market stability, and improve consumer outcomes. NIA is tasked with licensing, setting capital and solvency standards, supervising market conduct, approving products, regulating distribution, managing consumer complaints, and driving financial inclusion and market modernization.

Key Highlights

The NIA was elevated from a board to an autonomous authority with expanded powers. It introduced tightened paid-up capital thresholds, codified reinsurance prioritization for domestic capacity, and mandated microinsurance and inclusion. The authority also standardized market conduct and grievance redress and digitalized supervisory data.

Results

The establishment of NIA led to market consolidation and recapitalization, strengthened domestic reinsurance utilization, formalized market conduct and product approval, and expanded grievance redress channels with digitized supervisory reporting.
