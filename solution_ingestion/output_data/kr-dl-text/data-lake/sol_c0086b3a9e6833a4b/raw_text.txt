Introduction

This solution, IBRD Catastrophe Bonds for Earthquakes and Tropical Cyclones addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Philippines between Republic of the Philippines (Department of Finance; National Treasury), World Bank (IBRD), GC Securities, Swiss Re Capital Markets, Munich Re Capital Markets, and AIR Worldwide (now Verisk Extreme Event Solutions). The program was implemented in 2019 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The IBRD Catastrophe Bonds for Earthquakes and Tropical Cyclones were issued by the World Bank to provide the Philippines with financial protection against natural disasters. These bonds, part of the IBRD Capital-at-Risk Notes program, offer up to USD 225 million in coverage for earthquake and tropical cyclone risks. The bonds are structured to provide rapid payouts based on modeled loss triggers, ensuring quick financial relief to the Philippines in the event of a disaster.

Key Highlights

The initiative marked several firsts, including being the first sovereign-sponsored catastrophe bond from Asia and the first World Bank bond listed on the Singapore Exchange. The bonds feature a modeled-loss trigger mechanism, allowing for quick payouts without lengthy claims processes. The stepwise payout design offers clarity on financial relief based on the severity of the disaster, and the fixed premium ensures cost predictability over the bond's term.

Results

The bonds secured USD 225 million in catastrophe risk protection, with USD 75 million allocated for earthquakes and USD 150 million for tropical cyclones. The transaction was successfully marketed and settled at the targeted size, indicating strong investor demand.
