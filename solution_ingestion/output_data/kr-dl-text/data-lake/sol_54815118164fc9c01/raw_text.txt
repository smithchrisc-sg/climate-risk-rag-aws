Description

The Republic of Korea Landslide Information System (LIS) is a comprehensive, multi-component system designed to monitor, model, and provide early warnings for landslides across South Korea. It integrates efforts from various public and academic institutions to address the increasing risk of rainfall-induced landslides, exacerbated by climate change. The system includes national forecasting by the Korea Forest Service, machine learning-based susceptibility mapping by the OJeong Resilience Institute, and in-situ monitoring by KIGAM, providing a robust framework for landslide risk management.

Key Highlights

Multi-resolution architecture combining national-scale warnings, high-resolution susceptibility mapping, and ground-truth process instrumentation., 
Data-rich machine learning pipeline trained on over 4,000 historical events., 
Semi-automated daily to twice-daily operational cadence synchronized with hazardous weather windows., 
Real-time monitoring of matric suction and rainfall for direct estimation of slope stability., 
Hybrid integrated models for evidence-based microzonation with high predictive performance.

Results

Approximately 2,345 hectares affected by landslides over the last five years, highlighting the need for higher-resolution monitoring and early warning systems.
