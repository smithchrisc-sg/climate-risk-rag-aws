Introduction

This solution, Strengthening Institutions and Empowering Localities Against Disasters and Climate Change in the Philippines (SHIELD Programme) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Philippines between Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), Office of Civil Defense (OCD), Department of Science and Technology (DOST), United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), UN-Habitat, Philippine Business for Social Progress (PBSP), National Resilience Council (NRC), and Consortium of Bangsamoro Civil Society (CBCS). The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The SHIELD Programme is a five-year initiative in the Philippines, supported by Australia and led by UNDP, aimed at enhancing local climate and disaster resilience. It operates in 11 vulnerable provinces, focusing on translating climate and disaster science into actionable local resilience strategies. The programme involves multi-stakeholder collaboration at the local government level, national agency prioritization, and the provision of tailored scientific information to support local decision-making.

Key Highlights

The SHIELD Programme is unique in its multi-stakeholder approach, involving a consortium of international, public, and private organizations. It emphasizes the integration of scientific data into local planning and decision-making processes, and it aims to unlock funding for resilience actions at the local government level. The programme also includes a dedicated SHIELD Support Unit for monitoring, evaluation, and partnership brokering.

Results

Early results indicate strong stakeholder mobilization and institutional structuring, but quantitative outcome data is limited due to the programme's mid-course stage.
