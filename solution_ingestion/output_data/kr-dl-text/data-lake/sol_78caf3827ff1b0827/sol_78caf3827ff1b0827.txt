Introduction

This solution, Fourth Disaster Risk Management Development Policy Loan with a Catastrophe-Deferred Drawdown Option (CAT-DDO 4) addresses natural catastrophe in Republic of the Philippines between Government of the Republic of the Philippines, Department of Finance, Bureau of the Treasury, International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD), and World Bank Group. The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Fourth Disaster Risk Management Development Policy Loan with a Catastrophe-Deferred Drawdown Option (CAT-DDO 4) is a financial instrument designed to provide the Philippines with immediate access to funds following a state of calamity or public health emergency. It is part of a broader disaster risk financing strategy that includes catastrophe bonds and insurance arrangements, aimed at providing a multi-tiered response to natural disasters and public health emergencies.

Key Highlights

The CAT-DDO 4 offers a contingent credit line of US$500 million, which can be rapidly accessed upon the declaration of a State of Calamity or State of Public Health Emergency. It features a 3-year drawdown period, renewable up to 15 years, and a revolving feature allowing re-withdrawal of repaid amounts. The program requires the Philippines to maintain a satisfactory disaster and public health risk management program, monitored by the World Bank.

Results

The Philippines has utilized Cat DDOs since before 2018, drawing almost US$500 million after Typhoon Mangkhut in 2018. The CAT-DDO 4 was signed in November 2021, providing a financial safety net for immediate liquidity needs during disasters.
