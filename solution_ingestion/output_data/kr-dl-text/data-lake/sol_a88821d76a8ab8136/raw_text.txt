Introduction

This solution, Sindh Water and Agriculture Transformation Project (SWAT) addresses natural catastrophe in Pakistan between Government of Sindh, Irrigation Department, Agriculture Department, Sindh Irrigation & Drainage Authority (SIDA), Area Water Boards (AWBs), Farmers’ Organizations (FOs), Planning & Development Department/PCMU, World Bank, and FAO. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and regulation.

Description

The Sindh Water and Agriculture Transformation (SWAT) Project is a World Bank-financed initiative by the Government of Sindh, launched in 2023. It aims to enhance agricultural water productivity, strengthen integrated water resources management, and support the recovery of small and medium-sized farmers affected by the 2022 floods. The project involves policy reform, institutional strengthening, canal modernization, and a smart-subsidy scheme to promote water-efficient, high-value crops. It is aligned with Pakistan’s Vision 2025 and the National Water Policy 2018, and guided by the Sindh Strategic Water Plan.

Key Highlights

The project is the first in Pakistan to formalize contractual water service obligations to Farmers' Organizations and to develop the Sindh Strategic Water Plan. It includes a Hydro-Agro Informatics program and a smart-subsidy scheme targeting 220,000 acres. Early milestones include the approval of the Sindh Water Policy, establishment of governance arrangements, and initiation of environmental and social safeguard frameworks.

Results

Institutional milestones achieved include the launch of the project, constitution of the Project Steering Committee, delivery of training, and approval of the Sindh Water Policy. Quantified outcome reporting is still limited in publicly available sources.
