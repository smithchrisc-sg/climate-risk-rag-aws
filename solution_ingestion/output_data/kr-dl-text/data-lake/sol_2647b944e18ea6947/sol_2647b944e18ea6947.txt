Introduction

This solution, Philippine City Disaster Insurance Pool (PCDIP) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Republic of the Philippines between Department of Finance (DOF), Bureau of the Treasury (BTr), Government Service Insurance System (GSIS), cities/local government units (LGUs), Asian Development Bank (ADB), Asia-Pacific Climate Finance Fund (ACliFF), InsuResilience Global Partnership, SEADRIF Insurance Company, Global Shield initiative, and International reinsurance market. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Philippine City Disaster Insurance Pool (PCDIP) is a pioneering city-level parametric disaster insurance scheme in Southeast Asia, designed to provide rapid financial liquidity to cities in the Philippines following natural catastrophes such as typhoons and earthquakes. By pooling the risks of participating cities, the initiative aims to improve market access and affordability of insurance, ensuring that funds are quickly available for early response and recovery efforts.

Key Highlights

PCDIP is the first city-level parametric disaster insurance scheme in Southeast Asia, offering a pooled risk structure to enhance affordability and market access. It utilizes parametric triggers based on hazard parameters, enabling near-immediate payouts to participating cities. The initiative is integrated with the national Disaster Risk Financing and Insurance Strategy and the Disaster Resilience Improvement Program, ensuring alignment with broader institutional reforms.

Results

Quantified city-level payout data have not been published, but the design builds on the Philippines’ earlier sovereign parametric program that made three payouts totaling about $28 million, demonstrating the viability of rapid liquidity mechanisms.
