Introduction

This solution, KFS Comprehensive and Sectoral Implementation Plans for Landslide Prevention addresses natural catastrophe in Republic of Korea between Korea Forest Service (KFS), Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs (MAFRA), Ministry of the Interior and Safety, Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport (MOLIT), Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy (MOTIE), and Cultural Heritage Administration. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The KFS Comprehensive and Sectoral Implementation Plans for Landslide Prevention is an initiative led by the Korea Forest Service to mitigate landslide risks in the Republic of Korea. It involves cross-ministerial integration of slope-risk data into a national Digital Slope Integrated Landslide Information System, upgrading landslide early warning systems, expanding management to residential areas, and establishing a Digital Landslide Response Team. The initiative aligns with the National Forest Plan's disaster prevention and public safety objectives.

Key Highlights

The initiative features cross-ministerial data integration to eliminate prediction blind spots, a three-stage early warning system to provide more evacuation lead time, and a focus on expanding risk management to residential areas. It also includes a research-to-operations pipeline supported by the National Institute of Forest Science for climate-informed customization of warnings.
