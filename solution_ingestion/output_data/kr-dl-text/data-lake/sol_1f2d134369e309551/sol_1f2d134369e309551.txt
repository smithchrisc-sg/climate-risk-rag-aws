Introduction

This solution, National Indemnity Insurance Program (NIIP) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Philippines between Bureau of the Treasury (BTr), Department of Finance (DOF), Government Service Insurance System (GSIS), Department of Education (DepEd), Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) Monetary Board, World Bank Group, Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), and Sompo Risk Management Inc.. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The National Indemnity Insurance Program (NIIP) is a government-backed indemnity insurance scheme in the Philippines designed to provide comprehensive coverage for strategically important public assets against physical disaster risks such as typhoons and earthquakes. The program consolidates asset risks into a unified portfolio, enabling better risk diversification and more predictable claim servicing. It aims to cushion public finances against unexpected losses and ensure prompt, pre-arranged funding for reconstruction.

Key Highlights

The NIIP employs a strategic portfolio approach, moving away from fragmented single-asset insurance to create better risk diversification and premium efficiency. The pilot's premium was financed using excess payout from a catastrophe bond, demonstrating a sophisticated, layered risk financing strategy. The program also features formalized claims-to-budget channels to reduce disbursement lags and improve reconstruction timelines. Additionally, the development of a web-based national asset registry strengthens exposure data and supports scalable expansion.

Results

In 2024, the NIIP insured 132,862 DepEd school buildings nationwide, with an approximate insured asset value over PHP 800 billion. The program's operational readiness at a national scale for a large asset class was demonstrated. The GSIS reported a capacity of roughly PHP 843.11 billion for disaster insurance support, indicating significant underwriting capacity for such portfolios.
