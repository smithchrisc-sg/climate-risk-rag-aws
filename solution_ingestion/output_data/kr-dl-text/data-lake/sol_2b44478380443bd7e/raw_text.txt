Introduction

This solution, Engagement with the Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Insurance Facility (SEADRIF) on Disaster Risk Financing in Myanmar (SEADRIF–Myanmar DRF Engagement) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Myanmar between Government of Myanmar, Ministry of Planning and Finance, Disaster management institutions, SEADRIF Council, World Bank Group, Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR), ASEAN community, and SEADRIF Insurance Company (Singapore). The program was implemented in Cannot be determined and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The SEADRIF–Myanmar DRF Engagement is a country-level policy and technical initiative under the SEADRIF regional platform, aimed at strengthening Myanmar's financial resilience against natural hazards. It provides ASEAN members, including Myanmar, with financial protection solutions such as sovereign catastrophe insurance and risk analytics. Despite constraints since 2021, the engagement remains crucial for Myanmar's high exposure to cyclones, floods, and earthquakes, offering pre-arranged disaster response financing.

Key Highlights

SEADRIF operates an insurance company in Singapore to provide rapid liquidity to governments post-disasters. Myanmar's participation in SEADRIF's governance and technical dialogue positions it to access pre-arranged financial instruments like parametric sovereign insurance. The initiative is part of a broader regional effort to enhance financial resilience against natural hazards in ASEAN.
