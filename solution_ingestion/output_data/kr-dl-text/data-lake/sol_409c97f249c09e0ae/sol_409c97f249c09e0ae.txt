Introduction

This solution, Sindh Floods Emergency Housing Reconstruction Project (SFEHRP) addresses natural catastrophe in Pakistan between Government of Sindh, Sindh People’s Housing for Flood Affectees (SPHF), World Bank, and Asian Development Bank (ADB). The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Sindh Floods Emergency Housing Reconstruction Project (SFEHRP) is a provincial initiative in Pakistan's Sindh province aimed at rebuilding and safeguarding homes and communities affected by severe flooding. Implemented by the Sindh People’s Housing for Flood Affectees (SPHF), a public limited company under the Government of Sindh, the project is supported by the World Bank and the Asian Development Bank. It operates under a comprehensive framework of environmental and social safeguards to manage risks, ensure social protection, and engage stakeholders inclusively.

Key Highlights

The project is unique in its dual-MDB support structure, with both the World Bank and ADB financing distinct components under a common provincial program. It is implemented by SPHF, a dedicated Section 42 public limited company, which allows for focused governance and operational flexibility. The initiative is guided by a comprehensive set of publicly disclosed safeguards frameworks, including an Environmental and Social Management Framework (ESMF), a Resettlement Policy Framework (RPF), and a Stakeholder Engagement Plan (SEP).

Results

Quantitative outcomes are not reported in the cited safeguard documents; the focus is on risk control, inclusion, and accountability rather than output tallies. Implementation is ongoing.
