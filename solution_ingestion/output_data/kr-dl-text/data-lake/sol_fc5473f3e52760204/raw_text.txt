Introduction

This solution, Korean National Drought Information and Analysis Center (NDIAC) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in South Korea between Korea Water Resources Corporation (K-water), National Drought Mitigation Center (NDMC), and University of Nebraska–Lincoln. The program was implemented in 2019 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Korean National Drought Information and Analysis Center (NDIAC) was established to enhance South Korea's national drought early warning and risk management capabilities. It operates as a national platform under the Korea Water Resources Corporation (K-water) and collaborates with the National Drought Mitigation Center (NDMC) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln. The center aims to institutionalize drought risk governance by leveraging international expertise and domestic capacities, focusing on meteorological, hydrological, and agricultural drought risks.

Key Highlights

The center was launched during the International Asian Drought Forum, emphasizing its integration into a broader international dialogue on drought management. A formal partnership with NDMC was established through a memorandum of understanding (MOU), facilitating knowledge transfer and capacity building. The center focuses on improving early warning systems and risk management practices, aligning with global best practices.

Results

Documented milestones include the center's launch and the execution of the MOU in 2019, with ongoing collaboration in 2020. While specific performance metrics are not provided, foundational achievements include the establishment of international cooperation, targeted training for K-water staff, and integration into a global community of practice.
