Introduction

This solution, Structural Integrity Assessment and Retrofit Design of the Sandiganbayan Centennial Building addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Philippines between Sandiganbayan, Supreme Court of the Philippines, Government procurement bodies, University of the Philippines Building Research Service (UP BRS), University of the Philippines College of Engineering, and National Engineering Center. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Structural Integrity Assessment and Retrofit Design of the Sandiganbayan Centennial Building was initiated to ensure the safety and operational continuity of the aging judicial facility in Quezon City, Philippines. Led by the University of the Philippines Building Research Service, the project involved a comprehensive structural evaluation and retrofit design to address safety deficiencies. This initiative is part of a broader modernization agenda, which includes the construction of a new, green, ICT-ready Sandiganbayan Building II, expected to be operational by 2028.

Key Highlights

The initiative features an independent academic partner, UP BRS, ensuring technical independence and alignment with public-sector governance. It integrates with the development of a new green and ICT-enabled facility, Sandiganbayan Building II, under Supreme Court oversight and government procurement compliance. The project addresses the needs of approximately 600 personnel and a high volume of public transactions, ensuring infrastructure decisions are grounded in operational realities.

Results

The project completed the structural integrity assessment and retrofit design for the Centennial Building, providing a technical basis for risk-informed maintenance and structural interventions. It aligns with the broader modernization timeline, supporting a managed transition to the new facility by 2028 without compromising current service delivery.
