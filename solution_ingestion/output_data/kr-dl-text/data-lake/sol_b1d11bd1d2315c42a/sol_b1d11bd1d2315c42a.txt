Introduction

This solution, All-of-Government (AoG) Risk Financing and Insurance addresses cannot be determined through public private partnerships in New Zealand between Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE), New Zealand Government Procurement (NZGP), Aon New Zealand Ltd, Crombie Lockwood (NZ), Jardine Lloyd Thompson (JLT) Ltd, Marsh Limited, and Willis New Zealand Ltd. The program was implemented in 2015 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The All-of-Government (AoG) Risk Financing and Insurance initiative, led by the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE), is designed to manage insurable risks for New Zealand's central government agencies. It establishes a panel of insurance intermediaries to advise and place risks, using a data-driven approach to cluster agencies by risk profile for collaborative risk financing. The program aims to enhance risk management maturity and explore alternative risk financing options over time.

Key Highlights

The initiative features an exclusive panel model ensuring capacity and expertise, a cluster-based procurement strategy using agency insurance data, and a closed, sequential RFP process for tailored solutions. It aligns with AoG procurement frameworks to centralize and professionalize market engagement, aiming for economies of scale and improved data analytics.

Results

The AoG Risk Financing and Insurance Intermediary Services Panel was established in 2015 with five providers. In 2025, it evolved into a new AoG Insurance Brokering services panel with seven providers, indicating continuity and maturation. Quantified savings or outcomes are not provided.
