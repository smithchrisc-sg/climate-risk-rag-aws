Introduction

This solution, Australia’s Cyclone Recovery Program (CRP) addresses natural catastrophe in Fiji between Australian Government (Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade), Australian High Commission, Fiji, and Government of Fiji (Ministry of Education). The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and accessibility.

Description

Australia’s Cyclone Recovery Program (CRP) in Fiji is a post-disaster reconstruction initiative funded by the Australian Government through the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT). It focuses on rebuilding key education infrastructure to climate-resilient standards following the devastation of Tropical Cyclone Yasa in December 2020. The program aims to deliver quality, inclusive, and climate-resilient school facilities under the Fiji–Australia Vuvale Partnership framework.

Key Highlights

The program emphasizes 'building back better' by reconstructing nine schools in Vanua Levu to withstand future cyclones and climate-related hazards. It focuses on durable construction, accessibility, inclusion, and improved WASH/learning environments, representing a tangible commitment to resilient, human-centered development outcomes.

Results

Nine schools, including Wailevu West District School, were reconstructed, benefiting students, teachers, and surrounding communities in Vanua Levu.
