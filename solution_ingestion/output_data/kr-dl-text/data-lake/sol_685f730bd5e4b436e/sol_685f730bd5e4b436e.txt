Introduction

This solution, National Plan for Disaster Management (NPDM) 2021–2025 addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Bangladesh between Ministry of Disaster Management and Relief (MoDMR), Department of Disaster Management, Government of Bangladesh (GoB) line ministries, United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), and National Resilience Program (NRP). The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The National Plan for Disaster Management (NPDM) 2021–2025 is Bangladesh's comprehensive strategy for disaster risk reduction, preparedness, response, and recovery. It is led by the Ministry of Disaster Management and Relief and aligns with international frameworks like the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction. The plan emphasizes risk-informed development, stronger governance, and inclusion, particularly for people with disabilities, while integrating with national development plans such as Vision 2041 and the Bangladesh Delta Plan 2100.

Key Highlights

The NPDM 2021–2025 is distinctive for its integration with national development and climate agendas, its focus on disability-inclusive disaster risk management, and its emphasis on urban risk governance. It includes phased, prioritized actions through 2025 and promotes risk governance and accountability. The plan also highlights the role of the private sector in resilience building and aligns with international frameworks to ensure coherent disaster risk management.

Results

The plan has led to system-level consolidation of disaster risk reduction governance and alignment with national and international frameworks. However, there is limited publicly available, plan-specific quantifiable performance data, indicating a need for improved results reporting and monitoring and evaluation transparency.
