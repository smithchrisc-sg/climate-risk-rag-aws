Introduction

This solution, Scaling Climate and Disaster Risk Financing Framework and Parametric Insurance in Fiji addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Fiji between Government of Fiji, Reserve Bank of Fiji, United Nations Capital Development Fund (UNCDF), India–UN Development Partnership Fund, UN Office for South–South Cooperation (UNOSSC), United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Asian Development Bank (ADB), FijiCare Insurance Limited, Sun Insurance, Tower Insurance, Digicel, and Vodafone. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration, accessibility, and leveraging tech.

Description

The initiative, titled 'Scaling Climate and Disaster Risk Financing Framework and Parametric Insurance in Fiji,' aims to provide rapid financial relief following extreme weather events through parametric insurance. Launched in 2020 and scaled in 2023, it integrates public policy, development finance, private insurance, and digital payment systems to deliver quick payouts. The program targets vulnerable groups, including low-to-moderate income households, farmers, women, and MSMEs, with a focus on tropical cyclone winds and heavy rainfall.

Key Highlights

The initiative features VAT-exempt premiums, rapid digital payouts via mobile money, and explicit inclusion targets for women and persons with disabilities. It is supported by South–South cooperation with India and integrates with national policy objectives. The program uses parametric triggers for quick payouts, reducing delays and ambiguity, and includes outreach campaigns to increase literacy and uptake among vulnerable groups.

Results

As of now, the program has covered 14,000 individuals and 2,000 social welfare recipient households, with over 1,000 beneficiaries receiving payouts after excessive rainfall in 2022–2023. In 2023, 1,013 farmers were paid FJD 201,000, with payouts delivered within two weeks. The next target is to cover 5,000 households and 200 MSMEs in the scaling phase.
