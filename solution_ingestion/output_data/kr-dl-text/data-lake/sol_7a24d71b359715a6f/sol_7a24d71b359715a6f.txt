Introduction

This solution, Cyclone Reinsurance Pool addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between Australian Reinsurance Pool Corporation (ARPC), Australian Government Treasury, Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC), Bureau of Meteorology (BoM), and Private insurers. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Cyclone Reinsurance Pool, administered by the Australian Reinsurance Pool Corporation (ARPC), is a government-backed reinsurance mechanism designed to address the financial risks associated with tropical cyclones and related flood damage in Australia. It allows primary insurers to transfer cyclone-related risks to ARPC, supported by a A$10 billion Commonwealth guarantee. The pool aims to reduce reinsurance costs for high-risk properties, thereby lowering premiums for policyholders in cyclone-prone areas.

Key Highlights

The Cyclone Reinsurance Pool is backed by a A$10 billion Commonwealth guarantee, ensuring liquidity and solvency during severe cyclone seasons. It covers household, strata, and small business properties across Australia, with a focus on cyclone-prone regions. The pool's pricing structure recognizes property-level mitigation measures, offering premium discounts for strengthened construction and flood defenses. The ARPC's PACE Cyclone portal facilitates administrative management of ceded exposure, premiums, and claims, while collecting data to inform government disaster planning.

Results

As of 30 June 2024, 18 insurers had joined the pool, representing near-universal coverage across home and strata portfolios in cyclone-prone regions. The pool commenced operations on 1 July 2022, with the first risk accepted in January 2023.
