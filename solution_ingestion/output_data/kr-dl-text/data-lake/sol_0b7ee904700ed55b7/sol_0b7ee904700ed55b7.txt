Introduction

This solution, Government‑Led Efforts to Increase Earthquake Insurance Penetration in China (2020–2025) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in People’s Republic of China between PICC Property & Casualty, Government of China, World Bank, Munich Re, Swiss Re, AIR Worldwide, RMS, and EQECAT. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Government‑Led Efforts to Increase Earthquake Insurance Penetration in China (2020–2025) aimed to address the country's earthquake protection gap through preparatory actions. These efforts included policy advocacy for a public‑private earthquake insurance mechanism, technical enablement via catastrophe modeling, and market design discussions on parametric triggers and catastrophe bonds. The initiative focused on creating a foundation for a national earthquake insurance pool and leveraging capital markets to enhance insurance penetration and disaster risk management.

Key Highlights

The initiative emphasized the feasibility of parametric solutions using independent seismic data and the necessity of capital market capacity, such as catastrophe bonds, as China's insured values grow. It also focused on enhancing data transparency and modeling capabilities, with PICC releasing a China Earthquake Casualty Catastrophe Model in 2025 to aid in insurance pricing and disaster prevention.

Results

As of 2025, earthquake insurance penetration remained low, with prior estimates of approximately 5% penetration on commercial property. The PICC's release of the catastrophe model in 2025 was a concrete deliverable, but no national earthquake pool or cat bond issuance was documented.
