Introduction

This solution, Building Resilience to Climate Change in Papua New Guinea (ADB Project 46495-002) addresses natural catastrophe in Papua New Guinea between Government of Papua New Guinea and Asian Development Bank (ADB). The program was implemented in 2015 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The Building Resilience to Climate Change in Papua New Guinea initiative, administered by the Asian Development Bank, aims to enhance the country's resilience to climate-related hazards. The project focuses on deploying climate-resilience investments and embedding these within ADB’s environmental and social risk management system. It utilizes an Environmental Assessment and Review Framework (EARF) and Environmental and Social Monitoring Reports (ESMRs) to ensure compliance with environmental and social safeguards, transparency, and accountability.

Key Highlights

Programmatic safeguards instrument (EARF) enabling consistent screening and risk categorization across diverse subprojects., 
Continuous public disclosure and monitoring through regular ESMRs and audited financial statements., 
Integration within ADB’s broader environmental and social requirements and accountability mechanisms.

Results

Safeguards compliance and transparency through regular ESMRs., 
Financial governance evidenced by audited project financial statements from 2020 to 2023., 
Lifecycle continuity from 2015 to 2025, demonstrating sustained implementation and monitoring capacity.
