Introduction

This solution, Building Safety and Resilience in the Pacific Phase II – Papua New Guinea Country Program (BSRP II – PNG) addresses natural catastrophe in Papua New Guinea between National Disaster Centre, National Weather Service, Port Moresby Geophysical Observatory, Department of Community Development and Religion, Department of Education, Department of National Planning and Monitoring, The Pacific Community (SPC), and European Union. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Building Safety and Resilience in the Pacific Phase II – Papua New Guinea Country Program (BSRP II – PNG) is a national initiative under a broader regional effort led by The Pacific Community (SPC) and funded by the European Union. It aims to reduce disaster and climate risks in Papua New Guinea by addressing governance gaps, enhancing early warning systems, upgrading seismic networks, improving data management for vulnerable populations, and integrating disaster risk management into school curricula.

Key Highlights

The program includes the conversion of five national seismic stations to broadband, establishment of a National Multi-Hazard Early Warning Centre, development of a national school building infrastructure policy, rollout of a fire safety curriculum, and creation of an integrated data system for disability and elderly populations. It emphasizes strong national governance through a steering committee and regional technical support.

Results

The program was officially launched in May 2025, with initial training activities reported. Planned actions for 2025 include equipment procurement for the early warning center, enhancement of the Lae Seismic Station, and re-establishment of a database for vulnerable populations. The national allocation is EUR 770,000, with an additional EUR 93,242 for regional activities in PNG.
