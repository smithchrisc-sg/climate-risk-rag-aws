Introduction

This solution, Fourth Disaster Risk Management Development Policy Loan with a Catastrophe‑Deferred Drawdown Option (CAT‑DDO 4) addresses natural catastrophe in Republic of the Philippines between Government of the Republic of the Philippines, International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD), and World Bank. The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Fourth Disaster Risk Management Development Policy Loan with a Catastrophe‑Deferred Drawdown Option (CAT‑DDO 4) is a $500 million contingent credit facility provided by the World Bank to the Philippines. It aims to enhance the country's institutional and financial capacity to manage risks from climate change, natural disasters, and disease outbreaks. The facility offers rapid-access liquidity following qualifying shocks, complementing other risk transfer solutions and fiscal buffers to ensure timely response and recovery.

Key Highlights

CAT‑DDO 4 provides immediate liquidity upon government declaration of a State of Calamity or Public Health Emergency, minimizing response time. It is embedded in a Development Policy Loan that supports disaster risk management and financing policy reforms. The facility is part of a layered risk finance strategy that includes parametric insurance, microinsurance, reinsurance, and a sovereign catastrophe bond, offering a diversified safety net for fiscal resilience. It is initially available for three years, with the possibility of renewal up to 15 years.

Results

The facility was fully disbursed by its closing date in 2023, with an initial drawdown of $80 million following Super Typhoon Rai in December 2021. The program's objective to strengthen institutional and financial capacity was achieved, as evidenced by the rapid operationalization and full utilization of the facility.
