Introduction

This solution, Assessment of Government Drought Risk Reduction and Mitigation Initiatives in Myanmar (2020–2025) addresses natural catastrophe in Myanmar between Department of Meteorology and Hydrology (DMH), Relief and Resettlement Department (RRD), Ministry of Social Welfare, Relief and Resettlement, Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation (MOALI), National Water Resources Committee (NWRC), State/Region and Township Disaster Management Committees, United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR), Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), World Bank, and Asian Development Bank (ADB). The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The 'Assessment of Government Drought Risk Reduction and Mitigation Initiatives in Myanmar (2020–2025)' is a composite evaluation of Myanmar's efforts to reduce and mitigate drought risks during the specified period. It synthesizes actions documented under various national and regional frameworks, including the Myanmar Action Plan on Disaster Risk Reduction (MAPDRR), the updated Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC), and ASEAN's AADMER Work Programme. The assessment highlights improvements in technical capacity for drought monitoring and policy intent, but notes constraints in sustained mitigation financing and local implementation.

Key Highlights

The assessment is part of broader frameworks such as the MAPDRR 2017–2030, which sets targets for hazard risk reduction, and the NDC 2021, which aligns with the Paris Agreement. It also involves the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction and ASEAN's AADMER Work Programme. Key public actors include the Department of Meteorology and Hydrology and the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock and Irrigation, while international partners like UNDP and FAO support climate resilience and drought-resilient agriculture.

Results

Progress has been mixed, with improvements in technical capacity and policy intent but limited measurable reductions in drought losses due to constraints in financing and coordination.
