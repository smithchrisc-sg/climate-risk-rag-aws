Introduction

This solution, Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Insurance Facility (SEADRIF) 2.0: Boosting Financial Resilience in Southeast Asia addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Singapore, 
ASEAN+3 region between ASEAN Secretariat, ASEAN+3 governments, World Bank Group, Asian Development Bank (ADB), Asian Development Bank Institute (ADBI), Global Asia Insurance Partnership (GAIP), SEADRIF Insurance Company, and Intertrust (Singapore) Ltd. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

SEADRIF 2.0 is a World Bank-supported initiative aimed at enhancing financial resilience against natural catastrophes in Southeast Asia. It operates as a regional platform under the ASEAN+3 political umbrella, providing pre-arranged disaster risk financing and insurance solutions. The initiative is anchored in Singapore, with SEADRIF Insurance Company and SEADRIF Trust serving as key operational entities. SEADRIF 2.0 focuses on rapid post-disaster response through parametric insurance and pre-arranged financing, facilitating quick payouts and reducing the financial impact of disasters on member countries.

Key Highlights

SEADRIF 2.0 builds on the success of its predecessor by expanding its institutional architecture and partnerships. It leverages Singapore's regulatory environment to provide transparent governance and access to international re/insurance markets. The initiative's parametric insurance model ensures rapid liquidity for member countries, with payouts triggered by objective hazard indicators. SEADRIF 2.0 also emphasizes regional risk pooling, shared services, and governance reforms to enhance operational efficiency and sustainability.

Results

SEADRIF 2.0 has demonstrated its effectiveness through rapid payouts, such as a US$2 million disbursement to Lao PDR within six days following severe flooding. The initiative's governance reforms have reduced operating expenses by over 25%, increasing its cost-effectiveness and credibility.
