Introduction

This solution, ORC NbS Investigations (2023–2025) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in New Zealand between Otago Regional Council (ORC), Ministry for the Environment (MfE), Boffa Miskell Ltd, and GHD. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and regulation.

Description

The ORC NbS Investigations (2023–2025) is an initiative by the Otago Regional Council in New Zealand to explore and implement nature-based solutions (NbS) for flood resilience, water quality improvement, and biodiversity enhancement. The program aims to mainstream NbS into regional work programs by conducting feasibility assessments, developing technical specifications, and engaging stakeholders. It is part of a broader effort to align with national and global biodiversity frameworks and is supported by partnerships with mana whenua and technical experts.

Key Highlights

The initiative features a strong partnership with mana whenua, ensuring cultural integration and governance perspectives. It includes the development of a formal Technical Specification for NbS, rapid flood hazard modeling, and a structured regulatory pathway to facilitate implementation. The project is aligned with global standards and aims to enhance the 'investability' of nature-related projects by reducing perceived risks for investors.

Results

The initiative has produced multiple technical and engagement outputs, including evidence of the effectiveness of NbS, such as wetlands reducing flood peaks by up to 50%. It has also led to a council commitment to embed NbS into regional programs, creating a structured pathway for implementation and finance readiness.
