Introduction

This solution, Bangladesh Insurance Sector Development Project (ISDP) addresses retirement in Bangladesh between People’s Republic of Bangladesh, Insurance Development and Regulatory Authority (IDRA), Bangladesh Insurance Academy (BIA), State-owned insurance corporations, and World Bank. The program was implemented in 2017 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The Bangladesh Insurance Sector Development Project (ISDP) is a World Bank-financed initiative aimed at strengthening the institutional capacity of Bangladesh's insurance sector and expanding insurance coverage nationwide. The project focuses on regulatory capacity building, modernization of state-owned insurance corporations, and systematic project implementation, management, and monitoring. It addresses institutional and market-structure constraints that limit insurance penetration and sector performance, with a low environmental and social risk profile.

Key Highlights

The ISDP emphasizes dual-focus institutional strengthening by targeting both the regulator (IDRA) and state-owned insurers, with BIA as the human capital pillar. The project is categorized as low environmental and social risk (Category C) and ensures transparent implementation planning through public disclosure of procurement plans. Additionally, the World Bank Inspection Panel provides an independent complaint mechanism for accountability.

Results

The project aims to increase insurance coverage in Bangladesh by improving supervision, market conduct, service delivery, and public trust in insurance products. It seeks to enhance financial resilience of households and firms through broader and better insurance protection, contributing to financial sector stability and inclusive growth.
