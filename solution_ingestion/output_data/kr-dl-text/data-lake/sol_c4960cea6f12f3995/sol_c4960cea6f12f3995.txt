Introduction

This solution, Climate Investment Funds – Pilot Program for Climate Resilience (PPCR) in Papua New Guinea addresses natural catastrophe in Papua New Guinea between Climate Change and Development Authority (CCDA), Government of Papua New Guinea, Climate Investment Funds (CIF), and Asian Development Bank (ADB). The program was implemented in 2015 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Pilot Program for Climate Resilience (PPCR) in Papua New Guinea is a key initiative under the Climate Investment Funds (CIF) aimed at enhancing climate resilience in vulnerable countries. In PNG, the program is implemented through the Asian Development Bank's project 'Building Resilience to Climate Change in Papua New Guinea' (BRCC). The initiative focuses on mainstreaming climate resilience into national development planning, climate-proofing infrastructure, and enhancing food security and fisheries. It operates within a broader Pacific regional framework, collaborating with Samoa and Tonga to leverage regional cooperation and knowledge sharing.

Key Highlights

The PPCR in PNG is characterized by its programmatic and regional approach, integrating climate resilience into national development planning and infrastructure projects. It involves a layered financing structure with significant contributions from the CIF and the Government of Australia. The initiative emphasizes vulnerability assessments, adaptation planning, and climate-proofing critical infrastructure, such as ports and roads. It also focuses on building capacities at various levels, from communities to government agencies, to effectively plan and respond to climate impacts.

Results

The program has secured US$29.91 million in CIF-approved funding, with an expected co-financing of US$6.66 million. It has facilitated climate-resilient infrastructure projects, such as the climate-proofing of the Alotau Provincial Wharf, and supported capacity building for climate resilience and post-harvest technologies for smallholder coffee farmers.
