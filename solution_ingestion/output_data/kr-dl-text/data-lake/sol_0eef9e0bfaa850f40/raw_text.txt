Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA)

Introduction

This solution, PUB’s 2025 Drainage Upgrading Works addresses natural catastrophe in Singapore between Public Utilities Board (PUB) and Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

Singapore's 2025 drainage upgrade projects, led by the Public Utilities Board (PUB), aim to mitigate urban flood risks exacerbated by high-intensity rainfall and tidal interactions. With a S$150 million investment, the initiative includes the Syed Alwi Pumping Station, Bukit Timah Canal upgrade, and Sungei Serangoon subsidiary drains. These projects are part of Singapore's national Drainage Improvement Programme, following the Source–Pathway–Receptor flood risk management framework. The projects focus on increasing conveyance, managing storage, and protecting downstream areas to reduce flash flood risks in urban catchments.

Key Highlights

Syed Alwi Pumping Station: Utilizes high-capacity pumps and automated controls to manage stormwater in tide-influenced basins, ensuring rapid drawdown during intense rainfall., 
Bukit Timah Canal Upgrade: Enhances canal capacity through widening and deepening to handle higher rainfall, reducing overbank flooding., 
Sungei Serangoon Subsidiary Drains: Upsizes neighborhood drains to eliminate local ponding and improve response, protecting residential and transport assets.
