Introduction

This solution, Inclusive insurance and risk financing in Pakistan. Snapshot and way forward 2024 addresses natural catastrophe in Pakistan between UNDP Country Offices, United Nations Development Programme’s Insurance and Risk Finance Facility (UNDP IRFF), and German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ). The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The UNDP IRFF diagnostic for Pakistan, titled 'Inclusive insurance and risk financing in Pakistan. Snapshot and way forward 2024,' is a strategic planning tool aimed at enhancing financial resilience through inclusive insurance and disaster risk financing. It identifies key risks such as floods, drought, tropical cyclones, and earthquakes, and provides actionable recommendations to expand financial protection and inform policy and programming.

Key Highlights

Focus on micro-takaful and microinsurance to cater to cultural and market needs., 
Recommendation for compulsory catastrophe insurance for public infrastructure to mitigate fiscal shocks., 
Emphasis on enhancing underwriting capabilities to improve market capacity., 
Integration into a global diagnostics series for cross-country learning and benchmarking.

Results

The diagnostic was published on September 3, 2024, and a national disaster risk financing strategy is under development. Quantifiable implementation outcomes are not reported at this stage.
