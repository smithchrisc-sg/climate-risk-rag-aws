Introduction

This solution, ASEAN+3 Climate Disaster Risk Financing Collaboration (SEADRIF) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in South Korea between ASEAN Secretariat, ASEAN+3 member authorities, SEADRIF Insurance Company, World Bank, AMRO, ADB Institute, Hong Kong Monetary Authority, and Global Asia Insurance Partnership. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The ASEAN+3 Climate Disaster Risk Financing Collaboration, centered on the Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Insurance Facility (SEADRIF), is a regional risk financing platform designed to provide pre-arranged finance and insurance for climate and disaster shocks. It operates under the ASEAN+3 political umbrella, offering rapid, rules-based insurance disbursements and capacity-building across the region. SEADRIF aims to enhance fiscal resilience and regional financial stability through its integration with ASEAN+3's broader disaster risk finance architecture.

Key Highlights

SEADRIF is the first regional disaster risk financing facility in Asia, providing government-owned, not-for-profit insurance solutions. It features rapid payout mechanisms using official national impact data, progressive thresholds for cumulative events, and integration with ASEAN+3 DRF policy frameworks. The facility also offers technical assistance and knowledge sharing, with potential synergy with the Chiang Mai Initiative Multilateralization (CMIM) Rapid Financing Facility.

Results

In 2025, SEADRIF achieved a US$2 million payout to Lao PDR within 6 days based on official impact data. Over 30 ASEAN+3 senior policymakers participated in leadership training, and ongoing policy development for the 2026–2028 DRFI Roadmap was initiated.
