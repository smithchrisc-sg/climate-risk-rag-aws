The Philippine Health Insurance Corporation, widely known as "PhilHealth," is a government-organized health insurance program that ensures free healthcare for all Filipino citizens. Funded partly by government subsidies at both the local and national levels, as well as through company payroll deductions, PhilHealth embodies the Philippines' commitment to universal health coverage. The program is structured into two categories: those who can afford to pay premiums and those who cannot, such as the unemployed and elderly. For individuals unable to pay, the government steps in to sponsor their insurance coverage, thereby maintaining equitable access to healthcare services for all.

PhilHealth's array of health and welfare programs focuses on enhancing well-being and preparedness among its members. This includes initiatives like the PhilHealthy Movement, corporate events such as PhilHealthy Team Day, PhilHealth Family Day, and One PhilHealth Team Day, various medical programs, and disaster and emergency preparedness initiatives. In addition, the corporation is engaged in various learning and development programs. The Universal Healthcare Bill (UHB), signed into law by Filipino President Rodrigo Duterte in early 2019, marked a pivotal expansion in PhilHealth’s mandate. This legislation aims to include all citizens within the PhilHealth system, not just those formally employed, thereby extending its reach to informal workers and the unemployed.

As of June 30, 2023, PhilHealth boasts a remarkable enrolment of 103.8 million beneficiaries. This figure reflects the program's success following the implementation of the UHB, illustrating a significant stride towards achieving universal health coverage (UHC) in the Philippines. The extensive enrolment demonstrates PhilHealth's effectiveness in providing healthcare access to every Filipino, irrespective of their employment status or financial capacity.
