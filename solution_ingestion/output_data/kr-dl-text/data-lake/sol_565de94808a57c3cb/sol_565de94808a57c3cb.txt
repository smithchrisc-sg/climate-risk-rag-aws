Introduction

This solution, Building Resilience to Climate Change in Papua New Guinea (BRCC) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Papua New Guinea between Government of Papua New Guinea, Climate Change and Development Authority (CCDA), Provincial and local governments across ARoB, East New Britain, Manus, Milne Bay, and Morobe, Asian Development Bank (ADB), Climate Investment Funds (CIF), Government of Australia, PNG Ports Corporation Limited, National Maritime Safety Authority (NMSA), National Information & Communications Technology Authority (NICTA), and Particip GmbH. The program was implemented in 2015 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and regulation.

Description

The Building Resilience to Climate Change in Papua New Guinea (BRCC) project is a comprehensive initiative aimed at integrating climate risk management into subnational development planning, piloting community-level adaptation, and establishing a framework for climate-resilient infrastructure. It focuses on vulnerable atoll and island communities across five provinces, addressing climate change impacts through assessments, planning, and infrastructure development.

Key Highlights

First climate-proofed wharf pilot in PNG, serving as a model for future resilient marine infrastructure., 
Integration of climate change vulnerability and adaptation plans (CCVAPs) into local government plans., 
Ridge-to-reef and ecosystem-based adaptation strategies, including mangrove and coral rehabilitation., 
Establishment of early warning and communication networks across multiple provinces., 
Development of climate-resilient building codes and design standards.

Results

21 climate change vulnerability assessments completed., 
Early warning and communication systems installed in several provinces., 
142 water tanks and sanitation facilities delivered and installed., 
Marine resource assessments and ecosystem rehabilitation pilots conducted., 
Approximately 13,000 direct beneficiaries across 21 island/atoll communities., 
Ground-breaking for the Alotau Wharf, designed as the first climate-proofed wharf in PNG.
