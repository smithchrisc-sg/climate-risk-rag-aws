Introduction

This solution, Statutory Disaster-Related Budget Arrangements in Myanmar addresses natural catastrophe in Myanmar between Government of Myanmar, International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC), and United Nations Development Programme (UNDP). The program was implemented in 2013 and focuses on regulation and collaboration.

Description

The statutory disaster-related budget arrangements in Myanmar are designed to provide rapid public financing for disaster preparedness, response, early recovery, and rehabilitation. These arrangements are governed by the Natural Disaster Management Law (2013), its implementing Rules (2015), and provisions in the annual Union Budget Law. They establish a multi-tier Disaster Management Fund (DMF) and authorize budget flexibilities, such as contingency appropriations and in-year supplementary budget procedures, to enable swift mobilization and reallocation of public resources after disasters.

Key Highlights

The statutory arrangements have been consistently used across major events, including the 2015 nationwide floods, the COVID-19 pandemic starting in 2020, and Cyclone Mocha in 2023. These arrangements serve as Myanmar's primary fiscal response mechanism to disasters, demonstrating their persistence and effectiveness in mobilizing resources quickly.

Results

The arrangements have been successfully deployed in response to several major disasters, illustrating their effectiveness in enabling swift fiscal responses. However, specific quantifiable results such as the amount of money mobilized or the number of people impacted are not provided.
