Introduction

This solution, Cyclone Reinsurance Pool (CRP) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between Australian Reinsurance Pool Corporation (ARPC), Australian Government (Commonwealth of Australia), Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC), and Insurers. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The Cyclone Reinsurance Pool (CRP) is a government-backed initiative in Australia, administered by the Australian Reinsurance Pool Corporation (ARPC). It aims to provide affordable and available reinsurance solutions for cyclone and cyclone-related flood risks, particularly in cyclone-prone regions like Northern Australia. The CRP operates under a AUD 10 billion Commonwealth guarantee, allowing for risk-based reinsurance pricing without private-market profit margins. It covers household, strata, and SME property insurance, with insurers ceding cyclone risk to the pool in exchange for a premium.

Key Highlights

The CRP is distinguished by its government-backed AUD 10 billion guarantee, enabling sustainable pricing and capacity during severe cyclone seasons. It targets cyclone-prone regions, especially Northern Australia, where affordability pressures are most intense. The pool's pricing is risk-based and recognizes mitigation efforts, offering discounts for risk mitigation actions. Transparency is maintained through regular pricing reviews and reports, with independent monitoring by the ACCC to ensure consumer outcomes.

Results

By mid-2024, the CRP had onboarded 18 insurers, achieving near-universal exposure. The initiative has supported affordability and availability in cyclone-prone regions, with ongoing input from industry and research stakeholders to refine its design and operations.
