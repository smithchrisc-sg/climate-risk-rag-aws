Description

The National Seismic Hazard Assessment of Papua New Guinea (NSHA-PNG) and the National Probabilistic Seismic Hazard Assessment for Papua New Guinea (2019 Revision, PSHA19) are initiatives led by Geoscience Australia to provide updated seismic hazard estimates for PNG. These initiatives aim to modernize earthquake design actions by integrating contemporary hazard science into building standards. The Interim Amendment to PNGS 1001-1982: Part 4 updates design hazard levels and introduces modern loading approaches, aligning with practices in Australia and New Zealand. This effort is supported by stakeholder engagement, in-country training, and open technical documentation to ensure safer construction practices in PNG.

Key Highlights

The initiative includes a probabilistic seismic hazard model (PSHA19/NSHA-PNG) that provides updated ground shaking estimates. The Interim Amendment integrates this hazard into PNG’s design practice with modern load assessment methods. The initiative also features capacity-building through in-country seminars and professional development videos to support adoption. The alignment with Australian and New Zealand methods ensures regional consistency and leverages familiar methodologies among practitioners.

Results

The publication of PSHA19 (GA Record 2020/029) and the delivery of interim design hazard guidance have been achieved. In-country seminars and professional development video products have been conducted, identifying and mitigating under-design. Future regulatory development needs have been outlined.
