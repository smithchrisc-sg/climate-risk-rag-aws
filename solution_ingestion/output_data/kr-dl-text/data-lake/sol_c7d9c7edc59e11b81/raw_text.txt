The program was implemented in 2010 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and regulation.

Description

The Greater Mekong Subregion Flood and Drought Risk Management and Mitigation Project (Cambodia) – Technical Assistance is part of a larger initiative by the Asian Development Bank to enhance climate and disaster resilience in the Lower Mekong Basin. The project focuses on preparing national roadmaps, prioritizing interventions, and conducting feasibility studies to support flood and drought risk management in Cambodia. It includes structural measures like irrigation and flood control infrastructure, and non-structural measures such as forecasting and community risk management.

Key Highlights

Integration of ecosystem-based adaptation into national design guidelines; explicit transboundary action plan with Viet Nam; institutionalization via the National Flood Forecasting Center (NFFC); large-scale irrigation and drainage rehabilitation covering over 16,000 hectares; strong gender mainstreaming with 30% female representation in community-based disaster risk management committees.

Results

The project delivered national roadmaps, feasibility studies, and design guidelines. It strengthened the NFFC's capacity, agreed on a cross-border action plan with Viet Nam, rehabilitated the Damnak Chheukrom irrigation system benefiting ~8,000 households, improved flood management for at least 10,000 people, and trained ≥200 women with ≥30% female representation in CBDRM committees.
