Introduction

This solution, DRRS Fiji: Disaster Risk Finance Specialist Engagement addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Fiji between Government of Fiji, Fiji's Ministry of Finance, Netherlands Enterprise Agency (RVO), Pacific Islands Forum Secretariat (PIFS), and The Global Shield against Climate Risks. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The DRRS Fiji initiative is a targeted expert assignment aimed at addressing flood risk and building resilience in the Ba River catchment area. It is part of a broader intervention led by the Netherlands Enterprise Agency, focusing on identifying and mobilizing financing for flood risk reduction. The initiative integrates financing strategies with technical flood risk analysis and institutional capacity assessments to close critical financing gaps in Fiji, aligning with the country's climate and disaster risk finance agenda.

Key Highlights

The initiative is tailored to Small Island Developing States (SIDS) with a focus on rigorous technical flood diagnostics and resource mobilization. It emphasizes gender diversity and inclusion in expert selection and encourages national expert participation. The engagement is aligned with Fiji's national and regional disaster risk finance agendas, aiming to produce an actionable financing roadmap.

Results

As the initiative is set for 2025, outcomes are prospective. Expected outputs include a mapped financing landscape, gap analysis, prioritized financing options with indicative costs, and a donor engagement plan. Fiji's baseline climate-related economic loss is about FJ$500 million annually, highlighting the potential value of improved disaster risk financing.
