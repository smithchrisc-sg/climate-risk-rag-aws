Introduction

This solution, Insurance and Risk Finance Initiative for Nepal (IRFF) – 2024 Country Diagnostic and Implementation Pathway addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Nepal between Government of Nepal, Public authorities responsible for disaster risk management, financial sector regulation, and insurance policy, United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Nepal’s insurance market stakeholders, Microinsurance channels, and Agriculture insurance entities. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration, accessibility, and raising awareness.

Description

The Insurance and Risk Finance Initiative for Nepal is a national engagement under the UNDP's Insurance & Risk Finance Facility (IRFF), aimed at expanding inclusive insurance and strengthening disaster risk financing (DRF) in Nepal. The initiative involves a comprehensive diagnostic to map priority hazards, review the insurance and DRF landscape, and identify legal instruments to support the expansion of insurance coverage and risk financing. It emphasizes strategic moves such as expanding distribution channels, building public-sector capacity, addressing disaster risk assessment gaps, and leveraging existing government-subsidized agriculture insurance.

Key Highlights

The initiative is part of a global effort to advance risk-transfer and risk-financing solutions to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). It emphasizes expanding distribution channels, building public-sector capacity, and leveraging existing government-subsidized agriculture insurance. The diagnostic provides a roadmap for scaling inclusive insurance and DRF, aligning with Nepal’s national disaster risk strategy and broader climate and development agendas.

Results

The initiative has published a comprehensive diagnostic and recommendations, identifying priority hazards, policy baselines, and capacity and data gaps. However, quantifiable implementation outcomes beyond the diagnostic have not yet been publicly reported as of September 2025.
