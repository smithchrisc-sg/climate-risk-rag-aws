Introduction

This solution, Pacific Catastrophe Risk Assessment and Financing Initiative (PCRAFI) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Papua New Guinea between World Bank, Asian Development Bank (ADB), Secretariat of the Pacific Community (SPC) via SOPAC, Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR), ACP–EU Natural Disaster Risk Reduction Programme, Global Affairs Canada, AIR Worldwide, GNS Science, Geoscience Australia, Pacific Disaster Center (PDC), and OpenGeo. The program was implemented in 2006 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

The Pacific Catastrophe Risk Assessment and Financing Initiative (PCRAFI) is a multi-partner initiative aimed at improving the fiscal resilience of Pacific Island Countries, including Papua New Guinea, against natural disasters. It combines risk information and analytics, financial solutions, and institutional capacity building to address risks from tropical cyclones, earthquakes, and tsunamis. The initiative provides sovereign insurance and risk financing through the Pacific Catastrophe Risk Insurance Company (PCRIC) and the Pacific Catastrophe Risk Insurance Foundation (PCRIF).

Key Highlights

PCRAFI utilizes the Pacific Risk Information System (PacRIS) to house country-specific exposure inventories and hazard layers, supporting planning and fiscal risk assessment. It offers sovereign climate and disaster risk insurance, providing rapid liquidity post-disaster. The initiative has developed new insurance products for excessive rainfall and drought, expanding coverage beyond tropical cyclones and earthquakes. Governance and capacity building are integral, with training for national agencies to use PacRIS and interpret model outputs.
