Introduction

This solution, Cyclone-related Government Risk Financing in Myanmar, 
Disaster Management Fund (DMF), 
Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Insurance Facility (SEADRIF) addresses natural catastrophe in Myanmar between National Disaster Management Committee (NDMC), Ministry of Social Welfare, Relief and Resettlement (MoSWRR), Ministry of Planning and Finance, United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR), World Bank/GFDRR, United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (UN OCHA), and Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Insurance Facility (SEADRIF). The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

Cyclone-related Government Risk Financing in Myanmar (2020–2025) is a set of public financial management instruments and regional risk-sharing arrangements designed to provide rapid, rules-based financing to prepare for and respond to cyclone impacts. It is anchored in Myanmar’s Disaster Management Law and Rules and connected to the ASEAN-led Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Insurance Facility (SEADRIF). The initiative aims to complement humanitarian funding and international support, focusing on wind damage, coastal storm surge, and severe flooding.

Key Highlights

The initiative includes the Disaster Management Fund (DMF) established by the 2013 Natural Disaster Management Law, budgetary contingency mechanisms, and participation in the ASEAN disaster risk financing ecosystem via SEADRIF. It coordinates with humanitarian financing mechanisms and involves technical engagement with international partners on sovereign disaster risk financing diagnostics and strategy development.

Results

The initiative faced significant political and operational disruptions after February 2021, which affected the scale and speed of deploying domestic instruments during major events such as Cyclone Mocha in May 2023. Myanmar's active uptake of SEADRIF insurance products during 2020–2025 was limited or not publicly documented.
