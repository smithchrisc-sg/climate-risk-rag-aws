Description

The Korean Landslide Early Warning System (KLES) is a national initiative operated by the Korea Forest Service (KFS) to provide 24-hour landslide warnings at local administrative levels. It integrates threshold-based criteria, high-resolution spatial targeting, and short-range meteorological inputs to assess and disseminate landslide risk, particularly focusing on rainfall-induced landslides and post-wildfire slope failures. The system is supported by scientific analyses from the National Institute of Forest Science (NIFoS) and real-time data from the Korea Institute of Geoscience and Mineral Resources (KIGAM).

Key Highlights

KLES utilizes a threshold-based decision logic for issuing warnings, with customized protocols for post-wildfire areas. It relies on short-range weather forecasts, with research indicating higher accuracy for forecasts issued at 5:00 PM. Recent advancements include the integration of machine-learning-based susceptibility mapping to enhance hazard identification.
