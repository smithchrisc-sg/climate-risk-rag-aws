Introduction

This solution, National Flood Warning Infrastructure Network (FWIN) Program addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between Australian Bureau of Meteorology, Australian Government, Queensland Reconstruction Authority, Local Government Association of Queensland, and AQX Solutions. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration, leveraging tech, and regulation.

Description

The National Flood Warning Infrastructure Network (FWIN) Program is an initiative led by the Australian Bureau of Meteorology to modernize and centralize Australia's flood warning infrastructure. It aims to reduce the loss of life, property damage, and service disruption caused by riverine and flash flooding by upgrading and acquiring high-priority flood warning assets across the country. The program involves collaboration with state and local governments, as well as private sector partners, to improve the reliability, coverage, and timeliness of flood warnings.

Key Highlights

National-scale ownership simplification to address governance fragmentation and improve accountability., 
A 10-year, $236 million funding commitment for sustainable operations and maintenance., 
Standards-led, gap-driven expansion based on a 2020 Scoping Study., 
Structured state delivery model with a seven-region approach in Queensland., 
Explicit cost-sharing for maintenance between the Bureau and Queensland State Government.
