Introduction

This solution, Sindh Resilience Project – Additional Financing (SRP–AF) addresses natural catastrophe in Pakistan between Government of Sindh, Sindh Irrigation Department (SID), Provincial Disaster Management Authority, Sindh (PDMA Sindh), World Bank (International Development Association, and IDA). The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Sindh Resilience Project – Additional Financing (SRP–AF) is an initiative aimed at enhancing resilience to natural disasters and public health emergencies in Sindh, Pakistan. It is an expansion of the parent Sindh Resilience Project, focusing on mitigating flood and drought risks and strengthening disaster and emergency management capacity. The project is implemented by the Government of Sindh and financed by the World Bank, with a focus on infrastructure for risk mitigation, systems and institutions for emergency management, and flexible financing for rapid response.

Key Highlights

Integration of public health emergencies into disaster risk management, 
Establishment of Sindh Emergency Service for first-response capabilities, 
Decision Support System for flood forecasting and operations, 
Contingent Emergency Response Component for rapid crisis response, 
Disaster Risk Financing to enhance fiscal resilience

Results

Over 4.5 million beneficiaries from enhanced flood protection and small recharge dams, 
Completion of eight flood embankments totaling 198 km, 
Completion of four small dams with additional eight financed from cost savings, 
Satisfactory ratings in financial management, procurement, and safeguards compliance
