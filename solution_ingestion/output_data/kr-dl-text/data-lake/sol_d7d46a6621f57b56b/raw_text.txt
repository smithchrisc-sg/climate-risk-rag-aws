Introduction

This solution, Crucial Access Links Program addresses natural catastrophe in Australia between Queensland Reconstruction Authority (QRA), Australian Government, Queensland Government, and Fourteen remote local councils. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Crucial Access Links Program is a resilience investment initiative aimed at maintaining connectivity for remote communities in Queensland, Australia, during severe weather events. With a budget of $88 million, the program focuses on repairing and upgrading critical access infrastructure such as airstrips, boat ramps, seaports, and access routes. It is led by the Queensland Reconstruction Authority and funded through efficiencies under the joint Commonwealth–state Disaster Recovery Funding Arrangements. The program is designed to mitigate isolation risks during natural disasters, ensuring the continuity of essential supplies and services.

Key Highlights

The program is fully funded by efficiencies realized under the DRFA, repurposing savings from recovery frameworks into preventive resilience investments., 
It targets 14 of Queensland’s most remote councils, focusing on communities with the greatest isolation risk during severe weather., 
The program supports multi-modal access infrastructure, including airstrips, boat ramps, seaports, and access routes, to enhance redundancy and maintain connectivity., 
Projects are selected based on Regional Resilience Strategies and Local Resilience Action Plans, ensuring alignment with local risk reduction priorities., 
Incorporates lessons from the 2025 floods to inform project selection and design standards.

Results

Total investment of $88 million., 
15 high-impact projects across 14 remote councils., 
Expected outcomes include improved resilience and connectivity during severe weather events, reduced risk of isolation, and enhanced continuity of essential supplies.
