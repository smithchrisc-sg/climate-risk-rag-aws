Introduction

This solution, Cyclone Preparedness Programme (CPP) Modernization addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Bangladesh between Government of Bangladesh, Bangladesh Meteorological Department, Bangladesh Red Crescent Society (BDRCS), International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC), and American Red Cross. The program was implemented in 1972 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and accessibility.

Description

The Cyclone Preparedness Programme (CPP) Modernization is an initiative in Bangladesh aimed at reducing cyclone mortality and losses through a community-volunteer model. The program, a joint effort between the Government of Bangladesh and the Bangladesh Red Crescent Society, focuses on early warning, evacuation, sheltering, first aid, search and rescue, and post-disaster relief and recovery. The modernization phase, starting in 2018, includes gender parity among volunteers, risk communication, and evacuation support for displaced Myanmar nationals in Cox’s Bazar camps.

Key Highlights

The CPP Modernization features a community-led volunteer model with gender parity, inclusive coverage of displaced populations, and joint governance by national and Red Crescent entities. It employs multi-channel warning systems and has a recognized life-saving record, having received the Smith Tumsaroch Fund Award for its effectiveness. The program aligns with national disaster policies and emphasizes training residents in risk communication.

Results

The CPP aims to provide warnings to approximately 11 million coastal residents, significantly reducing cyclone-related deaths over five decades.
