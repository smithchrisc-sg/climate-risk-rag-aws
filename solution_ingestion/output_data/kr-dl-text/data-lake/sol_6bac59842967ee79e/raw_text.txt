Additional 1 Trillion Yuan Central Government Bonds for Disaster Relief and Flood Prevention (2023–2024)

National People’s Congress Standing Committee (NPCSC), 
Ministry of Finance (MOF), 
National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC), 
Provincial and local governments

Introduction

This solution, Additional 1 Trillion Yuan Central Government Bonds for Disaster Relief and Flood Prevention (2023–2024) addresses natural catastrophe in People’s Republic of China between National People’s Congress Standing Committee (NPCSC), Ministry of Finance (MOF), National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC), and Provincial and local governments. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The 'Additional 1 Trillion Yuan Central Government Bonds for Disaster Relief and Flood Prevention (2023–2024)' is a fiscal initiative by China aimed at rebuilding disaster-hit areas and enhancing the country's disaster-prevention, flood-control, and emergency management systems. The initiative involves the issuance of special treasury bonds to finance approximately 15,000 public projects across eight priority areas, including major river flood-control works, reservoir upgrades, urban drainage, and irrigation rehabilitation. The program is designed to address critical resilience gaps revealed by the 2023 floods and support domestic demand amid constrained local government finances.

Key Highlights

The initiative features rapid central financing to bypass local fiscal constraints, a dual mandate of post-disaster reconstruction and long-term resilience, and a strong focus on river-basin systems. It includes explicit urban drainage upgrades and integration with high-standard farmland and irrigation rehabilitation. The program's fast allocation cadence resulted in full allocation to approximately 15,000 projects by March 2024.

Results

Funding was fully allocated to approximately 15,000 public projects, with RMB 500 billion deployed in Q4 2023 and another RMB 500 billion in early 2024. The initiative increased the 2023 national fiscal deficit ratio to about 3.8%. In the Beijing–Tianjin–Hebei region alone, 142 counties and 5.53 million people were affected by 2023 flooding, with direct economic losses of about RMB 166 billion.
