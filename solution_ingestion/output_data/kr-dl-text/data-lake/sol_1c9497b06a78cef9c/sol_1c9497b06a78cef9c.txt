Description

The Western Australia (WA) initiative combines the AWARE program and the Disaster Ready Fund (DRF) to enhance disaster risk reduction. AWARE is a state-run program providing competitive grants to local governments to build capacity and preparedness. The DRF, a federal initiative, invests up to $1 billion over five years to fund resilience and risk reduction projects, with a focus on systemic risk mitigation and infrastructure. DFES coordinates these efforts, aligning them with national priorities to address natural hazards such as bushfires, cyclones, and floods.

Key Highlights

Complementary portfolio design: AWARE focuses on local capability and preparedness, while DRF funds capital-intensive risk reduction projects., 
Significant national investment: DRF commits up to $1 billion over five years, expanding resilience funding., 
Equity-oriented co-funding settings: Tiered waivers for First Nations organizations and remote councils to improve access., 
Clear access and transparency: DFES maintains a centralized information hub for resilience and recovery grants.
