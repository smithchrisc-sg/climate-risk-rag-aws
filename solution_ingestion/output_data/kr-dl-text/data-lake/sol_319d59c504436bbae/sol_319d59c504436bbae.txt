Introduction

This solution, Adaptation Fund Proposal for Papua New Guinea (March 2025) addresses natural catastrophe in Papua New Guinea between Government of Papua New Guinea, Adaptation Fund Board (AFB), United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), and Green Climate Fund (GCF). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Adaptation Fund Proposal for Papua New Guinea (March 2025) is a strategic initiative aimed at reducing climate risks across high-priority sectors such as flooding, drought-linked food insecurity, water/sanitation, health, and landslides. It seeks to strengthen institutions, mainstream climate risk into development planning, and enable medium- to long-term investment programming consistent with PNG’s adaptation pathways. The proposal is part of PNG's broader climate adaptation planning and finance mobilization efforts, building on previous UNDP and GCF-supported readiness work.

Key Highlights

The initiative is anchored in national policy and legal frameworks such as PNG Vision 2050 and the Climate Change (Management) Act of 2015. It builds on NAP readiness and planning supported by UNDP and GCF, focusing on strengthening multi-sectoral coordination and integrating climate risk into planning. The proposal targets nine adaptation priorities identified in PNG’s INDC/NDC, including coastal flooding, drought, and vector-borne diseases, with a combination of infrastructure, ecosystem-based measures, and institutional capacities.
