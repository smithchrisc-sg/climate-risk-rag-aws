Introduction

This solution, The Enabling Environment for Disaster Risk Financing in Cambodia: Country Diagnostics Assessment addresses natural catastrophe in Cambodia between Ministry of Economy and Finance, Disaster management authorities, Financial regulators, Statistical bodies, and Asian Development Bank (ADB). The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Enabling Environment for Disaster Risk Financing in Cambodia: Country Diagnostics Assessment is a technical publication by the Asian Development Bank (ADB) that provides a comprehensive diagnostic of Cambodia’s policy, regulatory, institutional, market, and data prerequisites for establishing and scaling disaster risk financing solutions. It aims to map the strengths and gaps of the enabling environment to help the Royal Government of Cambodia and development partners sequence reforms and investments that pre-arrange finance for disasters and reduce fiscal volatility from shocks.

Key Highlights

Country-focused, system-wide view covering laws, institutions, markets, and data., 
Synergy with parallel diagnostics, such as the Kyrgyz Republic, allowing cross-country benchmarking., 
Embedded within ADB’s DRM 2024–2030 drive, aligning with ADB’s renewed emphasis on disaster resilience., 
Clarity of categorization under climate change and environment, supporting integrated climate–disaster policy dialogue.

Results

Publication of a comprehensive diagnostic mapping Cambodia’s enabling environment for DRF., 
Inclusion in ADB’s 2025 publications roster under the climate change and environment category., 
Expected structured policy dialogue with Cambodian authorities to agree on an action roadmap., 
Pipeline development for technical assistance and/or financing aligned with the DRM Action Plan 2024–2030.
