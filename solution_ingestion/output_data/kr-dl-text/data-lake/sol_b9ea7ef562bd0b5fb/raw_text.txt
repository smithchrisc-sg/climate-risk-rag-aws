Introduction

This solution, From Pandemic to Greater Resilience: Enhancing Disaster Risk Financing in the People’s Republic of China addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in People’s Republic of China (PRC) between Ministry of Finance (MOF), Relevant line ministries, Provincial governments, Asian Development Bank (ADB), Domestic insurers, and International reinsurers. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

The initiative, proposed by the Asian Development Bank, aims to enhance disaster risk financing in the People’s Republic of China through five market-based parametric insurance pilot schemes. These pilots are designed to provide rapid post-disaster liquidity to public authorities using pre-agreed, objective hazard triggers, transferring a portion of the risk to domestic and international insurance and reinsurance markets. The proposal is part of ADB’s broader agenda to strengthen climate and disaster resilience and public financial management across Asia and the Pacific.

Key Highlights

The initiative proposes five pilot schemes tailored to PRC's public finance needs, emphasizing market-based parametric insurance with objective triggers for rapid liquidity. It critiques the limitations of current fiscal reserves and indemnity insurance, advocating for a blend of instruments. The proposal aligns with agricultural insurance modernization and potential public-private partnership structures, focusing on rapid, rules-based liquidity. It also integrates sectoral risk systems, particularly in agriculture, using technology and public-private pool structures to reduce basis risk and improve yield estimation.

Results

As of the latest information, the initiative remains a proposal and design framework with no formal rollout or results reported. Expected outcomes include faster liquidity, reduced budget volatility, and stronger fiscal resilience.
