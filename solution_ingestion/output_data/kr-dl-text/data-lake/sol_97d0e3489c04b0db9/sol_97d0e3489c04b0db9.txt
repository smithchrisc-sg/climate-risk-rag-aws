Description

HydroSOS‑BaNe is a regional initiative aimed at operationalizing a hydrological status-and-outlook system to provide actionable early warnings and water management intelligence for Bangladesh and Nepal. The project focuses on improving hydrometeorological services and decision support for floods, droughts, and broader water-related hazards, aligning technical hydrology with real-world risk management and planning needs.

Key Highlights

HydroSOS‑BaNe integrates observations, models, forecasts, and service delivery to bridge the gap between hydrological observing systems and real-time decisions in water management. It includes continuous monitoring of hydrological variables, generation of standardized status products, and creation of short-range to seasonal outlooks. The project emphasizes tailored dissemination to decision-makers and capacity development for sustainable operations.
