Introduction

This solution, Seoul Metropolitan Government 2025 Summer Disaster Management Plan addresses natural catastrophe in South Korea between Seoul Metropolitan Government, Ministry of the Interior and Safety (MoIS), and APEC Senior Disaster Management Officials’ Forum. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Seoul Metropolitan Government 2025 Summer Disaster Management Plan is an integrated, multi-hazard program designed to address heat waves, flooding, strong winds, and related public health risks in Seoul, South Korea. The plan operates from early May through mid-October 2025 and includes city-level early warning systems, engineered controls on flood-prone corridors, and widespread heat-health interventions for vulnerable groups. It also features pilot deployments of new flood alert technologies in select neighborhoods. The plan is part of a broader national effort to enhance seasonal readiness and resilience against climate-amplified extremes.

Key Highlights

The plan includes a three-level heat alert system, cooling and microclimate interventions such as cooling road zones and misting devices, and social protection measures like cooling shelters for vulnerable populations. It also involves proactive traffic and infrastructure controls during heavy rainfall, pilot deployments of flood alert technologies in basement and low-lying areas, and the installation of utility hole safety guards to prevent accidents during floods. The plan is aligned with national and international disaster management efforts, emphasizing anticipatory risk management and whole-of-society resilience.
