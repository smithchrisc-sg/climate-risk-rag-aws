Introduction

This solution, Black Kite Re Limited – Series 2025‑1 Class A Principal At‑Risk Variable Rate Notes addresses natural catastrophe in China between Hong Kong Insurance Authority, Peak Reinsurance Company, Black Kite Re Limited, GC Securities, Mercer Investments (HK) Limited, AIR Worldwide, and US Geological Survey. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

Black Kite Re Limited (Series 2025‑1) is a catastrophe bond issuance by Peak Reinsurance Company, utilizing a Hong Kong-domiciled special purpose insurer, Black Kite Re Limited. The bond provides multi-peril, multi-territory retrocessional reinsurance to Peak Re over a three-year term, with a focus on parametric earthquake protection for Mainland China. This initiative aims to mobilize capital markets to address Asia's protection gap, particularly in seismic risk areas.

Key Highlights

The bond marks the first reuse of a Hong Kong SPI for a 144A issuance, expanding coverage beyond Japan to include China and India earthquakes. It combines industry loss and parametric triggers, enhancing transparency and payout speed. The transaction is strategically significant for strengthening Hong Kong's ILS hub and increasing investor familiarity with Asian seismic risk.

Results

The bond was fully placed at the targeted USD 50 million, priced at an 8% spread. It provides three-year parametric earthquake protection for China, contributing to the credibility of Hong Kong's ILS hub. The bond's risk metrics include a 5.5% attachment probability and a 3.78% expected loss.
