Introduction

This solution, Cambodia Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Management Project 2 (CSADRMP2) addresses natural catastrophe in Cambodia between Government of the Kingdom of Cambodia, Ministry of Rural Development (MRD), World Bank, International Development Association (IDA), Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR), Japan–World Bank Program for Mainstreaming Disaster Risk Management (DRM), World Meteorological Organization (WMO), UNDRR, and CREWS Initiative. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Cambodia Southeast Asia Disaster Risk Management Project 2 (CSADRMP2) is a World Bank-financed initiative aimed at enhancing the climate and disaster resilience of Cambodia's rural transport network and improving national emergency preparedness. The project focuses on institutional upgrades, resilient rural road rehabilitation, and includes a contingency financing window for rapid response to crises or emergencies. It targets recurrent flood impacts, particularly in the Tonle Sap region, and aims to reduce disaster-related disruptions while enhancing the government's capacity to prepare for and respond to emergencies.

Key Highlights

The project features a hybrid green-gray design, integrating nature-based solutions like engineered wetlands and vegetated embankments with traditional infrastructure to enhance flood resilience. It emphasizes the Tonle Sap region's resilience, integrates road safety, and includes a Contingent Emergency Response Component (CERC) for rapid crisis response. The project aligns with regional early warning system strengthening initiatives.

Results

The project has been effective since September 8, 2022, with ongoing implementation. Audited financial statements have been issued for the period from September 8, 2022, to December 31, 2023. Procurement plans and competitive opportunities have been publicly disclosed, indicating active pipeline development and contracting.
