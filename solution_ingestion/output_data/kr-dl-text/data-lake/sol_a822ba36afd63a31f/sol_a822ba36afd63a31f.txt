Introduction

This solution, Climate and Disaster Resilience Enhancement Program (Subprogram 1) (CDREP-S1) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Pakistan between Government of Pakistan, Asian Development Bank (ADB), Domestic and international private investors, Insurance sector, and Agriculture stakeholders. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Climate and Disaster Resilience Enhancement Program (CDREP), Subprogram 1, is a policy-based lending initiative by the Asian Development Bank aimed at enhancing Pakistan's institutional, financial, and planning frameworks for climate and disaster resilience. It is the first of two subprograms, focusing on foundational policy and institutional reforms to catalyze investment and financing instruments, with a future Contingent Disaster Financing option to provide rapid budget support post-disaster.

Key Highlights

The program is notable for being the first to introduce a Contingent Disaster Financing (CDF) option within a policy-based lending framework in Central and West Asia. It aims to strengthen institutional capacity, increase investment in disaster risk reduction, and enhance disaster risk financing through a risk-layered approach. The program supports the issuance of domestic green sukuk and establishes a solidarity fund for risk transfer solutions, such as agriculture insurance.
