Introduction

This solution, National Disaster Risk Management Fund (NDRMF) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Pakistan between Ministry of Climate Change, Ministry of Planning, Development & Special Initiatives, Economic Affairs Division, Ministry of Finance, Pakistan Meteorological Department, Pakistan Council of Research in Water Resources (PCRWR), SUPARCO, Asian Development Bank (ADB), The World Bank, UNDP Pakistan, GIZ, British High Commission in Pakistan, International Water Management Institute (IWMI), Sustainable Development Policy Institute (SDPI), WWF-Pakistan, and Pakistan Red Crescent Society (PRCS). The program was implemented in 2016 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The National Disaster Risk Management Fund (NDRMF) is a government-owned, not-for-profit company in Pakistan, established to finance and catalyze pre-disaster risk reduction, preparedness, and risk financing solutions. It operates by providing up to 70% grant co-financing for structural and non-structural measures, focusing on ex-ante interventions such as planning, preparedness, early warning, and mitigation, while excluding post-disaster response activities.

Key Highlights

NDRMF is unique as a government-owned, Section 42, not-for-profit fund that operates with a corporate structure for agility while maintaining public accountability. It provides up to 70% co-financing grants to public and non-public entities, emphasizing ex-ante investments in planning, preparedness, mitigation, and early warning. It developed Pakistan's first national, probabilistic, multi-hazard catastrophe model with SUPARCO, and approved the first national Disaster Risk Financing Strategy in 2024, with a 10-year implementation roadmap.
