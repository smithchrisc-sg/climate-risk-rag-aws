Introduction

This solution, Future Drought Fund (FDF) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between Australian Government's Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry (DAFF), Regional Investment Corporation, State/Territory governments, Foundation for Rural & Regional Renewal (FRRR), Australian Rural Leadership Foundation (ARLF), and Universities and partners delivering Drought Resilience Adoption and Innovation Hubs. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and accessibility.

Description

The Future Drought Fund (FDF) is a national initiative by the Australian Government designed to provide secure, continuous funding for drought resilience initiatives across Australia. With an endowment of $5 billion, the FDF distributes $100 million annually to help farms and communities prepare for and adapt to future droughts, addressing economic, social, and environmental dimensions of risk. The fund is administered by the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry (DAFF) and aligns with the National Drought Agreement.

Key Highlights

The FDF features a perpetual $100 million annual funding flow, supported by a sovereign wealth investment approach. It emphasizes integrated delivery through Innovation Hubs, strong alignment with the National Drought Agreement, and a focus on building social capital. The fund also includes interoperable climate services and self-assessment tools, with oversight provided by an independent Productivity Commission review cycle.

Results

Since 2020, the FDF has allocated $100 million annually, with total allocations to 2023–24 amounting to approximately $353.1 million. This includes foundational and extension agreements totaling over $75.97 million, a community impact stream of $29.6 million over three years, and an additional $94.5 million for the continuation of the Drought Resilience Funding Plan.
