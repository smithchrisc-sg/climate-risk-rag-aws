Introduction

This solution, Sindh Resilience Project (SRP) addresses natural catastrophe in Pakistan between Sindh Irrigation Department (SID), Provincial Disaster Management Authority (PDMA) Sindh, and World Bank. The program was implemented in 2016 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

The Sindh Resilience Project (SRP) is an initiative by the Government of Sindh, implemented by the Sindh Irrigation Department and the Provincial Disaster Management Authority, with financing from the World Bank. It aims to enhance the institutional capacity to manage riverine flood risks in Sindh, particularly along the Indus River. The project employs a Decision Support System (DSS) that integrates satellite imagery, numerical modeling, and real-time hydrometric data to aid in water resources planning and operational river monitoring before and during flood seasons.

Key Highlights

End-to-end Decision Support System (DSS) that supports both pre-season water planning and real-time flood operations., 
Integration of satellite imagery and numerical models with live gauge data for evidence-based decision-making., 
Joint implementation by SID and PDMA, promoting a whole-of-government approach to flood risk management., 
Early adoption of Environmental and Social Management Framework (ESMF) and Resettlement Policy Framework (RPF) for standardized environmental and social screening., 
Scalable financing with a 2020 Additional Financing proposal indicating strategic relevance and scalability.

Results

The establishment and operationalization of a specialized DSS for strategic water resources planning and real-time Indus river monitoring, built on satellite imagery, numerical modeling, and live hydrometric data ingestion.
