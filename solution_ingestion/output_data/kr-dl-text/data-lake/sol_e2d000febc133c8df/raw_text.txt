Description

The Hopua te Nihotetea Detention Dam is a flood management asset located in the Northland region of New Zealand. It is designed to temporarily store floodwaters to reduce peak flows downstream, thereby mitigating the risk of river flooding. The dam is integrated with the Northland Regional Council's Environmental Data Hub, which includes a public-facing webcam to enhance situational awareness and emergency management.

Key Highlights

The dam features a live public-facing webcam integrated with the NRC’s Environmental Data Hub, providing real-time situational awareness. It is part of the NRC's River Flood Management Programme and reflects local cultural context through its te reo Māori name. The dam is embedded in a broader system of flood risk management and community awareness tools.

Results

Quantified outcomes are not published, but the dam is expected to reduce downstream flood peaks and enhance response readiness through real-time visibility. It is part of NRC's operational hydrometric network, contributing to risk communication and community confidence.
