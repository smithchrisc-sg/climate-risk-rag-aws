Introduction

This solution, ACCC Insurance Monitoring Program addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC), Australian Government, and General Insurers. The program was implemented in Early 2020s and focuses on regulation and raising awareness.

Description

The ACCC Insurance Monitoring Program is an initiative by the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission to oversee and report on the insurance market outcomes following the establishment of a government-backed cyclone and cyclone-related flood damage reinsurance pool. The program aims to ensure that the savings from the reinsurance pool are passed on to policyholders, particularly in cyclone-exposed regions, by monitoring costs, pricing, and the pass-through of reinsurance savings.

Key Highlights

The program focuses on cyclone and cyclone-related flood risks, providing transparent and periodic public reporting. It emphasizes the assessment of pass-through savings to consumers and involves multi-year tracking of market outcomes. The ACCC's independence enhances the credibility of the program, which is aligned with a specific policy intervention to improve insurance affordability and availability in high-risk areas.

Results

As of July 2025, the ACCC has released four monitoring reports, demonstrating sustained oversight and deepening data-driven analysis of premiums and pass-through outcomes following the reinsurance pool’s introduction.
