Description

The Building (Amendment) Act 2016 is a legislative measure passed by the National Parliament of Papua New Guinea to modernize and streamline building regulations. It serves as a legislative and institutional pipeline to incorporate building code advances into PNG's legal framework, addressing public safety, urban development, and energy performance considerations. The Act was passed and certified in 2016, with the Office of Legislative Counsel facilitating the consolidation and public access to updated laws through the Pacific Islands Legal Information Institute.

Key Highlights

The Act was a government-sponsored bill proposed by Hon. James Marape, passed swiftly in a single day of readings, and integrated with the OLC/PacLII consolidation for public visibility. It provides a transparent legislative archive and supports consistent recordkeeping and public oversight.

Results

The enactment and certification of the Building (Amendment) Act 2016, inclusion in the official Bills & Legislation archive, and durable online visibility in PNG’s legislative ecosystem. Quantitative impact metrics such as enforcement outcomes or compliance rates are not reported.
