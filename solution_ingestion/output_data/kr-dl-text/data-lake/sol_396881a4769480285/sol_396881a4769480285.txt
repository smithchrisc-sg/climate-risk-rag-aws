Introduction

This solution, Philippines Seismic Risk Reduction and Resilience Project (PSRRRP) addresses natural catastrophe in Republic of the Philippines between Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH), DPWH Unified Project Management Office – Buildings & Special Projects Management Cluster, DPWH Bureau of Equipment, and World Bank (International Bank for Reconstruction and Development). The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Philippines Seismic Risk Reduction and Resilience Project (PSRRRP) is a government-led initiative financed by the World Bank, aimed at reducing earthquake risk and enhancing post-disaster response capabilities in the Philippines. The project focuses on restoring mobility after major seismic events, integrating into the Department of Public Works and Highways' emergency preparedness programs, and aligning with national contingency plans for earthquakes.

Key Highlights

The project is directly integrated with 'The Big One' Earthquake Preparedness Program, ensuring that its assets are part of established mobilization plans. It features a strong commitment to operations and maintenance financing, with PHP 461.16 million allocated over five years. The project aligns with the Metro Manila Earthquake Contingency Plan and MMEIRS, enhancing strategic coherence. It also adheres to World Bank's Environmental and Social Framework, ensuring systematic risk management.

Results

Approximately 120 new pieces of emergency response equipment added to DPWH’s inventory., 
PHP 461.16 million allocated for a five-year operations and maintenance budget., 
Integration of PSRRRP equipment into DPWH's emergency preparedness programs.
