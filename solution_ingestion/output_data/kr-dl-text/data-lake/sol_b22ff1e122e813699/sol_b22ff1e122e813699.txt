Introduction

This solution, Ready to Rebuild: Disaster Rehabilitation and Recovery Program (R2R Program) addresses natural catastrophe in Philippines between Government of the Philippines, National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC), Office of Civil Defense (OCD), Department of Science and Technology (DOST), Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG), Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM) agencies (BPDA, MILG), World Bank, Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR), and Japan-World Bank Program for Mainstreaming DRM. The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Ready to Rebuild (R2R) Program is a collaborative initiative between the World Bank and the Government of the Philippines aimed at enhancing disaster rehabilitation and recovery. It provides national and local officials with a standardized 'playbook' for pre- and post-disaster recovery planning, including tools, templates, and operational guidance. The program is integrated with the GeoRiskPH multi-hazard database and aligns with catastrophe financing strategies to ensure effective and timely recovery efforts.

Key Highlights

The R2R Program features a pre-disaster recovery planning 'playbook' that includes standardized templates and operational guidance. It integrates with the GeoRiskPH multi-hazard database for science-based planning and aligns with the CAT-DDO financing facility to ensure readiness for funding recovery efforts. The program has a nationwide training reach, equipping local governments with the necessary skills and knowledge for effective disaster recovery.

Results

The program has trained 197 provinces and cities across all 17 regions, equipping 924 governors, mayors, and technical staff. It has accelerated the development of recovery plans post-Typhoon Rai and is complemented by a $200 million CAT-DDO 4 standby facility.
