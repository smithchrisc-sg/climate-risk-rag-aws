The program was implemented in 2019 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Climate Relocation of Communities (CROC) Program and Trust Fund (CROCTF) in Fiji is the world's first national initiative to integrate a comprehensive government relocation framework with a dedicated financing mechanism. Established in 2019, it aims to address the displacement of communities due to climate change impacts such as sea-level rise, erosion, and saltwater intrusion. The program is designed to safeguard lives, livelihoods, and cultural continuity by facilitating community relocations through a structured policy and financial support system.

Key Highlights

The CROC initiative is unique in its integration of policy and finance, providing a structured approach to planned relocations. It is anchored by Fiji’s Planned Relocation Guidelines and Standard Operating Procedures, ensuring community engagement and social inclusion. The Trust Fund is financed by domestic climate-levy revenues and seeks international support, making it a pioneering model for climate adaptation financing.

Results

The program is projected to provide approximately five million dollars per year through domestic financing, with additional international support being sought. It has created a pipeline of communities seeking relocation assistance, establishing planned relocation as a core adaptation measure.
