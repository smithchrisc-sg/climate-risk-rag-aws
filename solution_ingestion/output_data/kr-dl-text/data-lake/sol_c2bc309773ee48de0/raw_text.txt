Introduction

This solution, Developing Climate Disaster Risk Financing Framework and Parametric Insurance in Fiji (Pilot) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Fiji between Government of Fiji entities, Central Bank of Fiji, UN Capital Development Fund (UNCDF), UN Development Programme (UNDP), UN University – Institute for Environment and Human Security (UNU-EHS), India-UN Development Partnership Fund, UN Office for South-South Cooperation (UNOSSC), Local insurers, Weather Risk Agencies, Mobile Network Operators, and Cane Farmers’ Cooperative Savings and Loans Association (CCSLA). The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration, accessibility, and raising awareness.

Description

The initiative aims to enhance financial preparedness and resilience to climate-related shocks in Fiji, focusing on low-income households, smallholder farmers, fishers, and micro/small enterprises. It is part of the Pacific Insurance and Climate Adaptation Programme (PICAP) and involves developing a national Climate Disaster Risk Financing framework and parametric micro-insurance products to provide rapid post-disaster liquidity.

Key Highlights

First parametric micro-insurance in the Pacific; strong gender equality and social inclusion (GESI) mainstreaming; multi-donor South-South and traditional funding blend; aggregator model for last-mile reach; fast payouts within 2-3 days.

Results

Over 1,300 households covered regionally by May 2022; evidence-informed product pipeline; strengthened partnerships with local aggregators; advancing national CDRFI strategies and market infrastructure in Fiji.
