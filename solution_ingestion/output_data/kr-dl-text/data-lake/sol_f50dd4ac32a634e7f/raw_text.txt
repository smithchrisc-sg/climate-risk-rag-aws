Introduction

This solution, Government Flood Risk Reduction and Mitigation Initiatives in Brunei Darussalam (2020–2025) addresses natural catastrophe in Brunei Darussalam between Ministry of Development, Public Works Department, and Legislative Council. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Government Flood Risk Reduction and Mitigation Initiatives in Brunei Darussalam (2020–2025) aim to protect communities and critical assets from frequent and intense monsoon-driven rainfall. The program includes structural flood mitigation measures such as retention ponds, river dredging and widening, drainage upgrades, water control gates, and pumping systems. These initiatives are part of Brunei's National Development Plans and are led by the Ministry of Development and the Public Works Department, with oversight from the Legislative Council.

Key Highlights

The initiative has constructed 21 retention ponds at a cost of BND 80 million, significantly reducing flood-prone areas from 332 km² to 288 km². It addresses both inland flooding and coastal erosion along 57 km of coastline. The program includes comprehensive measures such as dredging and widening of major rivers, installation of water control gates, and enhancement of urban drainage systems.

Results

The initiative has reduced flood-prone areas by 44 km² and completed structural works across multiple rivers and urban sites. The construction of retention ponds has led to a significant reduction in flood occurrences.
