Introduction

Description

The report investigates the existence of a program titled 'Government Risk Financing for Drought in Brunei (2020–2025)'. However, there is no verifiable evidence that such an initiative was designed, launched, or implemented by Brunei Darussalam during this period. The report suggests that Brunei's government could potentially address climate and hydrometeorological shocks through general fiscal measures rather than a standalone drought-focused risk financing program.

Key Highlights

The report highlights the absence of any official documentation or evidence supporting the existence of a dedicated drought risk financing program in Brunei for the period 2020–2025. It notes Brunei's strong fiscal capacity and centralized governance, which could theoretically allow for self-financing of emergency responses without a specific program.
