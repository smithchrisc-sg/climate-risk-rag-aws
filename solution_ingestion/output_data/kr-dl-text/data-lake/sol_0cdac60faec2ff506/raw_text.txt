Introduction

This solution, Fiji Growth and Resilience First Development Policy Financing with a Catastrophe Deferred Drawdown Option (Cat-DDO) addresses natural catastrophe in Republic of Fiji between Government of Fiji, Ministry of Finance, Strategic Planning, National Development and Statistics, and World Bank (IDA financing). The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Fiji Growth and Resilience First Development Policy Financing with a Catastrophe Deferred Drawdown Option (Cat-DDO) is a World Bank-supported initiative aimed at enhancing Fiji's fiscal resilience, private sector development, and climate and disaster resilience. It combines immediate budget support with a pre-arranged contingent credit line for disasters, providing rapid liquidity post-disaster. The operation is aligned with Fiji's reform calendar and cyclone seasons, running through mid-2027.

Key Highlights

The initiative features a blended approach of US$95 million in development policy budget support and a US$30 million Cat-DDO, which can be rapidly drawn following a qualifying disaster. It builds on a previous 2021 Cat-DDO, demonstrating continuity and scaling up in ambition and financing. The program focuses on fiscal resilience, private sector enabling measures, and divisional disaster planning, with co-benefits in public health and regulatory simplification.

Results

The operation was approved in mid-2024, and measurable outcomes are at an early stage. Quantifiable inputs include US$125 million total financing, with expected outcomes such as increased government revenue, streamlined business operations, and improved disaster preparedness. The Cat-DDO provides immediate fiscal liquidity post-disaster.
