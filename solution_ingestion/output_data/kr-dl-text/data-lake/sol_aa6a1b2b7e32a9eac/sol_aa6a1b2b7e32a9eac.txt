Philippines between Government of the Republic of Korea, Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT), Government of the Philippines, and United Nations World Food Programme (WFP). The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

In November 2024, the Republic of Korea provided PHP 30 million in humanitarian financing through the United Nations World Food Programme to support households affected by severe tropical storm Kristine and subsequent typhoons in the Philippines. This initiative aimed to provide cash-based assistance, logistics support, and emergency telecommunications to affected areas, aligning with the Government of the Philippines' response efforts.

Key Highlights

The initiative was characterized by its rapid response, with aid delivered within days of the storms. It utilized the Philippines' 4Ps social assistance program for precise targeting of vulnerable households, particularly those with children under five. The program also integrated logistics support and emergency telecommunications to ensure effective delivery and coordination of aid. The initiative built on South Korea's history of humanitarian support to the Philippines, demonstrating continuity and institutional learning.

Results

The initiative provided cash assistance to 14,500 households in Albay and Camarines Sur, with each household receiving PHP 3,300 per month for two months. Additionally, the DSWD distributed 152,900 family food packs to over 764,500 people in the Bicol region.
