Introduction

This solution, Pakistan Hydromet and Climate Services Project (PHCSP/PHSCP) addresses natural catastrophe in Pakistan between Ministry of Climate Change & Environmental Coordination, National Disaster Risk Management Fund, Pakistan Meteorological Department, National Disaster Management Authority, International Development Association (IDA), and World Bank. The program was implemented in 2018 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Pakistan Hydromet and Climate Services Project (PHCSP) was a World Bank-financed initiative aimed at modernizing hydrometeorological services and strengthening disaster risk management in Pakistan. Initially focused on upgrading weather observation and forecasting systems, the project underwent significant restructuring to emphasize nature-based solutions and emergency response, aligning with national climate resilience goals.

Key Highlights

The project initially aimed to modernize Pakistan's hydrometeorological services to align with World Meteorological Organization standards, enhancing data quality and forecasting capabilities. It later pivoted towards nature-based solutions and crisis response, aligning with Pakistan's Nationally Determined Contributions and focusing on community resilience through ecosystem restoration and sustainable management. The project also supported legal and policy strengthening for disaster risk management.

Results

The project achieved completion after an 18-month extension, with significant restructuring leading to the cancellation of several hydromet modernization investments. The pivot towards nature-based solutions was reportedly supported by an estimated USD 35 million envelope.
