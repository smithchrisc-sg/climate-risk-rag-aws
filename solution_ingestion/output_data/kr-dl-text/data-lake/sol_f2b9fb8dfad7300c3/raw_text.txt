Introduction

This solution, Resilient Homes Program (RHP), 
Resilient Lands Program (RLP) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between NSW Reconstruction Authority, NSW Government, Australian Government, Local governments in target regions, Legal Aid NSW, Landcom, and Southern Cross University. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration, accessibility, and raising awareness.

Description

The Resilient Homes Program (RHP) and Resilient Lands Program (RLP) in New South Wales, Australia, are comprehensive initiatives aimed at addressing flood risk and enhancing housing resilience. Established in response to severe flooding in 2022, these programs offer voluntary buybacks, retrofits, and relocations for affected homeowners, while also unlocking flood-free land for safer resettlement. Led by the NSW Reconstruction Authority, with co-funding from the NSW and Australian Governments, the programs prioritize high-risk areas and integrate with broader regional recovery efforts.

Key Highlights

The RHP and RLP feature an integrated demand- and supply-side approach, combining voluntary buybacks and home upgrades with the creation of flood-free housing supply. The programs are region-specific, targeting the highest-risk areas with voluntary participation to build community trust. They offer flexibility in co-contributions and provide grants for design and approvals to expedite projects. Partnerships with Landcom, universities, and local councils facilitate the rapid aggregation of flood-free land, with significant milestones including the acquisition of 72 hectares for 400+ lots and a release for up to 1,000 homes in Grafton. The programs are embedded in broader regional resilience packages, addressing interconnected infrastructure and community needs.

Results

The programs have secured at least $920 million in funding for the RHP and $100 million for the RLP. Specific delivery statistics, such as the number of buybacks or retrofits completed, are not detailed, but the funding scale and land acquisition milestones indicate significant progress.
