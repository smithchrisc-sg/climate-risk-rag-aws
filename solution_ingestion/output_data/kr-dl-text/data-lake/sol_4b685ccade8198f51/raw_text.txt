Description

The Commission on Audit (COA) Nationwide Performance Audit in the Philippines is an initiative aimed at enhancing the effectiveness of public financial management through a results-based integrated audit methodology. This methodology focuses on the outputs and outcomes of public expenditures, integrating financial, compliance, and performance audits. The initiative is part of a larger program funded by the World Bank under the INTOSAI-Donor Cooperation, with the goal of strengthening COA's capacity and effectiveness. The project also includes a Citizen Participatory Audit (CPA) phase, engaging citizens in audit processes to enhance transparency and accountability.

Key Highlights

Results-based integrated audit methodology that integrates financial, compliance, and performance audits., 
Risk-based planning to prioritize audit focus and improve efficiency., 
Alignment with the Organizational Performance Indicators Framework (OPIF) to link public spending to measurable outcomes., 
Citizen Participatory Audit (CPA) to involve citizens in audit processes, enhancing transparency and oversight.
