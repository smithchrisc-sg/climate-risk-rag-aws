Resilient Homes and Buildings Action Plan

Toka Tū Ake EQC (Natural Hazards Commission), 
Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE)

Introduction

This solution, Resilient Homes and Buildings Action Plan addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Aotearoa New Zealand between Toka Tū Ake EQC (Natural Hazards Commission), Ministry of Business, and Innovation and Employment (MBIE). The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Resilient Homes and Buildings Action Plan is a national initiative led by Toka Tū Ake EQC, the Natural Hazards Commission of Aotearoa New Zealand, aimed at strengthening the country's housing and building stock against natural hazards. The plan, spanning from 2022 to 2027, focuses on raising standards for new builds, managing risks in existing buildings, and ensuring the built environment system enables resilience. It emphasizes strong building regulation and governance, sector partnerships, and integration with climate change adaptation considerations.

Key Highlights

The plan features a dual-plan strategy aligned with land use, emphasizing both new and existing building stock. It promotes 'low-damage' seismic design and regulatory governance as key levers. The partnership model with MBIE and sector professionals is central to its implementation, and it uniquely combines insurance-backed national commission leadership with practical regulatory and practice improvements.

Results

Strategic progress pathways and activities have been publicly identified, but specific quantitative outcomes have not been reported as of 2025.
