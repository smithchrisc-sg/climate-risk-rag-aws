Introduction

This solution, State of National Disaster for Drought in Gangneung (2025) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Republic of Korea (South Korea) between Office of the President, Central Disaster and Safety Countermeasures Headquarters (CDSCHQ), Ministry of the Interior and Safety, Ministry of Environment, Gangwon Provincial Government, Gangneung City Government, National Fire Agency, Gangwon Fire Headquarters, Republic of Korea Armed Forces, Korea Rural Community Corporation, and Korea Hydro & Nuclear Power Co. (KHNP). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

In 2025, South Korea declared a state of national disaster in Gangneung due to a severe flash drought, marking the first national disaster declaration for a natural hazard in the country. The initiative involved a comprehensive response to stabilize municipal water supplies, which included demand-side restrictions, emergency supply augmentation, and water quality management. The Central Disaster and Safety Countermeasures Headquarters coordinated the Level 2 response, mobilizing resources nationwide to address the crisis.

Key Highlights

The initiative was characterized by a multi-layered approach involving aggressive demand management, emergency supply augmentation through tanker deliveries and inter-municipal transfers, and preparations for an inter-basin emergency release from Doam Dam. A Water Quality Verification Committee was established to manage risks associated with alternative water sources. The initiative also prioritized vulnerable populations by distributing bottled water to schools and senior welfare facilities.

Results

The initiative successfully mobilized 112 vehicles, including fire and military tankers, delivering up to 5,071 metric tons of water per day. Approximately 20,000 tons of alternative water sources were secured, and 1.41 million bottles of water were stockpiled, with 283,000 distributed to vulnerable groups.
