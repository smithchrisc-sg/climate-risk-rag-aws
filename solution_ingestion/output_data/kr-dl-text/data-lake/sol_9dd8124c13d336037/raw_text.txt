Debris and Sediment Clearance and Cultural Asset Restoration (Budget 2024)

Department of Conservation (DOC), 
Ministry for Culture and Heritage

Introduction

This solution, Debris and Sediment Clearance and Cultural Asset Restoration (Budget 2024) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in New Zealand between Department of Conservation (DOC) and Ministry for Culture and Heritage. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The 'Debris and Sediment Clearance and Cultural Asset Restoration (Budget 2024)' initiative is a recovery-focused effort by the New Zealand Government, under the Department of Conservation, to address weather-related damage to public conservation lands and waters, and to protect historic and cultural heritage. It involves clearing debris and sediment, repairing damaged conservation assets, and safeguarding cultural heritage sites affected by the 2023 North Island weather events, including Cyclone Gabrielle.

Key Highlights

The initiative is part of New Zealand's Budget 2024 and includes a $9.1 million allocation for addressing damage to conservation sites and assets, and $13.2 million for protecting cultural heritage. It involves collaboration with iwi/hapū, local councils, community groups, and other partners. The initiative focuses on site assessments, debris clearance, asset repair, and heritage conservation, supported by DOC's statutory roles.
