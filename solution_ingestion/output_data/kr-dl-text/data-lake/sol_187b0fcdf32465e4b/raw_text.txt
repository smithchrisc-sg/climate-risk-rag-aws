Introduction

This solution, PCRIC Coral Reef Parametric Insurance Policy for Northern Lau, Fiji addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Fiji between Pacific Catastrophe Risk Insurance Company (PCRIC), Vatuvara Foundation (VVF), WTW, Reask, Insurance Holdings (Pacific) Pte Ltd., and BHP. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration, leveraging tech, and raising awareness.

Description

The PCRIC Coral Reef Parametric Insurance Policy is a pioneering initiative in Fiji designed to provide rapid financial support for coral reef restoration and community livelihoods following tropical cyclones. Administered by the Pacific Catastrophe Risk Insurance Company for the Vatuvara Foundation, this policy offers up to US$450,000 in coverage. It utilizes parametric triggers based on cyclone characteristics to ensure swift payouts, enabling timely reef recovery and community assistance in the Northern Lau group of islands.

Key Highlights

First coral reef insurance in Fiji, with a focus on rapid payout and community support. The policyholder is an NGO, Vatuvara Foundation, which allows for quick allocation of funds to ecological and social priorities. The initiative includes early-warning support and aligns with national and regional strategies, offering potential for geographic expansion. The policy's parametric nature ensures payouts are based on cyclone parameters, bypassing traditional loss adjustment processes.

Results

Following Tropical Cyclone Rae in 2025, a payout of approximately US$45,000 was initiated within 8 days, with funds reaching the Vatuvara Foundation within 14 days. This enabled immediate reef restoration and community support actions.
