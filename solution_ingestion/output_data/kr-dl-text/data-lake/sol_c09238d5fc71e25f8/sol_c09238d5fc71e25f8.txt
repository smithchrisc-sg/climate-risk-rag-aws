Introduction

This solution, Start Ready (Start Network’s disaster risk financing mechanism) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Bangladesh between Start Fund Bangladesh, Local humanitarian organizations in Bangladesh, Start Network, Swiss Re Foundation, Nirapad, FOREWARN Bangladesh, and Open Mapping Hub – Asia Pacific. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

Start Ready (Bangladesh) is a country-level deployment of Start Network’s disaster risk financing mechanism aimed at protecting vulnerable communities from floods and tropical cyclones. It utilizes pooled funds, probabilistic risk analysis, and reinsurance-backed rapid financing to enable anticipatory and early action, as well as immediate post-shock response. The initiative is supported by the Swiss Re Foundation and operates alongside Start Network’s broader mechanisms in Bangladesh.

Key Highlights

Start Ready employs an insurance-inspired disaster risk financing architecture that pre-arranges funds for rapid release based on risk analysis and pre-agreed parameters. It integrates with Start Fund Anticipation and Rapid Response windows and the national Start Fund Bangladesh platform. The initiative is characterized by its use of risk pooling, probabilistic risk modeling, and forecast-based triggers to support anticipatory action and immediate response. It also emphasizes locally led action and alignment with SDGs related to Zero Hunger, Climate Action, and Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure.

Results

Operational activations include a multi-mechanism response to Cyclone Remal in May 2024, which displaced an estimated 800,000 people. The initiative is 57.4% complete as of mid-2025, with substantial progress in developing country-specific risk models and financing architecture. However, specific quantifiable impact metrics tied to Start Ready allocations in Bangladesh are not yet published.
