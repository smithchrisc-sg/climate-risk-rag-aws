Introduction

This solution, NDRC Climate Resilience Infrastructure Initiative addresses natural catastrophe in People’s Republic of China between National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC), Ministry of Ecology and Environment (MEE), National Energy Administration (NEA), and National Bureau of Statistics (NBS). The program was implemented in 2007 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The NDRC Climate Resilience Infrastructure Initiative is not a standalone program but a policy-integrated approach embedded in China's national planning frameworks. It is driven by the National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) through the National Climate Change Programme (2007) and the Working Guidance for Carbon Dioxide Peaking and Carbon Neutrality (2021). This initiative integrates climate risk management and resilience into macroeconomic planning and infrastructure governance, focusing on systemic climate risks to infrastructure and economic systems through mitigation and adaptation strategies.

Key Highlights

The initiative is embedded in China's apex planning institution, integrating climate objectives into Five-Year Plans and major infrastructure strategies. It uses both administrative and market instruments, such as the national Emissions Trading System (ETS), to guide investment signals. The initiative also coordinates regional strategies and inter-ministerial policymaking, ensuring climate priorities are mainstreamed across development agendas.

Results

Institutional outputs include the launch of the national ETS in 2017 and the continued integration of climate into macro-plans. However, publicly available, quantified resilience outcomes specific to infrastructure are not reported.
