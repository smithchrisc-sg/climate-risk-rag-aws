Introduction

This solution, Addu City Land Reclamation and Shore Protection Project (ASRP) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Maldives between Ministry of National Planning, Housing and Infrastructure (MNPHI), Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), Maldives, Addu City Council, High Commission of India in Maldives, Export-Import Bank of India (Exim Bank of India), Afcons Infrastructure Ltd., and Various local and international environmental and engineering consultancies. Description

The Addu City Land Reclamation and Shore Protection Project (ASRP) is a comprehensive initiative aimed at addressing chronic shoreline erosion, sea-level rise, and storm surge risks in Addu City, Maldives. The project involves land reclamation and the construction of coastal defenses to mitigate erosion and flooding, while also expanding land for housing, public amenities, and economic activities. It is a collaborative effort involving the Government of Maldives, the Government of India, and private sector contractors.

Key Highlights

The project strategically targets multiple locations across Addu City to add substantial new land, relieving housing pressure and unlocking tourism and services. It employs marine dredging techniques with environmental safeguards to minimize ecological harm. The initiative is financed through a line of credit from the Government of India, with Afcons Infrastructure Ltd. serving as the EPC contractor.
