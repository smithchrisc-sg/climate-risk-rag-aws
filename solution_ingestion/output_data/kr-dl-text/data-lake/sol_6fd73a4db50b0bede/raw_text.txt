Introduction

This solution, Fiji Government VAT Exemption for Premiums on Climate and Disaster Risk Parametric Insurance Products addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Republic of Fiji between Government of Fiji, UN Capital Development Fund (UNCDF), UNDP, UNU-EHS, India-UN Development Partnership Fund, UNOSSC, Governments of New Zealand, India, Australia, Luxembourg’s Climate and Energy Fund, FijiCare, and Sun Insurance. The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on accessibility, collaboration, and raising awareness.

Description

Fiji's VAT exemption on premiums for climate and disaster risk parametric insurance is a fiscal policy aimed at making climate risk transfer more affordable for low-income and vulnerable households. This initiative is part of a broader effort to develop Climate and Disaster Risk Financing Instruments (CDRFI) and scale parametric microinsurance under the Pacific Insurance and Climate Adaptation Programme (PICAP). The policy includes subsidized coverage for social welfare recipients and is co-financed by the India-UN Development Partnership Fund. It aims to reduce price barriers and encourage insurers to innovate and expand inclusive parametric products in Fiji's high-risk market.

Key Highlights

First-in-Pacific parametric microinsurance, 
Landmark VAT exemption, 
Public funding for 2,000 welfare recipients, 
South-South cooperation leveraging India’s parametric expertise, 
Scaling to 5,000 households, 
UNCDF de-risking instruments to build market, 
Strong gender focus

Results

Initial pilots covered 500 then 1,000 beneficiaries before 2021/22 cyclone season, 
Coverage planned for minimum 5,000 households (~25,000 people) next cyclone season, 
2,000 social welfare recipients targeted for subsidized cover
