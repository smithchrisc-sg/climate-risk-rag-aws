Introduction

This solution, Systematic Observations Financing Facility (SOFF) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Bangladesh between World Meteorological Organization (WMO), United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP), Multilateral Development Banks (MDBs), and UN organizations. The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Systematic Observations Financing Facility (SOFF) is a United Nations Multi-Partner Trust Fund established to address the global gap in surface-based weather and climate observations, with a focus on Least Developed Countries (LDCs) and Small Island Developing States (SIDS). It aims to enhance early warning systems and climate monitoring by ensuring compliance with the Global Basic Observing Network (GBON) standards. SOFF provides long-term financing, peer advisory, and leverages complementary climate finance to support infrastructure, capacity building, and data sharing.

Key Highlights

SOFF is a foundational element of the UN Secretary-General’s Early Warnings for All (EW4All) initiative, aligning with GBON standards. It offers innovative long-term financing with a compliance focus and peer-to-peer advisory from advanced meteorological services. The initiative has a global approach, emphasizing international data exchange as a success metric and unlocking additional climate finance, with each SOFF dollar leveraging approximately five times more from partner funds.

Results

Globally, 101 countries have requested support, with 61 approved for Readiness and 18 for Investment. Once GBON compliance is achieved, supported countries will share roughly 20 times more data than before. Donor pledges to SOFF’s UN Fund stand at about USD 107 million, with a target of EUR 200 million for the first implementation period.
