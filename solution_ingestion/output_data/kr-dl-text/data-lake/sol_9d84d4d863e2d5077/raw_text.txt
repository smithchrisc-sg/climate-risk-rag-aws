Introduction

This solution, Coastal and Flood Protection Fund (CFPF) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Singapore between PUB, Singapore’s National Water Agency, Ministry of Finance, Surbana Jurong, and Jacobs. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Coastal and Flood Protection Fund (CFPF) is a dedicated financing mechanism established by Singapore in 2020 to address the challenges posed by sea-level rise and compound coastal-pluvial flood risks. The fund, managed by PUB, Singapore’s National Water Agency, supports capital and operational expenditures for coastal protection and drainage upgrades. In 2025, the fund received an additional S$5 billion, bringing its total to S$10 billion, to accelerate adaptation efforts. The fund is part of a broader government initiative to enhance coastal resilience, supported by long-term borrowing and the use of past reserves for land reclamation.

Key Highlights

The CFPF is a ring-fenced fund that ensures sustainable financing for multi-decade investments in coastal protection. It supports site-specific studies and planning for large-scale infrastructure projects, such as Long Island on the east coast and barriers to protect critical industrial and port assets. The fund's design allows for phased implementation, prioritizing vulnerable and economically critical areas. Early disbursements have already financed drainage projects and detailed studies across eight coastline segments.

Results

Early disbursements of about S$200 million from FY2021 to FY2023 have financed drainage projects and progressed coastal program studies. The fund's top-up in 2025 ensures continued support for these initiatives, with construction phases expected to commence in the 2030s.
