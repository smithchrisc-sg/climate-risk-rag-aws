NSW Flood Recovery and Resilience Grant Program

NSW Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (DCCEEW), 
NSW Department of Planning and Environment (DPE)

Introduction

This solution, NSW Flood Recovery and Resilience Grant Program addresses natural catastrophe in Australia between NSW Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (DCCEEW), and NSW Department of Planning and Environment (DPE). The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The NSW Flood Recovery and Resilience Grant Program is a New South Wales Government initiative aimed at reducing flood risk and enhancing community resilience in flood-prone areas. It was launched in response to the catastrophic floods of February–March 2022, with funding from the Australian Government's Emergency Response Fund. The program supports the implementation of the NSW Flood Prone Land Policy and the floodplain risk management process, providing financial assistance to local councils and government bodies within 62 disaster-declared LGAs.

Key Highlights

The program emphasizes a co-funding model with a 3:1 government-to-council cost-sharing ratio, incentivizing local co-investment. It funds a range of activities, including planning, design, property modification, flood modification, and response modification measures. The program is part of the broader NSW Floodplain Management Grants framework and is administered by the NSW Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (DCCEEW).

Results

The program has allocated $40 million to manage flood risk across 62 disaster-declared local government areas, supporting high-priority studies, designs, and implementation works recommended in council-level Floodplain Risk Management Studies and Plans.
