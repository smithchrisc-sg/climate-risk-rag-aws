Introduction

This solution, DRFA Category B TC Alfred funding for urban drainage works addresses natural catastrophe in Australia between NSW Reconstruction Authority, Transport for NSW, NSW Local Councils, Australian Government, Queensland Reconstruction Authority, and National Emergency Management Agency. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on regulation and collaboration.

Description

Following the impacts of Tropical Cyclone Alfred in March 2025, the NSW Reconstruction Authority activated Disaster Recovery Funding Arrangements (DRFA) Category B to fund urban drainage repairs and related flood-recovery works in New South Wales. This initiative includes a regulatory change allowing councils to request a special variation to reallocate funds from council waste funds to general funds, enabling co-funding of urgent urban drainage needs. Additionally, a Community Relief Program provides up to $1 million per impacted council for clean-up and make-safe funding for council-managed assets not covered by other packages.

Key Highlights

The initiative explicitly includes urban drainage works in DRFA Category B, providing regulatory flexibility to shift funds between council accounts. It establishes time-bound repair pathways, such as Emergent Works, Immediate Reconstruction Works, and Essential Public Asset Reconstruction Works, to expedite repairs. The initiative is complemented by clean-up funding through the Community Relief Program and aligns with broader mitigation planning through the Disaster Ready Fund.

Results

In Bellingen Shire, 516 Emergent Works sites and 302 damages were identified, with stormwater repairs included in the Immediate Reconstruction Works scope. Damages are estimated at $40–$60 million locally. Councils are receiving Community Relief Program funding up to $1 million.
