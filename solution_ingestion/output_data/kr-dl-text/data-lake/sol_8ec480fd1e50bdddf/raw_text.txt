Introduction

This solution, Earthquake Disaster Risk Indexing (EDRI) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in India between Government of India (GoI), Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), National Institute of Disaster Management (NIDM), Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), India Meteorological Department (IMD), United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) India, Indian Institutes of Technology (IIT Roorkee, IIT Kanpur, IIT Bombay), and RMSI. Description

The Earthquake Disaster Risk Indexing (EDRI) initiative is a comprehensive program aimed at screening and prioritizing Indian cities for focused earthquake risk reduction and preparedness actions. It involves collaboration between government bodies, international organizations, and technical agencies to institutionalize risk indexing across selected cities, providing a framework for urban earthquake vulnerability reduction.

Key Highlights

The EDRI initiative is notable for its collaborative approach, involving multiple stakeholders from government, international organizations, and technical agencies. It leverages expertise from Indian Institutes of Technology for methods guidance and training, and incorporates seismic design codes from the Bureau of Indian Standards. The initiative also utilizes seismic hazard inputs from the India Meteorological Department and allied geoscience agencies, ensuring a comprehensive and scientifically grounded approach to earthquake risk reduction.
