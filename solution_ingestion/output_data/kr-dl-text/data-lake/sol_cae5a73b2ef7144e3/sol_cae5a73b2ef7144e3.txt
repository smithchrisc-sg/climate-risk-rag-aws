Introduction

This solution, Hong Kong Special Administrative Region’s Insurance‑Linked Securities (ILS) Regulatory Regime and Pilot ILS Grant Scheme addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in China (Hong Kong Special Administrative Region) between Government of the Hong Kong SAR, Issuing institutions (ILS sponsors/insurers), and Mainland enterprises establishing captive insurers in Hong Kong. The program was implemented in Cannot be determined and focuses on regulation and collaboration.

Description

The Hong Kong Special Administrative Region’s Insurance‑Linked Securities (ILS) Regulatory Regime and Pilot ILS Grant Scheme is a government-led initiative aimed at facilitating the issuance of insurance-linked securities, such as catastrophe bonds, in Hong Kong. The initiative includes a dedicated regulatory framework and a Pilot ILS Grant Scheme that provides financial and developmental support to lower barriers for issuance and nurture talent. This initiative is part of Hong Kong's strategy to strengthen its status as a global risk management center and financial hub.

Key Highlights

The initiative features a dedicated ILS regulatory framework that reduces legal uncertainty and frictional costs, a Pilot ILS Grant Scheme that subsidizes issuance costs and supports talent development, and a focus on nurturing talent through training programs and professional engagement. It aligns with Hong Kong's position as a global risk management center and has facilitated the issuance of several catastrophe bonds.

Results

Several catastrophe bonds have been issued under Hong Kong’s regime, and the scheme is slated for a two-year extension to deepen market participation and talent development. Exact counts and volumes are not specified.
