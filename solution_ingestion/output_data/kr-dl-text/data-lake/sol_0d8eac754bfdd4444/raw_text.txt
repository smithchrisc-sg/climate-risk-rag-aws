Introduction

This solution, Second Disaster Resilience Improvement Program (Second DRIP) addresses natural catastrophe in Philippines between Government of the Philippines, Local government units (LGUs), Government-owned or -controlled corporations (GOCCs), and Asian Development Bank (ADB). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The Second Disaster Resilience Improvement Program (Second DRIP) is a policy-based loan initiative by the Asian Development Bank, providing the Philippines with contingent disaster financing. It aims to enhance disaster risk reduction and management through policy reforms and rapid financing access post-disasters. The program includes a gender equity theme and focuses on anticipatory disaster response, integrating national state-owned enterprises into disaster risk management frameworks, and rolling out a voluntary city parametric insurance scheme.

Key Highlights

Replenishable Contingent Financing: The program allows for up to two replenishments within five years, maintaining liquidity for successive shocks. City-Level Parametric Insurance: A voluntary scheme for cities to enable quick payouts based on predefined hazard triggers. Integration of State-Owned Enterprises: Involves national SOEs in disaster risk management, recognizing their role in infrastructure and service continuity. Anticipatory Response Focus: Shifts from reactive to proactive disaster management, emphasizing planning and risk reduction.
