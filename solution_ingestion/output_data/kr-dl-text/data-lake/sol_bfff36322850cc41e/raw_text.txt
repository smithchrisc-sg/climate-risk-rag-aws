Introduction

This solution, Scaling Climate and Disaster Risk Financing Framework and Parametric Insurance in Fiji, 
Fiji’s Parametric Climate and Disaster Risk Insurance Product addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Republic of Fiji between Government of Fiji, UN Capital Development Fund (UNCDF), UN Development Programme (UNDP), United Nations University – Institute for Environment and Human Security (UNU-EHS), India–UN Development Partnership Fund, UN Office for South–South Cooperation (UNOSSC), Governments of Australia, India, New Zealand, Commonwealth Secretariat, FijiCare Insurance Limited, and Sun Insurance. The program was implemented in 2021, 
2023 and focuses on collaboration, accessibility, leveraging tech, and raising awareness.

Description

Fiji's Climate and Disaster Risk Financing and parametric insurance initiative aims to provide rapid financial relief to vulnerable groups affected by cyclones and extreme rainfall. Launched in 2021, it is the first parametric insurance product in the Pacific, designed to deliver quick payouts based on objective triggers like wind speed and rainfall intensity. The initiative is part of the Pacific Insurance and Climate Adaptation Programme (PICAP) and involves collaboration between public, private, and international organizations to enhance inclusion, digital delivery, and scalability.

Key Highlights

The initiative is the first parametric microinsurance in the Pacific, offering payouts within two weeks of verified disaster events. It focuses on inclusion, targeting women and persons with disabilities, and utilizes digital disbursement methods. The project is supported by a multi-donor and multi-country framework under PICAP, with local underwriting by FijiCare and Sun Insurance.

Results

As of the report, 1,013 farmers have received payouts totaling FJD 201,000, with a settlement time of two weeks. The initiative targets 40% women and 24% persons with disabilities.
