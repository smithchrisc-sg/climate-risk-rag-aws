Introduction

This solution, United Nations Development Programme – Insurance and Risk Finance Facility, Nepal (UNDP IRFF Nepal) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Nepal between Government of Nepal, United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), Insurance Development Forum (IDF), German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ), Milliman, and IIN. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and leveraging tech.

Description

The UNDP Insurance and Risk Finance Facility (IRFF) in Nepal is a country-level initiative aimed at enhancing financial resilience against high-impact hazards such as earthquakes, floods, landslides, and droughts. It is part of UNDP's global IRFF portfolio, working with governments and the insurance industry to create inclusive insurance and disaster risk finance solutions. The initiative focuses on diagnostics, policy reforms, capacity building, and innovation to develop a robust insurance and risk-finance ecosystem in Nepal.

Key Highlights

AI-embedded livestock insurance using muzzle print ID and geo-tagging, climate-smart platform for potato farmers, and the Tripartite Agreement support. The initiative also includes actuarial capacity-building through GAIN and a national training programme with 180 participants.

Results

One insurance solution in development, three institutional/policy processes in progress, and two innovative insurance solutions selected in March 2025. The initiative has trained 180 participants through its national training programme.
