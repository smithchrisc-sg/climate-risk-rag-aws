Introduction

This solution, Sovereign Risk Transfer Scheme Development in Fiji addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Republic of Fiji between Government of Fiji, Reserve Bank of Fiji (RBF), World Bank Group, International Finance Corporation (IFC), and Willis Towers Watson (WTW). The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

The Sovereign Risk Transfer Scheme Development in Fiji is a national initiative aimed at structuring pre-arranged financial protection for climate and disaster events. Led by Willis Towers Watson, with technical partnerships from the Government of Fiji, the World Bank, and the IFC, the scheme is designed to provide rapid liquidity in the aftermath of disasters. It integrates with Fiji's layered Climate and Disaster Risk Financing strategy and leverages modernized national payment systems to ensure swift disbursement of funds.

Key Highlights

The scheme emphasizes rapid payout features and strong public-private collaboration. It aligns with the modernization of Fiji's national payment system (RTGS) and broader disaster risk financing reforms. The initiative is part of a comprehensive strategy to move Fiji from ad-hoc disaster financing to pre-arranged, rules-based instruments, improving speed, transparency, and predictability of post-disaster liquidity.

Results

Process milestones include the introduction of upgraded RTGS in 2022 and the initiation of the Global Shield process in Fiji in August 2024. Quantifiable payout or coverage metrics have not been publicly reported as of September 2025.
