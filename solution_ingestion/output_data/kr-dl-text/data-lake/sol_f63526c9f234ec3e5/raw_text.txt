The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Plan (NDRRMP) 2020–2030 is a comprehensive framework designed to reduce disaster risk and enhance resilience in the Philippines. It aligns with national laws and international frameworks to integrate disaster risk reduction (DRR) and climate change adaptation (CCA) into national development planning. The plan is structured around four thematic areas: Prevention and Mitigation, Preparedness, Response and Early Recovery, and Rehabilitation and Recovery. It emphasizes inclusivity, human rights, and evidence-based decision-making, with a focus on vulnerable communities and ecosystems.

Key Highlights

The NDRRMP is notable for its explicit integration of DRR and CCA, aligning with the National Climate Change Action Plan (NCCAP) and the National Adaptation Plan (NAP). It employs a systems-based approach to improve institutional coherence and cross-sector collaboration. The plan is supported by a Results-Based Monitoring and Evaluation System (RBMES) to track progress and ensure accountability. A Midterm Review (MTR) is underway to assess progress and propose an Accelerated Disaster Action Plan (ADRAP) to enhance implementation.

Results

Since its implementation in 2020, the NDRRMP has guided national disaster risk reduction efforts across its four thematic areas. A comprehensive Midterm Review is in progress to evaluate the plan's effectiveness and address implementation challenges.
