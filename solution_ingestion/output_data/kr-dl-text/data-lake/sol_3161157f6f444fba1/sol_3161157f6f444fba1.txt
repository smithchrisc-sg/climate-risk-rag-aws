Introduction

This solution, Flood Protection Sector Project‑III (FPSP‑III) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Pakistan between Federal Flood Commission (FFC), Provincial Irrigation Departments (PIDs), National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), Provincial/District Disaster Management Authorities, Pakistan Meteorological Department (PMD), Water and Power Development Authority (WAPDA), Pakistan Army, Pakistan Commissioner for Indus Waters (PCIW), Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), NESPAK, and Deltares. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Flood Protection Sector Project‑III (FPSP‑III) is a strategic initiative under Pakistan’s National Flood Protection Plan‑IV (NFPP‑IV), aimed at managing flood risks and enhancing river training across the country. It involves both structural and non-structural measures to mitigate flood impacts, including embankment strengthening, river training works, and upgrades to early warning systems. The project is coordinated by the Federal Flood Commission and implemented by Provincial Irrigation Departments, with support from various national and international partners.

Key Highlights

FPSP‑III enforces national standards while allowing local adaptation, integrating both structural and non-structural measures to address flood risks comprehensively. It benefits from international cooperation, notably JICA's advisory services, which enhance project preparation and institutional capacity. The initiative also features a clear institutional framework for crisis coordination, ensuring effective implementation and response.
