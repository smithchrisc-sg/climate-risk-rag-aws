Lao People's Democratic Republic between Ministry of Water Resources and Meteorology (MOWRAM), National Committee for Disaster Management (NCDM), Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment (MONRE), Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare (MLSW), World Meteorological Organization (WMO), United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction (UNDRR), and World Bank Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR). The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The CREWS Cambodia and Lao PDR project is a four-year initiative aimed at enhancing multi-hazard early warning systems with a focus on hydrometeorological risks such as droughts, floods, and severe weather. It operates through national and international partnerships to strengthen the capacities of meteorological and hydrological services, improve risk assessments, and enhance communication and response capabilities. The project is part of the global CREWS Initiative, supporting least developed countries and small island developing states in establishing risk-informed early warning services.

Key Highlights

The project features an end-to-end, inclusive early warning system design with a binational scope, aligning with national Early Warnings for All (EW4All) roadmaps. It emphasizes strengthening the four pillars of early warning systems: risk knowledge, monitoring and warning services, dissemination and communication, and response capability. The project is regionally networked via the WMO Regional Forecast Support Centre in Ha Noi and is set to continue into a second phase (CREWS 2.0) after its initial completion in 2025.

Results

The project has been documented as successful by the WMO, leading to the authorization of a second phase. However, specific quantified outputs are not publicly detailed as of September 2025.
