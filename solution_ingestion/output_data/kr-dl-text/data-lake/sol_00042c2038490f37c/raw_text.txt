Description

The initiative involves the procurement of servers for the National Multi-Hazard Early Warning Centre (N-EWC) in Papua New Guinea, under the Building Safety and Resilience in the Pacific Phase II (BSRP II) program led by the Pacific Community's Geoscience, Energy and Maritime (GEM) Division. It aims to establish and operationalize a national multi-hazard early warning capability through the provisioning of core ICT infrastructure and staffing, including the appointment of a National Multi-Hazard Early Warning Centre Coordinator.

Key Highlights

The initiative features a competitive and transparent procurement process with documented deadline extensions to improve bid quality and competition. It emphasizes localization by awarding the coordinator role to a PNG-based contractor and aligns with the regional BSRP II framework to ensure coherence. The phased approach begins with securing national coordination and institutional presence, followed by provisioning technical infrastructure to enable multi-hazard early warning operations.

Results

The N-EWC Coordinator contract was awarded for EUR 30,396 in 2024, and the server procurement process is ongoing with an extended deadline to enhance competition. These steps mark progress towards establishing operational capability for multi-hazard early warning in PNG.
