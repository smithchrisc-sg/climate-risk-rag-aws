Introduction

This solution, Project LAWA at BINHI (Local Adaptation to Water Access and Breaking Insufficiency through Nutritious Harvest for the Impoverished) addresses natural catastrophe in Philippines between Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), Department of Agriculture (DA), Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), Municipal Social Welfare and Development Offices (MSWDOs), Municipal Agriculture Offices (MAGROs), Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Offices (MDRRMOs), and Municipal Information Offices (MIOs). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

Project LAWA at BINHI is a flagship initiative under the DSWD's Risk Resiliency Program for Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation (RRP‑CCAM) in the Philippines. It aims to improve resilience against climate- and disaster-related risks such as water scarcity, food insecurity, and environmental degradation. The project focuses on local adaptation strategies and sustainable farming to address water access and nutritious food production in impoverished areas.

Key Highlights

The project employs a convergent social protection approach through Cash-for-Training (CFT) and Cash-for-Work (CFW) programs. CFT provides training on drought-resistant farming and disaster risk reduction, while CFW involves constructing small-farm reservoirs and planting climate-resilient crops. Participatory planning and site validation ensure interventions are data-informed and tailored to local conditions. Governance steps, such as the formal turnover of completed sites, anchor asset stewardship and sustainability.

Results

In May 2025, 250 beneficiaries were trained in Davao City. Six Project LAWA at BINHI sites were turned over in Monkayo, Davao de Oro, with association officers sworn in for long-term operation and maintenance.
