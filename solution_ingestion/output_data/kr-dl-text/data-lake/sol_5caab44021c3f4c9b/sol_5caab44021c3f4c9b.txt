Before the Deluge: Building Flood Resilience in Aotearoa

Greater Wellington Regional Council, 
Environment Canterbury, 
Horizons Regional Council

Introduction

This solution, Before the Deluge: Building Flood Resilience in Aotearoa addresses natural catastrophe in Aotearoa New Zealand between Greater Wellington Regional Council, Environment Canterbury, and Horizons Regional Council. The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

Before the Deluge is a cross-regional policy and investment proposal aimed at establishing a formal central-local co-investment model for river management and flood protection infrastructure across Aotearoa New Zealand. Initiated by the Greater Wellington Regional Council, the proposal seeks to leverage existing regional schemes and targeted rates to accelerate upgrades and resilience measures for river systems, stopbanks, floodgates, and nature-based solutions. The initiative underscores the urgency of addressing flood risks exacerbated by climate change, as highlighted by the impacts of Cyclones Hale and Gabrielle.

Key Highlights

Cross-regional coalitioning: The initiative represents a coordinated effort across multiple regional councils, advocating for a unified approach to flood resilience., 
Post-event recalibration: The 2.0 update responds to recent cyclones, demonstrating an agile and evidence-responsive approach., 
Integration of braided-river stewardship: Emphasizes nature-based management in dynamic river systems., 
Use of established Scheme frameworks: Leverages mature regional schemes with targeted rates for co-funded works., 
Public transparency: Proposal documents are publicly accessible, supporting scrutiny and cross-jurisdictional learning.

Results

The proposal has established a coherent, cross-regional case for co-investment, but specific central funding commitments directly traceable to the initiative are not documented.
