Introduction

This solution, Tripartite Agreement (UNDP–IDF–BMZ) – Bangladesh Programme (TPA–Bangladesh) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Bangladesh between Germany’s Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ), United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), and Insurance Development Forum (IDF). The program was implemented in 2019 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Tripartite Agreement (TPA) in Bangladesh is a public-private partnership initiative aimed at developing a parametric cyclone insurance solution to address climate and disaster risks. It is part of a global effort under the InsuResilience Vision 2025 to expand risk finance and insurance for climate-vulnerable countries. The program focuses on capacity-building for inclusive insurance and risk finance across sectors like agriculture and public financial management, with a flagship product targeting cyclone risk.

Key Highlights

The TPA is pioneering the first parametric cyclone insurance solution in Bangladesh, aligning with the InsuResilience Vision 2025. It features a robust financing commitment from BMZ and risk capacity from IDF, an open modelling ecosystem for risk analytics, and synergy with UNDP’s Financial Resilience in Agriculture initiative. The program aims to close the protection gap in Bangladesh, where insurance penetration is currently low.

Results

The program has mobilized over 40 private-sector members and secured EUR 30 million in financing from BMZ, with up to USD 5 billion in risk capacity offered by IDF. A tailored parametric cyclone solution is under development, and a 2024 diagnostic has been published. Current insurance penetration in Bangladesh is 0.15%, with inclusive insurance coverage at 10%, indicating a significant protection gap.
