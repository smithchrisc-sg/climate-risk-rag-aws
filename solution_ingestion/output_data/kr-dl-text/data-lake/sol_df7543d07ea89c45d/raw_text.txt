Introduction

This solution, Design.Resilience.NZ addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in New Zealand between Natural Hazards Commission Toka Tū Ake (NHC), Ministry of Business, Innovation & Employment (MBIE), BRANZ, New Zealand Society for Earthquake Engineering (NZSEE), Structural Engineering Society New Zealand (SESOC), and New Zealand Geotechnical Society (NZGS). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

Design.Resilience.NZ is a national online hub in New Zealand aimed at facilitating the design and upgrade of buildings to exceed minimum building code requirements, focusing on functional performance during and after natural hazard events. It consolidates technically reviewed, above-code guidance from multiple agencies into a single, trusted platform, addressing the need for practical, resilient design resources.

Key Highlights

The hub offers a centralized, curated repository of guidance documents, reviewed by an expert steering panel to ensure technical soundness. It features a commissioning pathway for new guidance topics and emphasizes above-code, performance-focused design for rapid re-occupancy and continuity of services post-event. The hub aggregates resources from various public and private entities, overcoming previous fragmentation in the field.

Results

Quantifiable outcomes are not yet reported as the hub is in its early stages. However, it shows strong potential impact by aligning with public expectations for buildings to enable shelter-in-place and service continuity post-earthquake, and by demonstrating cost-effectiveness in achieving above-code performance.
