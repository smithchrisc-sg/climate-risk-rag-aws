Introduction

This solution, Climate and Disaster Resilient Small-Scale Water Resources Management Project (CDRSSWRMP/SSWRM) addresses natural catastrophe in Bangladesh between Local Government Engineering Department (LGED), Local Government Division, Government of Bangladesh, Asian Development Bank (ADB), International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD), and Government of the Netherlands. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The Climate and Disaster Resilient Small-Scale Water Resources Management Project (CDRSSWRMP) in Bangladesh aims to modernize small-scale water resources to enhance climate and disaster resilience in rural agriculture and fisheries. The project, led by the Local Government Engineering Department (LGED), is co-financed by the Asian Development Bank (ADB), the International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD), and the Government of the Netherlands. It focuses on infrastructure rehabilitation, community-based water governance, and agribusiness interventions to improve productivity and incomes in climate-vulnerable areas.

Key Highlights

The project integrates participatory governance with infrastructure and market systems support, establishing 150 new Water Management Cooperative Associations (WMCAs) and strengthening 230 existing ones. It includes climate- and disaster-resilient infrastructure investments, such as the modernization of the Bakkhali Rubber Dam, and agribusiness development to enhance market access and income stability. The project covers 42 districts, targeting over 220,000 hectares and benefiting approximately 380,000 households.

Results

The project targets more than 220,000 hectares of improved water resources management and aims to support the livelihoods of roughly 380,000 rural households. It plans to establish 150 new WMCAs and enhance the performance of 230 existing ones. The project also includes the emergency reconstruction of the Bakkhali Rubber Dam and the construction of 110 km of climate-resilient farm access roads.
