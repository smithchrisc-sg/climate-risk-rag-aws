Introduction

This solution, PNG Disaster Management Reference Handbook (August 2025) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Papua New Guinea between National Disaster Centre (NDC), Government of Papua New Guinea, Papua New Guinea Defence Force, Royal Papua New Guinea Constabulary, Center for Excellence in Disaster Management and Humanitarian Assistance (CFE-DMHA), United Nations, Australia, Japan, New Zealand, and United States. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The PNG Disaster Management Reference Handbook, published by the Center for Excellence in Disaster Management and Humanitarian Assistance in August 2025, serves as a comprehensive guide for disaster management in Papua New Guinea. It addresses the country's vulnerability to a range of natural and human-caused hazards, providing a strategic framework for coordination among national and international actors. The handbook emphasizes the importance of pre-disaster preparedness and outlines operational realities and coordination mechanisms to enhance disaster response effectiveness.

Key Highlights

The handbook highlights PNG's high frequency of disasters, with over 60 major events in 25 years, making it the most at-risk Pacific Island nation., 
It emphasizes operational realism by focusing on PNG's geographical challenges, such as mountainous terrain and remote communities., 
Joint leadership for international aid is established through coordination between the NDC Director and the UN's in-country lead., 
The handbook recognizes the critical role of churches and faith-based organizations in reaching remote areas., 
A strong emphasis is placed on preparedness over post-disaster cleanup, advocating for anticipatory action and resilience-building.

Results

The handbook aims to facilitate faster international mobilization, improve alignment and clarity for aid workers, and reinforce a preparedness agenda. However, specific quantitative performance metrics are not provided.
