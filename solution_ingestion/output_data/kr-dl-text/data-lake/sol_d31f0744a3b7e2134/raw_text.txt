Introduction

This solution, PNG DataCo Parametric Earthquake Insurance for Submarine Fibre-Optic Cable Network addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Papua New Guinea between PNG DataCo Limited, Pacific Catastrophe Risk Insurance Company (PCRIC), Global Earthquake Model (GEM), United States Geological Survey (USGS), Insurance Development Forum (IDF), and WTW. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

The PNG DataCo Parametric Earthquake Insurance is a bespoke parametric insurance policy designed to provide rapid post-earthquake financing for repairs to Papua New Guinea’s submarine fibre-optic cable network. Developed by the Pacific Catastrophe Risk Insurance Company (PCRIC) in collaboration with WTW, the solution triggers on measured ground shaking reported by authoritative seismic agencies to release funds quickly after earthquakes that are likely to damage undersea telecommunications infrastructure.

Key Highlights

This initiative is the first-of-its-kind parametric earthquake cover for submarine cable systems, offering non-sovereign coverage by a regional catastrophe risk pool. It utilizes open-source seismic models from GEM and objective triggers from USGS to ensure rapid payouts. The policy is strategically aligned with PNG DataCo's role in regional connectivity, complementing large-scale investments in digital infrastructure.

Results

The policy is operational and integrated within PNG DataCo’s risk management framework. While no payouts have been triggered yet, the policy's existence closes a specific protection gap for submarine cables, demonstrating market acceptance and potential for replication in other high-seismicity regions.
