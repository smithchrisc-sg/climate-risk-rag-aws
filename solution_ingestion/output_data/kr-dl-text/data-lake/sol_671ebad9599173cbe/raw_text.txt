Introduction

This solution, 2020–2025 NZ Flood Risk Reduction and Mitigation Initiatives addresses natural catastrophe in New Zealand between Ministry for the Environment, Hawke’s Bay Regional Council, and Wairoa District Council. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The 2020–2025 NZ Flood Risk Reduction and Mitigation Initiatives encompass a suite of strategic and practical measures aimed at reducing flood risk in New Zealand. These initiatives include a national strategic framework, legislative streamlining, and local project implementation, notably the Wairoa Flood Mitigation Project. The initiatives focus on integrating national policy, legislative facilitation, and community-led local projects to address riverine flooding risks exacerbated by climate change.

Key Highlights

The initiatives are characterized by a 'policy-to-delivery' approach, aligning national guidance with local implementation. The Wairoa Flood Mitigation Project exemplifies this through community engagement, cultural impact assessments, and a streamlined legislative pathway via the Hawke’s Bay Flood Protection Works Order 2024. The project aims to protect homes, cultural sites, and infrastructure while reducing economic losses from flooding.

Results

The initiatives aim to move 627 properties from higher-risk categories to safer ones, with NZD $70 million in Crown funding allocated for the Wairoa project. The project is expected to significantly reduce flood extents and depths, lowering exposure for households and community assets.
