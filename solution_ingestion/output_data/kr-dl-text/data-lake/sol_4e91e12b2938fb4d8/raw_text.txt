Roadmap to 30x30 addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between United Nations, Australian Government, UNESCO, Fondation Prince Albert II de Monaco, World Food Programme (WFP), Monaco Asset Management, Citizens of the Sea, and The Lancet. The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration, leveraging tech, and raising awareness.

Description

Minderoo Foundation is a philanthropic organization based in Australia that employs an integrated approach combining impact investment, science, and policy advocacy to address global challenges related to oceans, climate, health, and humanitarian risks. It operates through various initiatives such as the Strategic Impact Fund, ReOcean Fund, OceanOmics, and the Roadmap to 30x30 financing commitment, focusing on scalable solutions and leveraging catalytic capital to mobilize larger financial resources.

Key Highlights

Minderoo Foundation's unique integrated impact model includes a A$250 million Strategic Impact Fund with over A$125 million committed to date, and A$25 million in catalytic capital for blended finance solutions. The foundation's initiatives include the ReOcean Fund for ocean innovation, OceanOmics for advanced marine monitoring using AI and eDNA, and a US$10 million commitment to Marine Protected Areas. Minderoo also supports UN Charter reform and humanitarian efforts in conflict zones.

Results

Minderoo Foundation has committed over A$125 million through its Strategic Impact Fund, invested €5 million in the ReOcean Fund, and contributed more than US$13 million to the World Food Programme since 2022. Its humanitarian support in Gaza has reached approximately US$28 million by October 2024.
