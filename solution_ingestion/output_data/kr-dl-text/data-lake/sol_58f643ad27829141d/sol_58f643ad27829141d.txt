Introduction

This solution, Building Safety and Resilience in the Pacific Phase II (BSRP II) addresses natural catastrophe in Papua New Guinea between Papua New Guinea National Disaster Management Office (NDMO), The Pacific Community (SPC), and European Union (EU). The program was implemented in 2022 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and accessibility.

Description

Building Safety and Resilience in the Pacific Phase II (BSRP II) is a regional program funded by the European Union and implemented by the Pacific Community (SPC) to reduce disaster and climate risk impacts across 15 Pacific Island Countries, including Papua New Guinea. The initiative focuses on strengthening multi-hazard early warning systems and operational coordination, with a country-led approach anchored in the National Disaster Management Office (NDMO).

Key Highlights

BSRP II is a €14 million initiative with continuity from Phase I, emphasizing country-owned implementation through dedicated Country Implementation Plans and national coordinators. It integrates multi-sector inclusion, focusing on finance, government, education, civil society, and vulnerable groups. The program establishes a National Multi-Hazard Early Warning Centre Coordinator in PNG and invests in digital tools training for disaster risk management.

Results

Institutional deployment of the MHEWC Coordinator role in PNG and completion of digital tools training for PNG officials. Quantifiable outcome-level metrics are expected to emerge as activities mature through 2025–2027.
