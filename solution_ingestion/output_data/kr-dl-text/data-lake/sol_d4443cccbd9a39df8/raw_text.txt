Introduction

This solution, State Disaster Mitigation Plan (SDMP), NSW Reconstruction Authority (NSW RA) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia (State of New South Wales) between NSW Reconstruction Authority, NSW Treasury, NSW Government agencies, Local councils, Industry (building code bodies), and Insurance sector. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The State Disaster Mitigation Plan (SDMP) is New South Wales' comprehensive strategy to proactively reduce disaster risks before they occur. Released in February 2024 by the NSW Reconstruction Authority, it is Australia's first state-level multi-hazard mitigation plan. The SDMP aims to shift disaster spending from response and recovery to risk reduction, addressing risks such as floods, storms, coastal erosion, bushfires, and heatwaves. It integrates mitigation into investment decisions, develops local Disaster Adaptation Plans (DAPs), and collaborates with industry and insurers to enhance resilience and affordability.

Key Highlights

The SDMP is the first state-wide, multi-hazard mitigation plan in Australia, integrating prevention and preparedness into government investment. It features place-based Disaster Adaptation Plans (DAPs), whole-of-government coordination, and explicit inclusion of Aboriginal advisory structures. The plan embeds natural hazard risk into NSW Treasury Business Case Guidelines and emphasizes evidence-based risk identification, funding alignment, and culturally informed governance.

Results

As of May 2025, 2 actions have been delivered and 26 are underway. Delivered actions include updated NSW Treasury Business Case Guidelines and the establishment of a Strategic Aboriginal Advisory Group. A subsequent update reports 7 actions delivered and 22 underway, with DAP guidelines released and DAPs initiated in high-risk regions.
