Introduction

This solution, Disaster Ready Fund – Round 3 (DRF Round 3) addresses natural catastrophe in Australia between Australian Government (National Emergency Management Agency, NEMA), NSW Reconstruction Authority (RA), Queensland Reconstruction Authority (QRA), and Tasmanian Department of Premier and Cabinet (DPAC). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Disaster Ready Fund – Round 3 is a national competitive grant program in Australia aimed at funding infrastructure and systemic disaster risk reduction projects. It is part of the broader Disaster Ready Fund initiative, which commits $1 billion over five years to reduce disaster risk through state and territory-led applications. The program focuses on multi-hazard risks, including extreme weather and geological hazards, and emphasizes alignment with existing disaster risk reduction plans.

Key Highlights

The DRF Round 3 offers up to $200 million annually, with a total of $1 billion over five years. It supports both construction and preparatory activities, such as business cases and feasibility studies. The program requires strong project logic, SMART milestones, and evidence of delivery capability within a three-year window. It also provides structured webinars and information packs to support applicants.

Results

Round 3 outcomes are pending announcement in the second half of 2025. Previous rounds have shown significant impact, with Queensland receiving over $84 million across 50 projects in Round 1 and over $29.3 million across 28 projects in Round 2.
