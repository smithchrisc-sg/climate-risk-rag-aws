Introduction

This solution, Second Cambodia Growth and Resilience Development Policy Financing (DPF2) addresses natural catastrophe in Cambodia between Royal Government of Cambodia, Ministry of Economy and Finance, International Development Association (IDA), and World Bank. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Second Cambodia Growth and Resilience Development Policy Financing (DPF2) is a policy-based financial operation aimed at supporting Cambodia's economic growth and resilience. With a total financing of US$275 million, it focuses on enhancing private sector competitiveness, fiscal resilience, social protection, and environmental regulation. The initiative is a collaboration between the World Bank and the Royal Government of Cambodia, targeting systemic risks such as macroeconomic challenges, structural competitiveness, fiscal vulnerabilities, and disaster risks.

Key Highlights

Programmatic continuity with a previous operation in 2022, reinforcing a multi-year reform partnership., 
Dual-instrument IDA financing comprising a US$244 million Shorter Maturity Loan and an SDR 23.5 million IDA Credit., 
Strategic focus on competition and market functioning to address structural concentration and barriers to firm dynamics., 
Emphasis on fiscal risk management and domestic capital market development to reduce vulnerability to external financing conditions., 
Explicit support for disaster and climate-related resilience through improved environmental regulation and disaster risk management.

Results

Financing volume and structure: US$275 million total, comprising a US$244 million Shorter Maturity Loan and an SDR 23.5 million (US$31 million equivalent) IDA Credit., 
Series continuity with a cumulative US$549 million in policy-based financing over two operations., 
Expected outcomes include a more competitive market environment, enhanced fiscal resilience, and strengthened sovereign bond issuance capacity.
