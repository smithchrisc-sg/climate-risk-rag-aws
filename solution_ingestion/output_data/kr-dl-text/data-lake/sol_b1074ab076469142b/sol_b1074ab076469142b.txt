Introduction

This solution, 2025 Supplementary Budget – Disaster Prevention and Recovery Tranche addresses natural catastrophe in South Korea between Ministry of Economy and Finance (MOEF), Cabinet, National Assembly, and Korea Asset Management Corporation (KAMCO). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on regulation and collaboration.

Description

The 2025 Supplementary Budget for Large-Scale Disasters in South Korea is a targeted fiscal allocation within the broader supplementary budget aimed at disaster prevention and recovery, specifically addressing the aftermath of severe wildfires. This initiative is part of South Korea's established supplementary budgeting process, which allows for rapid mobilization of funds to address unforeseen shocks. The disaster tranche, amounting to approximately US$2.25 billion, is integrated into a larger macroeconomic stabilization agenda that includes household financial relief and strategic industry support.

Key Highlights

The disaster tranche is a dedicated allocation within the first supplementary budget, specifically targeting wildfire recovery and prevention., 
The supplementary budgeting process allows for rapid fiscal mobilization, expanding fiscal space significantly., 
The second supplementary budget includes universal consumption vouchers and a 'bad bank' for small-business debt restructuring, indirectly supporting disaster resilience., 
The initiative reflects fiscal transparency, with the government disclosing financing methods and potential fiscal risks.

Results

Approximately US$2.25 billion dedicated to disaster prevention and recovery., 
The second supplementary budget plan totals 30.5 trillion won, with significant fiscal implications, including a projected fiscal deficit of 4.2% of GDP., 
19.8 trillion won of supplementary spending financed via additional treasury bond issuance.
