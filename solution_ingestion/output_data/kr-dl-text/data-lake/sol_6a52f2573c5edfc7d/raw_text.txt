Introduction

This solution, National Emergency Operations Center (NEOC) addresses natural catastrophe in Republic of Maldives between National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), Maldives National Defence Force (MNDF), Maldives Police Service (MPS), Health Protection Agency (HPA), Maldives Meteorological Service (MMS), Local Government Authority, Atoll and Island Councils, Sectoral ministries, United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), World Health Organization (WHO), United Nations Children's Fund (UNICEF), World Food Programme (WFP), World Bank, IOC-UNESCO, International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC), and International Committee of the Red Cross (ICRC). The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The National Emergency Operations Center (NEOC) in the Maldives is a central hub for coordinating multi-agency responses to national emergencies. Operated by the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), it is part of the country's disaster risk management system, aligned with the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction. The NEOC addresses a wide range of hazards, including natural, biological, and technological risks, and is designed to mitigate challenges posed by the Maldives' geographical vulnerabilities.

Key Highlights

The NEOC serves as the operational nerve center for the Maldives' disaster risk management, converting policy into coordinated incident management. It operates under the NDMA's mandate and is activated in graded modes to address various hazards. The NEOC's multi-agency approach includes partnerships with national defense, police, health, meteorological services, and local councils, as well as international organizations for technical assistance and support.

Results

The NEOC was prominently activated for the COVID-19 pandemic in March 2020, demonstrating its capability to coordinate a national response to health emergencies. It continues to function as the all-hazard operational mechanism for non-health and multi-sectoral crises.
