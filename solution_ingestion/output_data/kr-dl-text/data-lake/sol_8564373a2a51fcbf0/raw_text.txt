Introduction

This solution, Cyclone Reinsurance Pool Strata Mitigation Discounts (CRP Strata Mitigation Discounts) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Australia between Australian Reinsurance Pool Corporation (ARPC) and James Cook University (JCU). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Cyclone Reinsurance Pool Strata Mitigation Discounts initiative is a strategic enhancement to Australia's cyclone risk management framework. Administered by the Australian Reinsurance Pool Corporation, it introduces discounts within the premium pricing model for strata properties that implement specific cyclone risk reduction measures. This initiative aims to financially incentivize resilience investments by aligning insurance pricing with actual reduced risk, thereby encouraging strata schemes to undertake practical risk mitigation activities.

Key Highlights

The initiative is characterized by its strata-specific focus, targeting multi-dwelling buildings where risk reduction requires coordinated decisions. It features algorithm-based incentives, developed in collaboration with James Cook University, ensuring scientific credibility and alignment with government mitigation programs. The initiative also emphasizes targeted algorithmic integration, refining the pricing model to recognize verified mitigation efforts without compromising actuarial consistency.

Results

Quantifiable outcomes are pending post-implementation monitoring, expected to commence after the effective date of 1 April 2025. Anticipated results include increased uptake of resilience measures, more granular risk-differentiated pricing, and potential reductions in cyclone-related claims severity and frequency for participating strata portfolios.
