Introduction

This solution, Pacific Catastrophe Risk Assessment and Financing Initiative (PCRAFI) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Papua New Guinea between World Bank, Secretariat of the Pacific Community (SPC) through SOPAC, Asian Development Bank (ADB), Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR), European Union (ACP-EU), GNS Science, Geoscience Australia, and AIR Worldwide. The program was implemented in 2006 and focuses on collaboration, leveraging tech, and raising awareness.

Description

The Pacific Catastrophe Risk Assessment and Financing Initiative (PCRAFI) is a multi-partner program aimed at enhancing the financial resilience of Pacific Island Countries, including Papua New Guinea, against natural disasters. It provides a comprehensive risk information-to-action pipeline, integrating hazard data, exposure databases, and probabilistic risk modeling to inform sovereign and sectoral decision-making. The initiative supports the development of financial tools such as catastrophe insurance and budget reserves to mitigate fiscal impacts of disasters.

Key Highlights

PCRAFI is recognized for its extensive geospatial risk collection, PACRIS, which is one of the largest standardized repositories of hazard, exposure, and risk data for Pacific Island Countries. It has digitized over 500,000 buildings using high-resolution imagery, providing a robust foundation for risk modeling. The initiative also established a regional data-sharing platform, balancing regional efficiency with national accessibility, crucial for countries like Papua New Guinea with diverse capacities.
