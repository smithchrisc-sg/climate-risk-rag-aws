Introduction

This solution, Disaster Ready Fund (DRF) addresses natural catastrophe in Australia between National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA), Minister for Emergency Management, NSW Reconstruction Authority, and Queensland Reconstruction Authority. The program was implemented in 2023 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Disaster Ready Fund (DRF) is the Australian Government's primary program for disaster risk reduction, resilience, and preparedness. It allocates up to $1 billion over five years to state and territory governments for infrastructure mitigation and systemic risk reduction projects addressing multiple natural hazards, including extreme weather and geological events. The program aims to increase understanding of natural hazard impacts, build resilience and preparedness, and reduce exposure and harm to lower recovery burdens, particularly for vulnerable communities.

Key Highlights

The DRF provides up to $200 million annually, replacing the Emergency Response Fund (ERF) and shifting focus from recovery to prevention. It emphasizes strong co-funding and implementation discipline, aligning with national, state, and local strategies. The program funds both mitigation infrastructure and systemic risk reduction projects, with a competitive, merit-based selection process.

Results

In Round Two (2024–25), 165 projects nationwide shared $200 million, with over 500 proposals received. In Queensland, Round Two funded 29 projects with over $30.8 million, while Round One funded 50 projects with over $84 million, indicating strong demand and substantial uptake.
