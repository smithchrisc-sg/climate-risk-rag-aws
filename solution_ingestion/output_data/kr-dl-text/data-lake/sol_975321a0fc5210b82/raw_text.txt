Introduction

This solution, Government Risk Financing Initiatives for Droughts in Myanmar, 2020–2025 addresses natural catastrophe in Myanmar between Disaster Management Fund, ASEAN DRFI/SEADRIF, World Bank, and Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR). The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The Government Risk Financing Initiatives for Droughts in Myanmar, 2020–2025, is a set of sovereign and public-finance measures developed to manage drought risk within Myanmar's national disaster risk management framework. These initiatives are part of regional ASEAN financial resilience platforms and are supported by international organizations like the World Bank and GFDRR. The measures aim to enhance Myanmar's capacity to handle drought-related challenges through financial strategies and risk management.

Key Highlights

The initiatives are integrated into Myanmar's national disaster risk management framework and regional ASEAN financial resilience platforms. They are supported by international organizations such as the World Bank and GFDRR, focusing on financial strategies to manage drought risk effectively.
