Introduction

This solution, EQC Operational Policy Review (OPR) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in New Zealand between Toka Tū Ake EQC (Earthquake Commission), The Treasury (New Zealand), Private insurers operating in New Zealand, and KPMG. The program was implemented in 2020 and focuses on collaboration and regulation.

Description

The EQC Operational Policy Review (OPR) is an initiative by Toka Tū Ake EQC to overhaul and codify its operational policies following the Public Inquiry into EQC. The review focuses on strengthening EQC’s operational policies and procedures across claims handling, customer conduct, insurer interface, and land/building assessment. It aligns with the recommendations of the Public Inquiry into EQC and the government's modernization of the EQC Act, with independent assurance provided by KPMG.

Key Highlights

The OPR is directly aligned with Public Inquiry recommendations and integrates with legislative reforms led by The Treasury. It features insurer-led operating model refinements, explicit conduct requirements, and independent assurance by KPMG. The initiative also includes a structured focus on clarifying roles between EQC and private insurers, and embedding conduct expectations into policy settings.

Results

Independent assurance by KPMG confirmed progress in EQC's response program as of November 2021. The initiative has improved clarity in insurer-EQC roles and incorporated conduct requirements into policy settings. It continues to develop risk tolerance guidance to inform operational decision-making.
