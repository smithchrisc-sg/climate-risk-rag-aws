Introduction

This solution, Adaptation of Small-Scale Agriculture for improved food security of resilient communities in Papua New Guinea addresses natural catastrophe in Papua New Guinea between Government of Papua New Guinea, Adaptation Fund Board (AFB), and The Pacific Community (SPC). The program was implemented in 2025 and focuses on collaboration and raising awareness.

Description

The initiative aims to enhance food security and resilience in Papua New Guinea by adapting small-scale agriculture to withstand climate risks. It focuses on climate-proofing production systems, reducing post-harvest losses, and building capacity among communities and institutions. The project is aligned with national strategies and the Adaptation Fund's environmental and social safeguards, with technical support from the Pacific Community.

Key Highlights

The proposal is designed to address climate risks through three interlinked components: climate-proofed smallholder production, post-harvest loss reduction, and capacity building. It is community-centered and focuses on climate-smart agriculture, with a near-$10M funding scale. The proposal has been iteratively refined through technical reviews and aligns with national strategies and Adaptation Fund safeguards.

Results

At the proposal stage in 2025, with requested funding of USD 9,998,879. Quantifiable implementation results are not yet available.
