Introduction

This solution, Building Safety and Resilience in the Pacific Phase II – Papua New Guinea (BSRP II – PNG) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Papua New Guinea between National Disaster Centre, National Weather Service, Port Moresby Geophysical Observatory, Department of Community Development and Religion, Department of Education, Department of National Planning and Monitoring, The Pacific Community (SPC), and European Union. The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration, raising awareness, and leveraging tech.

Description

Building Safety and Resilience in the Pacific Phase II – Papua New Guinea (BSRP II – PNG) is a national initiative under a regional program led by the Pacific Community (SPC) aimed at addressing multi-hazard disaster and climate risks in Papua New Guinea. The initiative focuses on enhancing disaster risk management through technology upgrades, institutional strengthening, inclusive data systems, and risk-informed education policy. It involves multi-sector participation to ensure sustainability and operational uptake, aligning with global commitments like the Sendai Framework.

Key Highlights

Multi-hazard, multi-sector design integrating finance, disaster agencies, education, and civil society., 
Establishment of PNG’s first integrated database for tracking vulnerable populations., 
Modernization of seismic network with upgrades to five seismic stations., 
Development of risk-informed education policies and fire safety curriculum., 
Emphasis on local leadership and capacity building with a focus on Pacific Islander practitioners.

Results

Regional program envelope: EUR 14 million., 
PNG national allocation: EUR 770,000; additional PNG-related regional activities: EUR 93,242., 
Concrete progress in governance, staffing, planning, and early technical actions as of September 2025.
