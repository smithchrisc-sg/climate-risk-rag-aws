Introduction

This solution, 2024 Taiwan–Philippines Landslide and Debris Flow Disaster Research Cooperation MOU addresses natural catastrophe in Taiwan, 
Philippines between Agency of Rural Development and Soil and Water Conservation (ARDSWC), Manila Economic and Cultural Office (MECO), and Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (PHIVOLCS). The program was implemented in 2024 and focuses on collaboration and leveraging tech.

Description

The Taiwan–Philippines Landslide and Debris Flow Disaster Research Cooperation MOU is a bilateral agreement aimed at enhancing scientific collaboration, capacity building, and technology application for landslide and debris flow risk reduction. It involves Taiwan's ARDSWC and the Philippines' MECO and PHIVOLCS, focusing on soil and water conservation, geological disaster research, climate change impact assessment, and the use of big data and AI in disaster management.

Key Highlights

The MOU includes immediate operationalization with a technical workshop and on-site visit, a focus on deep-seated slope failures, and the application of big data and AI for early warning systems. It emphasizes cross-agency and cross-country knowledge transfer and aims to modernize risk analytics.

Results

The MOU was signed on May 6, 2024, with over 70 attendees at the kickoff workshop. Four technical talks were delivered, and ongoing joint discussions, workshops, and training are planned. Quantifiable outcomes such as pilot systems or reductions in false alarm rates are yet to be reported.
