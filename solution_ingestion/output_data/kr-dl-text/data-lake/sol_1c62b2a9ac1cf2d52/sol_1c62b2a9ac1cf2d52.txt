Introduction

This solution, National Plan for Disaster Management (2021–2025) addresses natural catastrophe through public private partnerships in Bangladesh between Ministry of Disaster Management and Relief (MoDMR), Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA), and United Nations Development Programme (UNDP). The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration, regulation, and raising awareness.

Description

The National Plan for Disaster Management (2021–2025) is a strategic framework developed by the Government of Bangladesh to address multi-hazard disaster risks in the context of rapid urbanization, industrialization, and climate change. It aligns with the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction and integrates with national development strategies such as Vision 2041, the 8th Five Year Plan, and the Delta Plan 2100. The plan emphasizes preparedness, early warning, emergency response, and rehabilitation/reconstruction/recovery, with a focus on urban risk, disability inclusion, and private-sector engagement.

Key Highlights

The NPDM is characterized by its strong alignment with international and national frameworks, ensuring coherence across government planning. It is designed to be flexible and adaptive to evolving risks, particularly in urban areas. The plan's four-pillar model—preparedness, early warning, emergency response, and recovery—aligns with Sendai priorities. It emphasizes governance, compliance, and monitoring, and incorporates disability-inclusive DRM and private-sector resilience perspectives.
