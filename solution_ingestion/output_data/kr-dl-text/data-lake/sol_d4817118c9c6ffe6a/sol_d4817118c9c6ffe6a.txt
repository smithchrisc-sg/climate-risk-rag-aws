Introduction

This solution, Modernization and Densification of National Seismic Monitoring Infrastructure addresses natural catastrophe in People’s Republic of China between China Earthquake Administration (CEA), Ministry of Emergency Management, China Earthquake Network Center, Provincial earthquake bureaus, and National Earthquake Science Data Center. The program was implemented in 2021 and focuses on collaboration, leveraging tech, and regulation.

Description

The 'Modernization and Densification of National Seismic Monitoring Infrastructure' is a comprehensive initiative under China's 14th Five-Year Plan aimed at enhancing earthquake preparedness and disaster reduction. Led by the China Earthquake Administration, the initiative focuses on constructing and digitalizing the China Earthquake Early Warning Network, expanding fault investigations, conducting nationwide risk surveys, and implementing a three-year 'earthquake informationization' action plan to improve data integration and service delivery.

Key Highlights

The initiative integrates the China Earthquake Early Warning Network with high-speed rail systems, conducts nationwide remote sensing evaluations, and emphasizes digitalization and procurement standards. It also involves international cooperation through Belt and Road EEW/tsunami systems and technical training, alongside the development of standardized public service 'white papers'.

Results

As of now, 86 pilot counties/cities have completed earthquake disaster risk investigations, a nationwide risk census has been initiated, and advanced data pipelines have been activated. National expert forums have been convened to address bottlenecks in short-term prediction and data methods.
